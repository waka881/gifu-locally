Shirakawa-go is a small village, so most visitors sleep in Takayama or Kanazawa and come in by bus. This guide compares the three choices using the Nohi Bus timetable and the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association.

## Why is Takayama the easiest base?

It is the closest town with plenty of hotels. Buses from the Takayama Bus Terminal, next to JR Takayama Station, reach Shirakawa-go in about 50 minutes, starting at 7:20 and arriving at 8:10, and the last bus back from Shirakawa-go leaves at 17:30 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026). Most departures need a reserved seat. Staying in Takayama also lets you see its morning markets, which close at noon. See [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) for every bus and [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html) for areas.

## When is Kanazawa the better base?

When you arrive from Tokyo on the Hokuriku Shinkansen or want a bigger city. The first bus from Kanazawa Station leaves at 8:10 and reaches Shirakawa-go at 9:25, and the last bus back leaves the village at 17:30, arriving in Kanazawa at 18:45. Nohi Bus lists nine buses a day from Kanazawa Station, five of which end at Shirakawa-go ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026). See [Shirakawa-go from Kanazawa](shirakawa-go-from-kanazawa.html) and [Takayama or Kanazawa](takayama-or-kanazawa.html).

## Should you stay in Shirakawa-go itself?

If you want the village without the day-trip crowds, yes. The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association lists 41 places to stay, including gassho farmhouse inns, ryokan, minshuku and guest houses, and takes bookings through its "Book a room in Shirakawa" search or by phone on +81-5769-6-1013 (9:00–17:00) ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/stay/), as of November 2026). Rooms are limited, so book early. Our [Shirakawa-go overnight stay guide](shirakawa-go-overnight-stay.html) explains the options, and the [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html) covers the January evenings.

## Can you stay in one town and end in the other?

Yes, and many people do. The same bus line runs Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa, so you can sleep in Takayama, stop in Shirakawa-go and continue to Kanazawa the same day with two tickets. See [Takayama to Kanazawa by bus](takayama-to-kanazawa-bus.html) and the [Japan Alps route](nagoya-takayama-shirakawa-go-kanazawa.html).

## FAQ

### Is it better to stay in Takayama or Kanazawa for Shirakawa-go?

Takayama is closer (about 50 minutes by bus, versus about 1 hour 15 minutes from Kanazawa) and its first bus arrives earlier, at 8:10 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026). Kanazawa suits travellers arriving by Hokuriku Shinkansen.

### Can you stay overnight in Shirakawa-go?

Yes. The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association lists 41 places to stay, including gassho farmhouse inns ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/stay/), as of November 2026).

### What time is the last bus from Shirakawa-go?

17:30 to both Takayama (arriving 18:35) and Kanazawa (arriving 18:45), on the timetable valid through November 30, 2026 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Check the new timetable from December 1.

### How much is the bus to Shirakawa-go?

¥2,800 one way from Takayama or Kanazawa, ¥2,900 from December 1, 2026 (same source).
