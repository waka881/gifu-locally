Takayama is a common next stop after Kyoto and Osaka. You can take a direct train, change at Nagoya, or ride the highway bus. This guide compares them using Hida Takayama Tourism and Nohi Bus.

## Osaka and Kyoto to Takayama at a glance

| Route | Time | Adult fare | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shinkansen Nozomi Shin-Osaka → Nagoya + Limited Express Hida | About 50 min + 140 min, plus the change | Check JR | The fastest straightforward option |
| Limited Express Hida, Osaka → Takayama direct | About 255 min | Check JR | No change of train |
| Highway bus from Osaka (Namba OCAT, Higashi-Umeda) | About 5 h 30 min from Namba | ¥7,000 | Lowest fare from Osaka |
| Highway bus from Kyoto Station (Hachijo-guchi) | About 4 h 15 min | ¥6,500 | Lowest fare from Kyoto |

Bus times are from the timetable for the 8:30 departure from Namba and the 9:50 from Kyoto.*

## How do you get from Osaka to Takayama by train?

- **Via Nagoya:** Shinkansen Nozomi from Shin-Osaka to Nagoya, about 50 minutes, then the Limited Express Hida to Takayama, about 140 minutes ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)). Our [Takayama from Nagoya guide](takayama-from-nagoya.html) covers the Hida, including a stop at Gero Onsen on the way.
- **Direct:** the Limited Express Hida also runs from Osaka to Takayama, about 255 minutes (same source). It takes longer, but you stay in one seat.

Japan Rail Pass holders can't use the Nozomi; they ride Hikari or Kodama to Nagoya, or buy the separate Nozomi ticket for pass holders ([Japan Rail Pass](https://japanrailpass.net/en/about_jrp/route/)).

- **Fares (adult, one way):** Takayama–Osaka ¥7,000; Takayama–Kyoto ¥6,500; Gujo Hachiman–Osaka ¥6,000; Gujo Hachiman–Kyoto ¥5,500 (same source).
- **Booking:** reservations open one month before, by phone or online through Japan Bus Online; buy the ticket promptly after reserving. All buses have a toilet (same source).
- **Gujo Hachiman stop:** the bus stops at Gujo Hachiman IC, so you can split the trip with a night in the old castle town; see the [Gujo Hachiman day trip guide](gujo-hachiman-day-trip.html).

## Is there a rail pass for Osaka, Kyoto and Takayama?

The Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass (¥19,800 for 5 days) covers Osaka–Kyoto–Tsuruga, the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama, the Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa bus and Nagoya–Takayama–Toyama, but not the Tokaido Shinkansen ([JR Central and JR West](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/), as of October 2026). That suits a loop such as Osaka → Kanazawa → Shirakawa-go → Takayama → Nagoya. ## Which should you choose?

- **Train via Nagoya** if you want the shortest trip or have a Japan Rail Pass.
- **Direct Hida** if you'd rather not change trains with luggage.
- **Bus** if price matters most, or you want to stop at Gujo Hachiman. From Kyoto, the bus fare is ¥6,500.
- **The pass** if you are doing a loop through Kanazawa and Shirakawa-go in 5 days.

## FAQ

### How long does it take from Osaka to Takayama?

About 50 minutes by Shinkansen to Nagoya plus about 140 minutes on the Limited Express Hida, or about 255 minutes on the direct Hida from Osaka ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)).

### How much is the bus from Kyoto to Takayama?

¥6,500 for adults one way (¥7,000 from Osaka); all seats are reserved ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/osaka_en/), as of October 2026).

### Is there a direct train from Osaka to Takayama?

Yes, the Limited Express Hida runs from Osaka to Takayama in about 255 minutes ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)).

### Can I stop at Gujo Hachiman on the way to Takayama?

Yes. The Kyoto/Osaka–Takayama highway bus stops at Gujo Hachiman IC; the adult fare from Gujo Hachiman to Takayama is not shown on the same table, so check when booking ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/osaka_en/)).
