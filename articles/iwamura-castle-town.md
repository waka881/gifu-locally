Iwamura is a small castle town in the hills of Ena, in eastern Gifu. This guide uses the Gifu Prefecture tourism site and Iwamura's own site.

| | Details |

| Castle | Iwamura Castle ruins, a mountain castle at 713 m, one of Japan's 100 Fine Castles; stone walls remain |

## What is Iwamura known for?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site describes Iwamura as a castle town that keeps the look of the Edo period: it was the political, economic and cultural centre of the Tono region, and its main street is now a national Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings, with stone paving and latticed fronts. Along the street are shops selling the local castella, gohei-mochi and kankara-mochi, sake breweries such as Iwamura Jozo, and antique and handmade-goods shops ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1345.html)). Iwamura's own site calls the castle ruins, where stone walls including the main bailey remain, one of Japan's 100 Fine Castles ([Iwamura](https://iwamura.jp/)).

## How do you get to Iwamura?

By train, change at JR Ena Station to the Akechi Railway, which runs 25.1 km from Ena to Akechi; Iwamura is about 30 minutes from Ena. By car, it is about 20 minutes from Ena IC along Route 257 ([Iwamura](https://iwamura.jp/access); [Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/model/detail_95.html), as of November 2026).

## How long does the castle walk take?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site's model route takes about 6 hours on foot, with these walking times between stops ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/model/detail_95.html)):

| From | To | On foot |
|---|---|---|
| Iwamura Station | Iwamura Art Museum (former Shibata house) | About 4 min |
| Art museum | Kano house (gunsmith) | About 7 min |
| Kano house | Katsukawa house, Tosaya, Kimura house | About 4 + 1 + 1 min |
| Kimura house | Iwamura History Museum | About 13 min |
| History museum | Iwamura Castle ruins (713 m) | About 20 min |
| Castle ruins | Iwamura Jozo sake brewery | About 29 min |
| Brewery | Iwamura Station | About 12 min |

The route has many stone steps and slopes up to the castle, so wear comfortable shoes and clothes. Opening days vary by house: the prefecture notes, for example, that the art museum and the Kano house open on different days, so check each before you go (same source).

At the foot of the castle hill, the castle-ruins park has a history museum, a rebuilt drum tower and the restored study of Shimoda Utako, founder of Jissen Women's University. It is free and always open, about 30 minutes on foot from Iwamura Station, with free parking by the museum ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_8388.html)).

## FAQ

### How do I get to Iwamura by train?

Take the Akechi Railway from JR Ena Station; Iwamura is about 30 minutes away ([Iwamura](https://iwamura.jp/access), as of November 2026).

### How long does it take to see Iwamura and the castle ruins?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site's walking route from the station through the old town to the castle ruins and back takes about 6 hours ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/model/detail_95.html)).

### How high is Iwamura Castle?

The castle ruins stand at 713 m, and the path up has many stone steps (same source).

### Is the castle-ruins park free?

Yes. The park at the foot of the castle hill is free and open at all times ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_8388.html)).
