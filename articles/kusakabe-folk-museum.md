The Kusakabe Folk Museum is one of the old merchant houses you can walk into in Takayama. This guide uses the museum's own English and Japanese pages, which differ on opening hours, so both are shown.

## Kusakabe Folk Museum at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| What it is | The Kusakabe family's merchant house, rebuilt in 1879 after the fire of 1875 |
| Designation | National Important Cultural Property (1966) |
| Adult admission | ¥1,000 |
| High school / elementary and junior high | ¥500 / ¥300; infants free |
| Hours (Japanese page) | 10:00–16:00, closed Tuesdays (next day if Tuesday is a holiday) |
| Hours (English page) | 9:00–16:30 March–November; 9:00–16:00 December–February, closed Tuesdays December–February |
| Location | About 5 minutes on foot from the old town |

*Sources: [Kusakabe Folk Museum (Japanese)](https://kusakabe-mingeikan.com/); [Kusakabe Folk Museum (English)](https://kusakabe-mingeikan.com/eng_tour.html), as of November 2026.*

## What can you see inside?

The museum describes a large timber frame with strong beams and posts, an open hearth, a double-height hall, and a Buddhist altar with a hidden mechanism that was saved from the fire of 1875. A second exhibition hall, a two-storey library, displays about 5,000 handicraft items. Free guides are available on request ([Kusakabe Folk Museum](https://kusakabe-mingeikan.com/eng_tour.html), as of November 2026).

The current building was rebuilt in 1879, after the house burned in the great fire of 1875, and it was designated a National Important Cultural Property as a Meiji-era house in 1966 ([Kusakabe Folk Museum](https://kusakabe-mingeikan.com/)).

## When is it open?

The museum's two pages disagree. The Japanese page lists 10:00–16:00, closed every Tuesday (the next day if Tuesday is a public holiday); the English page lists 9:00–16:30 from March to November and 9:00–16:00 from December to February, closed on Tuesdays only in winter (same sources, as of November 2026). To be safe, go between 10:00 and 16:00 and avoid Tuesdays.

## How does it fit into a day in Takayama?

The museum says it is about five minutes on foot from the old town ([Kusakabe Folk Museum](https://kusakabe-mingeikan.com/eng_tour.html)). ## FAQ

### How much is the Kusakabe Folk Museum?

¥1,000 for adults, ¥500 for high school students, ¥300 for elementary and junior high students, and free for infants ([Kusakabe Folk Museum](https://kusakabe-mingeikan.com/), as of November 2026).

### Is the Kusakabe Folk Museum open on Tuesdays?

The Japanese page says it closes every Tuesday (the next day if Tuesday is a holiday), while the English page lists Tuesday closures only from December to February (same sources). Avoid Tuesdays to be safe.

### How old is the Kusakabe house?

The current house was rebuilt in 1879 after the fire of 1875, and it became a National Important Cultural Property in 1966 ([Kusakabe Folk Museum](https://kusakabe-mingeikan.com/)).

### Is there an English guide?

The museum offers free guides on request; its English page doesn't say whether they speak English ([Kusakabe Folk Museum](https://kusakabe-mingeikan.com/eng_tour.html)).
