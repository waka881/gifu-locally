The Gujo highlands in Gifu, about two hours from Nagoya by tour bus, have ski resorts and a farm park that turns into a snow park in winter. This guide is for travellers who want snow without skiing, using the Gifu Prefecture tourism site and the Klook tour pages.

## What is there to do at Bokka no Sato in winter?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site lists snow tubing and sledding, which it recommends for first-timers in the snow, a wide slope where you can build snowmen for free, and a "snow raft" ride, alongside the farm park's animals and craft activities. In the 2025–26 season it was open from December 20 to March 30, 10:00–15:30, closed on Tuesdays (open if the Tuesday is a public holiday). Snow play can be cancelled because of the snow or weather, and fees are on the park's own site ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3237.html)). The park is in Takasu, Gujo, about 4 minutes from the Hirugano Kogen SA smart IC on the Tokai-Hokuriku Expressway, and the Bokka no Sato onsen is next to it (same source).

## Which tour should you choose?

- **For the most snow time and a guide in English:** the Bokka no Sato tour leaves Nagoya at 9:00 and is back at 19:00, with over an hour of sledding and snow tubing, a snow speedboat ride, horse riding and 1 hour 30 minutes at the hot spring ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/37991/)).
- **To add a historic town:** the second tour adds Gujo Hachiman's old streets, with 1 hour of snow play and 1 hour at the onsen; it needs at least 6 people to run, and private groups are also offered ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/187440/)). For Gujo Hachiman itself, see our [Gujo Hachiman day trip guide](gujo-hachiman-day-trip.html).
- **If some of your group want to ski:** the Washigatake bus tour from JR Nagoya Station has lift-ticket, rental and "Playing in the Snow" options, so skiers and non-skiers can share the bus ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/138873/)). Klook's Takasu Snow Park package also has a bus-plus-"YUKINOS" option ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/138789/)). More resorts are compared in [skiing near Nagoya](skiing-near-nagoya.html).

## FAQ

### Where can I play in the snow near Nagoya?

At Bokka no Sato in Gujo, Gifu, which has snow tubing, sledding and a snow raft in winter ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3237.html)).

### Is there a snow day tour from Nagoya without skiing?

Yes. Klook lists a Bokka no Sato snow play and hot spring tour from Nagoya for US$82.45, 9:00–19:00, from December 20, 2026 ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/37991/), as of November 22, 2026).

### When does Bokka no Sato's snow park open?

In the 2025–26 season it ran from December 20 to March 30, 10:00–15:30, closed Tuesdays; 2026–27 dates were not on the prefecture's page as of November 2026 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3237.html)).

### Do the snow tours include lunch?

No. Meals are not included on the Bokka no Sato tours or the Washigatake bus tour (Klook pages above).
