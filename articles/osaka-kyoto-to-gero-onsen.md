Gero Onsen is on the JR Takayama Line between Nagoya and Takayama, so from Osaka or Kyoto you either catch the one direct train of the day or change in Nagoya. This guide uses JR West's timetable, Hida Takayama Tourism, Kyoto City's tourism site and Visit Gifu.

## Osaka or Kyoto to Gero at a glance

| Route | From Osaka | From Kyoto | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct Limited Express Hida 5 | Osaka 7:58 (Shin-Osaka 8:04) → Gero 11:29 | Kyoto 8:31 → Gero 11:29 | No changes; arriving for lunch |
| Shinkansen to Nagoya + Limited Express Hida | Shin-Osaka → Nagoya about 50 min, then about 1 h 30–40 min | Kyoto → Nagoya about 35 min, then about 1 h 30–40 min | Any other time of day |

## Is there a direct train from Osaka or Kyoto to Gero Onsen?

Yes, one a day. JR West's timetable shows Limited Express Hida 5 leaving Osaka at 7:58, Shin-Osaka at 8:04 and Kyoto at 8:31, then stopping at Gifu and arriving in Gero at 11:29 before going on to Takayama (12:14) and Hida Furukawa ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027), timetable for October 27, 2026). That is about 3 hours 30 minutes from Osaka and 3 hours from Kyoto with no change. JR West notes that schedules can change, so check your date.

## How do you get to Gero via Nagoya?

Take the Tokaido Shinkansen to Nagoya: Hida Takayama Tourism gives Shin-Osaka to Nagoya as 50 minutes on the Nozomi ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)), and Kyoto City's tourism site gives Kyoto to Nagoya as about 35 minutes ([Kyoto City Official Travel Guide](https://www.kyoto.travel/en/info/getting-to-kyoto/from-major-cities.html)). Then change to the Limited Express Hida, which Visit Gifu gives as about 1 hour 30–40 minutes from Nagoya to Gero ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya)).

For the Hida ticketless charge, the Hayatoku advance discount and the walk from the station, see [Nagoya to Gero Onsen](nagoya-to-gero-onsen.html).

## Can you do Gero Onsen as a day trip from Osaka or Kyoto?

It's possible but long: about 3 hours or more each way, and the direct train arrives only at 11:29, leaving an afternoon in town. Gero is better as an overnight stop, with an evening bath and a ryokan dinner. See [is Gero Onsen worth visiting?](is-gero-onsen-worth-visiting.html), [where to stay in Gero Onsen](where-to-stay-gero-onsen.html) and the [Gero Onsen guide](gero-onsen-guide.html) for baths and foot baths.

## Should you combine Gero with Takayama?

Usually yes. The same direct Hida continues to Takayama, and JR Central gives Gero to Takayama as about 45–60 minutes by train ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/itineraries/takayama-gero-craftsmanship-onsen/)). You can spend a night in Gero, then go on to Takayama the next morning; see [Osaka or Kyoto to Takayama](osaka-kyoto-to-takayama.html), [Gero vs Okuhida](gero-vs-okuhida-onsen.html) and our [Gifu 3-day itinerary](gifu-3-day-itinerary.html).

## FAQ

### How long does it take from Osaka to Gero Onsen?

About 3 hours 30 minutes on the direct Hida (Osaka 7:58, Gero 11:29), or about 50 minutes by Shinkansen to Nagoya plus about 1 hour 30–40 minutes on the Hida ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027); [Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya)).

### Is there a direct train from Kyoto to Gero?

Yes, one a day: Limited Express Hida 5 leaves Kyoto at 8:31 and reaches Gero at 11:29 (JR West timetable, October 2026).

### Is Gero Onsen on the way to Takayama?

Yes. It is on the JR Takayama Line, about 45–60 minutes by train before Takayama ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/itineraries/takayama-gero-craftsmanship-onsen/)).
