Shirakawa-go is a working village, so a few practical things work differently from a city sight. Everything below comes from the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association's own FAQ.

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Time needed | About 2–3 hours on foot |
| Walking around the village | Free |
| Inside individual houses | ¥300–¥400 per person; open-air museum ¥600 |

## How long do you need, and what does it cost?

The Tourist Association says walking around the village takes about 2–3 hours. Walking around is free; entering individual houses and facilities costs ¥300–¥400 per person, and the open-air museum costs ¥600. There is no sightseeing bus inside the village, because everything is within walking distance ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of November 2026).

## Where can you leave luggage?

At Shirakawago Bus Terminal, where coin lockers are available from 8:00 to 17:00: ¥500 per use for small and medium boxes and ¥1,000 for large ones. There is also a luggage storage service next to the terminal, open until 17:00 (same source). If you are carrying large bags between Takayama and Kanazawa, plan to arrive and leave within those hours. Bus times are in [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) and [Shirakawa-go from Kanazawa](shirakawa-go-from-kanazawa.html).

## How do you get up to the observatory?

A shuttle runs from in front of Wada House every 20 minutes from 10:00 to 14:40, leaving at :00, :20 and :40 and coming back at :10, :30 and :50, with a break from 12:00 to 13:00. It costs ¥200 one way, paid in cash on the bus (same source).

## Is there anywhere to throw away rubbish or change money?

There are no trash cans in the village, to protect the scenery; take rubbish back to where you bought it or home with you. For cash, an automatic exchange machine at the bus terminal converts 12 currencies, including US dollars, euros, pounds, Australian dollars, yuan, won, Hong Kong and Taiwan dollars, rupiah, baht, Singapore dollars and ringgit, into yen (same source). Bring enough yen for the shuttle, which is cash only.

## FAQ

### Are there coin lockers in Shirakawa-go?

Yes, at Shirakawago Bus Terminal, 8:00–17:00: ¥500 for small or medium boxes and ¥1,000 for large ones, plus a luggage storage service next door until 17:00 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of November 2026).

### Is there an entrance fee for Shirakawa-go?

No. Walking around the village is free; individual houses charge ¥300–¥400 and the open-air museum ¥600 (same source).

### How do I get to the Shirakawa-go observatory?

By the shuttle from in front of Wada House, every 20 minutes from 10:00 to 14:40 (no service 12:00–13:00), ¥200 one way in cash (same source).

### Can I exchange money in Shirakawa-go?

Yes. An automatic exchange machine at the bus terminal converts 12 currencies to yen (same source).
