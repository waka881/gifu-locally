## Where are the sake breweries in Takayama?

JNTO says Takayama has seven sake breweries, all with a history of more than 100 years, and that most are in the Sanmachi old town a short walk east of JR Takayama Station; about 330 years ago, the town reportedly had 56 ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/153/)). Hida Takayama Tourism describes the Sanmachi area as having many sake breweries marked by cedar-leaf balls, and puts it 12 minutes on foot from the station ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)).

One example from Gifu Prefecture's product site: Funasaka Shuzoten, at 105 Kamisannomachi in the old town, which the site says is housed in a 200-year-old building ([Gateway to Gifu Products](https://gifuproduct.jp/sp/english/company/detail/index/FOD0139)).

## How does sake tasting work in Takayama?

You can walk around town and sample sake from the breweries; JNTO notes that Harada Sake Brewery offers more than 10 types and that you keep the cup as a souvenir ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/153/)). Opening days and tasting fees vary by brewery and aren't listed by JNTO, so check at each door or on the brewery's own site.

| Option | What you get | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Walk in and taste | Tastings at individual breweries, at each one's own fee and hours | Flexible, self-paced |
| Guided tasting (Klook) | 30 min at Hirase Sake Brewery, at least five sakes, with an English explanation of sake basics; from about US$46 | Understanding what you're drinking |
| With a meal | JNTO says local sake is also served at restaurants around the city | Pairing with Hida beef |

## What should you know before tasting?

- **Drinking age:** 20 in Japan; Klook's Takayama sake listings ask for adults aged 20 and over with ID.
- **Go after the morning markets:** the markets close at noon and the breweries are in the same part of town; see our [Takayama morning market guide](takayama-morning-market.html).
- **Eat something too:** pair sake with Hida beef; see [Hida beef in Takayama](hida-beef-takayama.html).
- **Don't drive:** stay in town and walk. See [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html).

## FAQ

### How many sake breweries are there in Takayama?

JNTO says there are seven, all with a history of over 100 years, mostly in the Sanmachi old town ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/153/)).

### What does the cedar ball outside a brewery mean?

The ball of cedar leaves (sugidama) hanging over the door marks a sake brewery (same source).

### Can I do a guided sake tasting in English in Takayama?

Yes. Klook lists a 30-minute English-guided tasting at Hirase Sake Brewery with at least five sakes, from about US$46 ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/activity/35490-sake-brewery-tour-takayama/), as of October 2026).

### How far are the breweries from Takayama Station?

Most are in Sanmachi, about a 12-minute walk from JR Takayama Station ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)).
