## How cold and snowy is Takayama in January?

It is the coldest month of the year. The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages give −1.2°C for January, with daily highs of 3.2°C and lows of −4.8°C, and 117 cm of snowfall over the month, more than any other month ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)). The full year is in [Takayama weather by month](takayama-weather-by-month.html). Bring a warm coat, gloves and shoes with grip for icy streets.

## What is open in Takayama in January?

Most of the town. Takayama Jinya is closed from December 29 to January 3 and then opens 8:45–16:30 until March ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)). The Miyagawa and Jinya-mae morning markets run all year, from 8:00 to 12:00 from December to March ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)). If you arrive over New Year, see [Takayama at New Year](takayama-new-year.html) for what else closes; for the rest of the season, see [Takayama in winter](takayama-in-winter.html).

## What events are on in January 2027?

- **Hida Folk Village winter illumination:** the thatched farmhouses are lit every evening from January 9 to February 28, 2027, 17:30–20:30 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3323.html)). Prices and buses are in our [Hida Folk Village illumination guide](hida-folk-village-winter-illumination.html).
- **Shirakawa-go light-up:** on January 11, 17, 24 and 31, 2027, lit 17:30–19:30, with Nohi Bus light-up tours from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center on all four dates ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)). You need a reservation to go; see the [Shirakawa-go light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html).
- **Winter illumination in town:** Hida Takayama Tourism listed lights in the Nakabashi Bridge and Miyagawa River area from December 1 to March 15 in 2025–26, 16:30–21:30; check the [event calendar](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000181.html) for this winter.

For a night in an onsen town, Gero Onsen holds Saturday-night winter fireworks from January; the 2027 dates are in our [Gero Onsen winter fireworks guide](gero-onsen-winter-fireworks.html) once announced.

## FAQ

### Is January a good time to visit Takayama?

If you want snow and light-ups, yes: January 2027 has the Hida Folk Village illumination from January 9 and the Shirakawa-go light-up on four evenings. It is also the coldest month, averaging −1.2°C (sources above).

### How much snow is there in Takayama in January?

The Japan Meteorological Agency's averages give 117 cm of snowfall in January, the most of any month ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)).

### Is Takayama Jinya open in January?

It is closed from December 29 to January 3, then open 8:45–16:30 ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)).

### When is the Shirakawa-go light-up in 2027?

January 11, 17, 24 and 31, 2027, from 17:30 to 19:30, with a reservation required ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)).
