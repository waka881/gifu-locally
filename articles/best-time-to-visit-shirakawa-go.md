Shirakawa-go looks very different through the year: deep snow in winter, cherry blossoms in spring, green rice fields in summer and coloured mountains in autumn. This guide lists what each season offers, using dates from the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association, and the travel points that change with the season.

## Shirakawa-go by season at a glance

| Season | What you see | Official timing | Travel notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Winter | Snow on the thatched roofs; light-up evenings | Snow can reach 170 cm or more in February; 2027 light-up on Jan 11, 17, 24, 31 | Bus delays possible; light-up needs reservations |
| Spring | Snow melting, then cherry blossoms | Snow melts around March; cherry blossoms mid-April to early May | Golden Week (late April to early May) is a Japanese holiday period |
| Summer | Green scenery and mountain views | No specific dates given | Easiest roads; long days for walking |
| Autumn | Coloured leaves around the village | Late October to mid-November | A popular season; book buses early |

*Source: [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/highlights/), as of October 2026, unless noted below.*

## When is the best time to see Shirakawa-go in snow?

Winter is the image most people have of Shirakawa-go. The Tourist Association says February snow can reach 170 cm or more, and the highest snowfall on record was 4.5 m. The steep gassho roofs are built for this snow ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/highlights/), as of October 2026).

What to plan for in winter:

- **Bus delays.** Gifu Bus says highway buses can be delayed by road and weather conditions, and it does not refund fares or cover missed connections caused by delays ([Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html), as of October 2026). Leave a buffer before onward trains or flights.
- **Shorter opening hours.** For example, the Gassho-zukuri Minkaen open-air museum opens 9:00–16:00 from December to February and closes on Thursdays from December to March ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/active/7/)).
- **Warm, waterproof shoes.** You walk everywhere in the village.

Our [Shirakawa-go in winter guide](shirakawa-go-in-winter.html) covers driving rules, what to wear and winter hours, and the [Gifu winter guide](gifu-winter-guide.html) has more on travelling in snow.

## When is the Shirakawa-go light-up in 2027?

The 41st Shirakawa-go Light-Up takes place on four days in 2027: **January 11, 17, 24 and 31** ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/), as of October 2026).

It is the most difficult time to visit:

- The 2026 event was run fully by advance reservation, and people without a booking could not take part ([Gifu Prefecture tourism site](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1287.html)).
- For 2027, the first round of standard car parking reservations is sold out, and lodging handled by the Tourist Association is allocated by lottery ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/), as of October 2026).
- On normal nights, day visitors are not admitted to the village after dark; only overnight guests are ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/106/)). So outside light-up evenings, seeing the village at night means staying there.

Full details are in our [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html). Many visitors go on an organised tour that includes the required reservations.

## When do the cherry blossoms bloom in Shirakawa-go?

The Tourist Association says the snow melts around March and cherry blossoms bloom from **mid-April to early May** ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/highlights/), as of October 2026).

The end of this window overlaps with Golden Week, a run of Japanese public holidays from late April to early May, when domestic travel is busy. Book bus seats as soon as reservations open, one month ahead ([Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html)).

## Is summer a good time to visit Shirakawa-go?

Summer brings fresh green scenery and mountain views ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/highlights/)). It is the easiest season for travel logistics: no snow on the roads, and long days for walking up to the Shiroyama viewpoint. The viewpoint shuttle runs daily from 10:00 to 14:40 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)).

## When are the autumn leaves in Shirakawa-go?

The Tourist Association says the best time for autumn leaves is **late October to mid-November** ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/highlights/), as of October 2026). Combine it with Takayama, about 50 minutes away by express bus ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)), for a two-day autumn trip; see our [Takayama 2-day itinerary](takayama-2-day-itinerary.html).

## How can you avoid the crowds?

- **Stay overnight.** The Tourist Association's accommodation search lists 41 places to stay, including gassho-style farmhouse inns ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/stay/)). Overnight guests can walk the village in the early morning and evening.
- **Avoid the light-up evenings and Japanese holiday periods** unless they are the reason you are going.
- **Expect tour-bus management from December 2026.** The village is introducing advance reservations for tour buses at its Seseragi Park Oro parking lot from December 1, 2026, to ease congestion ([Shirakawa Village](https://www.vill.shirakawa.lg.jp/2897.htm)).

## FAQ

### What month is best to visit Shirakawa-go?

It depends on the scenery you want. Winter brings deep snow, with February snow reaching 170 cm or more; late October to mid-November is the autumn leaf season; and cherry blossoms bloom from mid-April to early May ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/highlights/), as of October 2026).

### When is there snow in Shirakawa-go?

Snow covers the village in winter and melts around March, according to the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association. February snow can reach 170 cm or more.

### When is the Shirakawa-go light-up 2027?

January 11, 17, 24 and 31, 2027 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/), as of October 2026). The 2026 event was reservation-only, so check the official rules before planning.

### Can I see Shirakawa-go at night?

Only on light-up evenings or if you stay overnight. On other nights, day visitors are not admitted to the village after dark ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/106/)).
