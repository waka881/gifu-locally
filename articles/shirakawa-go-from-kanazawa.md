If you are staying in Kanazawa, Shirakawa-go is one of the easiest day trips you can make: the bus ride is shorter than from Nagoya or Takayama. This guide covers the express bus, what to book, a sample day and the guided-tour option, using figures from the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association and Nohi Bus.

## Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Bus time | About 1 h 20 min from Kanazawa Station to Shirakawago Bus Terminal (about 1 h 30 min door to village) |
| Adult fare | ¥2,800 one way (Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa) |
| Booking | Reserved services can be booked through Japan Bus Online |
| Time in the village | About 2–3 hours to walk around |
| Winter | Delays of two hours or more can occur |
| Alternative | Guided day tour from Kanazawa, 8–10 hours, adding Takayama |

*Sources: [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/); [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026.*

## How do you get from Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go by bus?

The express bus leaves from Kanazawa Station and takes about 1 hour 20 minutes to Shirakawago Bus Terminal. The village is about 1 minute on foot from the terminal, so the whole trip is about 1 hour 30 minutes ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/), as of October 2026).

The bus is part of the line that runs between Takayama, Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa, Takaoka or Toyama. Nohi Bus lists the adult fare between Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa as **¥2,800 one way**, and between Shirakawa-go and Toyama as ¥2,400 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026).

**Reservations:** services marked "R" on the timetable need a reserved seat, and reserved services can be booked online through Japan Bus Online. Mobile tickets are only for non-reserved buses (same source). The Tourist Association also links to the reservation pages of Gifu Bus, Nohi Bus and Hokuriku Hida 3-Star Road ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)). Book your return bus at the same time as the outbound one: it sets how long you can stay.

**Frequency:** Nohi Bus lists 16 round trips a day on the line, but some services do not run on weekends, national holidays, August 13–15 or December 29–January 3 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).

Flying in? The Tourist Association says the Komatsu Airport Limousine Bus reaches Kanazawa Station in about 40 minutes, where you change to the express bus ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)).

## What can you see in a day?

The Tourist Association estimates that walking around the village takes about 2–3 hours ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026). A simple plan:

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| Morning | Bus from Kanazawa Station |
| First hour | Shiroyama viewpoint by shuttle from in front of Wada House (¥200 one way, cash; runs 10:00–14:40, with a break 12:00–13:00) |
| Late morning | Wada House (¥400, cash only) and the village streets |
| Early afternoon | Lunch, then Gassho-zukuri Minkaen open-air museum (¥600) |
| Afternoon | Bus back to Kanazawa, or on to Takayama |

*Sources: [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/); [Wada House](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/active/13/); [Gassho-zukuri Minkaen](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/active/7/), as of October 2026.*

Go up to the viewpoint early: the shuttle's last run is 14:40. More on each sight is in our [Shirakawa-go guide](shirakawa-go-from-nagoya.html).

## What should you know before you go?

- **No night visits for day-trippers.** Day visitors are not admitted to the village after dark; only overnight guests are, except on light-up evenings ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/106/)).
- **Carry cash** for the viewpoint shuttle and Wada House.
- **Take your trash with you.** There are no trash cans in the village ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)).
- **Luggage:** coin lockers at Shirakawago Bus Terminal cost ¥500 or ¥1,000 per use from 8:00 to 17:00 (same FAQ). If you are moving on to Takayama the same day, this lets you visit with your suitcase.
- **Winter:** Nohi Bus warns that delays of two hours or more can occur in winter, because of traffic waiting for parking at Shirakawa-go, heavy snow or expressway closures ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Don't plan a tight connection afterwards. See [the best time to visit Shirakawa-go](best-time-to-visit-shirakawa-go.html) and our [2027 light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html).

Yes. The same line continues from Shirakawa-go to Takayama in about 50 minutes for ¥2,800 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). ## FAQ

### How long is the bus from Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go?

About 1 hour 20 minutes from Kanazawa Station to Shirakawago Bus Terminal, or about 1 hour 30 minutes including the short walk to the village ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/), as of October 2026).

### How much is the bus from Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go?

Nohi Bus lists the adult fare between Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa as ¥2,800 one way ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026).

### Do I need to reserve the bus from Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go?

Check the timetable: services marked "R" need a reserved seat, and they can be booked online through Japan Bus Online ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Booking ahead is the safest choice, especially for your return.

### Is Shirakawa-go worth a day trip from Kanazawa?

If you want to see the thatched gassho-style farmhouses, it is one of the shortest ways to reach them, with about 2–3 hours needed in the village. Read [Is Shirakawa-go worth visiting?](is-shirakawa-go-worth-visiting.html) to decide.
