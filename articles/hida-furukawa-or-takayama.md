Hida Furukawa is a small castle town one stop beyond Takayama on the JR Takayama Line. It has the same kind of old streets as Takayama on a smaller, quieter scale. This guide compares the two using the Hida City tourism site and JR West, and shows how to add Furukawa to a Takayama stay.

## Hida Furukawa vs Takayama at a glance

| | Hida Furukawa | Takayama |
|---|---|---|
| Feel | Small, quiet castle town | Larger old town, the region's main base |
| Signature sight | Seto River canal and white-walled storehouses | Sanmachi old town, Takayama Jinya |
| Getting there | About 15 min by train from Takayama; about 2 h 40 min from Nagoya | About 2 h 20 min from Nagoya by Limited Express Hida |

## What is there to see in Hida Furukawa?

- **Seto River and the white-walled storehouse street.** Hida City describes this as the face of the town: white storehouses along a clear canal, about 5 minutes on foot from Hida-Furukawa Station. Carp swim in the canal from April to November; from late November to early April they are moved to the pond at the Masujima Castle ruins ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/spot/278), as of October 2026). The 2026 move is listed for November 29 ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/event/425)).
- **Hida Furukawa Festival Hall.** Festival floats on display, 3-D footage of the Furukawa Festival and wind-up doll performances. Open 9:00–17:00 from March to November and 9:00–16:30 from December to February, closed December 28 to January 2; ¥500 for adults; about 5 minutes on foot from the station ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/ticket/hokuriku-arch-pass/Explore_Japan/spot/gifu/hida-furukawa-festival-hall/index.html), as of October 2026).
- **Sake breweries.** The "Hida Furukawa Sankura Mairi" event on November 3, 2026 takes place around the Furukawa Festival Plaza, Kamo Brewery and Watanabe Brewery ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/event/861)).

## When is the Furukawa Festival?

Every year on **April 19 and 20**. On the night of April 19, the Okoshi Daiko procession carries a large drum through town late into the night, and on the night of April 20 lantern-lit floats tour the streets. The float parade is cancelled in rain, but the Okoshi Daiko goes ahead. The Okoshi Daiko and float events are registered as UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/event/408), as of October 2026).

The Spring Takayama Festival is on April 14–15 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/4000105.html)), so a mid-April trip can take in both; book a Takayama hotel early for those dates.

## How do you get from Takayama to Hida Furukawa?

By JR train on the Takayama Line, about 15 minutes ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/features/132)). Check the return train times at Takayama Station or on [JR Central's train information pages](https://traininfo.jr-central.co.jp/zairaisen/index.html?lang=en) before you go. From October 1, 2025, TOICA IC cards can be used at Takayama and Hida-Furukawa stations for the base fare ([JR Central](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044393.pdf)).

From Nagoya, Hida City gives about 2 hours 40 minutes by train or 2 hours 45 minutes by bus; from Toyama, about 1 hour 15 minutes by train (same source).

## Which should you choose?

**Stay in Takayama** in almost every case: it has more places to stay, the morning markets and the Nohi Bus Center for Shirakawa-go, Okuhida and Kamikochi. See [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html).

**Add Hida Furukawa** as a half day if you have a second or third day in Takayama, want a quieter old town, or are visiting from April to November when the carp are in the canal. A morning in Furukawa and an afternoon in Takayama fits easily. ## FAQ

### Is Hida Furukawa worth visiting?

Yes, as a half day from Takayama if you want a quieter old town: the Seto River canal with its white-walled storehouses and carp (April to November) and the Festival Hall are both about 5 minutes on foot from the station ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/spot/278)).

### How far is Hida Furukawa from Takayama?

About 15 minutes by JR train on the Takayama Line ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/features/132), as of October 2026).

### When can you see the carp in Hida Furukawa?

From April to November. From late November to early April they are moved to the pond at the Masujima Castle ruins (same source).

### When is the Furukawa Festival?

April 19 and 20 every year, with the Okoshi Daiko drum procession on the night of April 19 ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/event/408)).
