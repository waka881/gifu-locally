The bus is the direct way from Takayama to Kanazawa, and it passes through Shirakawa-go, so you can see the village on the way. This guide lists the through buses and fares from the Nohi Bus timetable and explains how to break the journey in Shirakawa-go.

## Takayama to Kanazawa at a glance

| | Through bus | With a stop in Shirakawa-go |
|---|---|---|
| From | Takayama Nohi Bus Center (next to JR Takayama Station) | Same |
| To | Kanazawa Station | Same |
| Buses | 4 a day | Takayama–Shirakawa-go many a day; Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa several a day |
| Time on the bus | About 2 h 15 min | About 50 min + about 1 h 15 min |
| Adult fare (one way) | ¥4,200 | ¥2,800 + ¥2,800 = ¥5,600 |
| Reservations | Required | Required on most buses |

*Source: [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), checked October 24, 2026. Timetables change; check the current one for your date.*

## How do you stop in Shirakawa-go on the way?

Buy two separate tickets and pick a later bus for the second leg. The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association suggests 2–3 hours to walk around the village ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)).

| Plan | Takayama → Shirakawa-go | Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa |
|---|---|---|
| Morning in the village | 8:10 → 9:00 | 11:25 → 12:50 or 12:20 → 13:35 |
| Late morning start | 9:35 → 10:25 | 13:50 → 15:05 or 15:10 → 16:35 |

*Times from the [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/) timetable, checked October 2026. Other departures from Shirakawa-go to Kanazawa include 10:25, 10:55, 15:55 and 17:30.*

**Luggage:** coin lockers at the Shirakawago Bus Terminal cost ¥500 or ¥1,000 per use and are available 8:00–17:00 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026). Nohi Bus warns that very large items may not fit in the bus trunk, and asks you to keep mobile batteries with you, not in the trunk ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).

For what to see in the village, see [Is Shirakawa-go worth visiting?](is-shirakawa-go-worth-visiting.html) and the full list of Takayama departures in [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html).

## How do you book?

- **Online:** reserved buses are booked through [Japan Bus Online](https://japanbusonline.com/en/CourseSearch/11900040002) ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).
- **By phone:** Nohi Bus Reservation Center, 9:00–17:00, or the Hokuriku Tetsudo Reservation Center for the Kanazawa line, 9:00–18:00 (same source).

With a stopover, book both legs: the Shirakawa-go to Kanazawa buses also need reservations.

## Winter travel

Nohi Bus warns that delays of two hours or more can happen on this line, caused by traffic waiting to park at Shirakawa-go, heavy snow or expressway closures ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Avoid booking a tight train or flight connection in Kanazawa on the same day. ## FAQ

### How long is the bus from Takayama to Kanazawa?

About 2 hours 15 minutes on the through buses, which stop at Shirakawa-go on the way ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026).

### How much is the bus from Takayama to Kanazawa?

¥4,200 one way for adults from the Takayama bus terminal to Kanazawa Station (same source).

### Can I stop at Shirakawa-go between Takayama and Kanazawa?

Yes. Buy a Takayama–Shirakawa-go ticket (¥2,800) and a Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa ticket (¥2,800), and use the coin lockers at the Shirakawago Bus Terminal for your bags, available 8:00–17:00 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)).

### Do I need to reserve the Takayama to Kanazawa bus?

Yes. All four through buses are reservation only; book on Japan Bus Online or by phone ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).
