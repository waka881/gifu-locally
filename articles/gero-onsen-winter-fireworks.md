Gero Onsen holds short fireworks shows over the Hida River through the winter, so on the right night you can watch them on the way back from dinner or a bath. This guide uses the Gifu Prefecture tourism site and the Gero fireworks site.

## Gero Onsen winter fireworks at a glance

| | Fireworks Musical (winter) | Hanabi Monogatari |
|---|---|---|
| Dates | December 5, 12, 19 and 24, 2026 | Every Saturday, January–March (Jan 10–Mar 28 in 2026) |
| Time | 20:00–20:15; Christmas show December 24, 20:00–20:30 | 20:30, about 10 minutes |
| Launched from | Upstream of Gero Bridge (Shirasagi Ryokuchi Park), about 5 minutes on foot from JR Gero Station | Hida River bank downstream of Gero Bridge |
| Cost to watch | Not listed for 2026; paid riverside seats were sold in 2023 | Free |
| Weather | Postponed in storms or high water (past years) | Cancelled in storms or high water |

*Sources: [Gifu Prefecture tourism, Fireworks Musical winter shows](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1279.html); [Gifu Prefecture tourism, Hanabi Monogatari](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3279.html); [Gero fireworks site](https://gero-hanabi.com/), as of November 2026.*

## When are the December 2026 fireworks?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site lists the winter run of the Gero Onsen Fireworks Musical on Saturdays December 5, 12 and 19, 2026, from 20:00 to 20:15, plus a Christmas special on Thursday, December 24, from 20:00 to 20:30. They are launched upstream of Gero Bridge, at Shirasagi Ryokuchi Park, about 5 minutes on foot from JR Gero Station ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1279.html), as of November 2026).

The prefecture's listing doesn't give a price. The Gero fireworks site, which still shows the 2023 shows, sold 55 riverside seats per show (¥5,000, or ¥10,000 for the Christmas show), with about 7,000 fireworks at a regular show and about 11,000 at the special ([Gero fireworks site](https://gero-hanabi.com/)). Treat those as last published figures, not 2026 prices.

## When are the January–March fireworks?

The "Hanabi Monogatari" (Fireworks Story) runs every Saturday in winter. For 2026 it was January 10 to March 28, 12 nights in all, at 20:30 for about 10 minutes, launched from the Hida River bank downstream of Gero Bridge. It was free and cancelled in storms or when the river was high ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3279.html)). The 2027 dates had not been posted when we checked in November 2026; the Gero Tourist Information Center (+81-576-25-4711) is the contact the prefecture lists.

## How do you plan a fireworks night in Gero?

Stay the night. The shows start at 20:00 or 20:30, so a Saturday in a ryokan lets you watch after dinner without worrying about a train. The Limited Express Hida takes about 1 hour 30–40 minutes from Nagoya; see [Nagoya to Gero Onsen](nagoya-to-gero-onsen.html), [Takayama to Gero Onsen](takayama-to-gero-onsen.html) and [Osaka or Kyoto to Gero Onsen](osaka-kyoto-to-gero-onsen.html).

## FAQ

### When are the Gero Onsen fireworks in December 2026?

December 5, 12 and 19 from 20:00 to 20:15, and a Christmas show on December 24 from 20:00 to 20:30 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1279.html), as of November 2026).

### Are there fireworks in Gero in January and February?

Yes, in past years: the Hanabi Monogatari ran every Saturday from January 10 to March 28, 2026, at 20:30 for about 10 minutes ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3279.html)). The 2027 dates had not been posted in mid-November 2026.

### Do you have to pay to watch?

The Hanabi Monogatari was free. The prefecture's listing for the December shows gives no price; in 2023 the organisers sold paid riverside seats (same sources).

### How far is it from Gero Station?

The December shows are launched near Shirasagi Ryokuchi Park, about 5 minutes on foot from JR Gero Station ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1279.html)).
