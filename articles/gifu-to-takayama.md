Gifu City and Takayama are both in Gifu Prefecture, about two hours apart. You can take the Limited Express Hida from JR Gifu Station or the express bus from Meitetsu Gifu, which runs via Gujo Hachiman. This guide uses JR West's timetable and Nohi Bus.

## Gifu to Takayama at a glance

| | Limited Express Hida (train) | Express bus |
|---|---|---|
| From | JR Gifu Station | Meitetsu Gifu (stop No. 6) |
| Time | About 2 h (e.g. 10:12 → 12:14) | About 2 h |
| Adult fare | Check JR | ¥2,900 one way |
| Departures | Check JR for your date | 7:40, 10:30, 12:30, 15:20, 17:05, 19:10 |
| Arrives | JR Takayama Station | Takayama Bus Terminal, next to JR Takayama Station |
| Booking | Reserve a seat (all reserved December 25–January 5) | All seats reserved; bookings open one month ahead |

*Sources: [JR West timetable, Limited Express Hida 5/25](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027) and [Hida 16](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/77031?date=20261027), timetables for October 27, 2026; [Nohi Bus, Gifu–Takayama](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/); [Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/), as of November 2026.*

## How long is the train from Gifu to Takayama?

About 2 hours. On JR West's timetable, the Limited Express Hida leaves Gifu at 10:12 and arrives in Takayama at 12:14, stopping at Mino-Ota and Gero on the way ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027)). Going back, Hida 16 leaves Takayama at 15:34 and reaches Gifu at 17:37 ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/77031?date=20261027)). Both timetables are for October 27, 2026, so check your date.

Over the New Year, the pass site says all Hida seats are reserved from December 25, 2026 to January 5, 2027 ([Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/)), so get a seat reservation before boarding. Want a hot spring stop on the way? The same trains stop at Gero; see [Takayama to Gero Onsen](takayama-to-gero-onsen.html).

## What about the express bus from Gifu?

Nohi Bus lists a reserved express bus from Meitetsu Gifu (stop No. 6) to the Takayama Bus Terminal, about 2 hours, stopping at Gujo Hachiman IC (boarding only towards Takayama) ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/)):

*Source: [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/), as of November 2026. Fares from Takayama: ¥2,900 to Gifu, ¥2,400 to Gujo Hachiman IC; a 4-ticket book to Gifu costs ¥9,900 (valid 3 months).*

Reservations are required, open one month ahead, and can be made online at highwaybus.com or by phone; round-trip tickets aren't sold on the bus (same source). The bus suits a stop in [Gujo Hachiman](gujo-hachiman-day-trip.html), the castle town known for its [food samples](gujo-hachiman-food-samples.html).

## Train or bus: which should you choose?

- **Take the Hida** if you are staying near JR Gifu Station, or want to stop at Gero Onsen.
- **Take the bus** if you are near Meitetsu Gifu, want a fixed fare of ¥2,900, or plan to stop at Gujo Hachiman.

Both arrive next to JR Takayama Station. For your first day there, see [things to do in Takayama](things-to-do-in-takayama.html) and [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html).

## FAQ

### How long does it take from Gifu to Takayama?

About 2 hours by Limited Express Hida from JR Gifu or by express bus from Meitetsu Gifu ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/)).

### How much is the bus from Gifu to Takayama?

¥2,900 one way, with all seats reserved ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/), as of November 2026).

### Where does the Gifu–Takayama bus leave from?

Meitetsu Gifu, stop No. 6. Nohi Bus lists no JR Gifu stop for this bus (same source).

### Does the Gifu–Takayama bus stop at Gujo Hachiman?

Yes, at Gujo Hachiman IC, about 45 minutes after leaving Gifu (same source).
