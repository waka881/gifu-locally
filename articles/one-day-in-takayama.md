Takayama is compact enough to see its main sights in one day on foot, plus one short bus ride. This plan uses opening hours and fees from Hida Takayama Tourism, Takayama Jinya and Hida Folk Village.

## One day in Takayama at a glance

| Time | Stop | Cost | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early morning | Jinya-mae or Miyagawa morning market | Free | 7:00–12:00 (Apr–Nov); 8:00–12:00 (Dec–Mar) |
| Mid-morning | Takayama Jinya | ¥500 (18 and under free) | 8:45–17:00 (Apr–Oct); 8:45–16:30 (Nov–Mar) |
| Late morning | Sanmachi old town and sake breweries | Free to walk | Shops vary |
| Lunch | Hida beef | Varies | Varies |
| Afternoon | Hida Folk Village, by Sarubobo Bus (about 10 min, ¥210 each way) | ¥700 | 8:30–17:00 |

## Where should you start the day?

At a morning market. Takayama has two: Miyagawa, about 350 metres along the river with around 60 stalls and about 15 minutes on foot from JR Takayama Station, and Jinya-mae, in front of Takayama Jinya. Both open 7:00–12:00 from April to November and 8:00–12:00 from December to March, all year round. Hida Takayama Tourism notes that stalls can drop to about 10 in rain or winter ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html), as of November 2026). See our [Takayama morning market guide](takayama-morning-market.html).

## What time does Takayama Jinya open?

At 8:45. It closes at 17:00 from April to October and 16:30 from November to March; the ticket window closes 30 minutes earlier, and it is closed December 29–January 3. Admission is ¥500 for adults and free for those 18 and under ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/), as of November 2026). Since the Jinya-mae market is right outside, the two fit together. See our [Takayama Jinya guide](takayama-jinya.html).

## What should you see in the old town?

The Sanmachi merchant quarter is about 12 minutes on foot from JR Takayama Station ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)). JNTO says Takayama has seven sake breweries, all over 100 years old ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/153/)); most are in or near the old town. See [Takayama sake breweries](takayama-sake-breweries.html).

For lunch, Hida beef is the local specialty, served as street snacks or sit-down meals; see [Hida beef in Takayama](hida-beef-takayama.html).

## How do you get to Hida Folk Village in the afternoon?

Take the Sarubobo Bus from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center, next to JR Takayama Station, to Hida-no-Sato-Shita: about 10 minutes and ¥210 each way. The village is open 8:30–17:00 and costs ¥700 for adults ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html), as of November 2026). See our [Hida Folk Village guide](hida-folk-village.html).

## Can you do this as a day trip from Nagoya?

Only partly. The Limited Express Hida takes about 2 hours 20 minutes from Nagoya ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/)), so a day-tripper usually arrives late in the morning, close to when the markets close at noon. If you can, sleep in Takayama the night before and follow the plan from the start. See [Takayama from Nagoya](takayama-from-nagoya.html) and [how many days in Takayama](how-many-days-in-takayama.html).

## FAQ

### Is one day enough for Takayama?

For the main sights, yes: a morning market, Takayama Jinya, the old town and Hida Folk Village fit in one day. Add a second night for Shirakawa-go. See [how many days in Takayama](how-many-days-in-takayama.html).

### What time do the Takayama morning markets close?

At 12:00. They open at 7:00 from April to November and at 8:00 from December to March ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html), as of November 2026).

### How much does Takayama Jinya cost?

¥500 for adults, free for those 18 and under ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/), as of November 2026).

### How do I get to Hida Folk Village from Takayama Station?

By the Sarubobo Bus from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center, about 10 minutes and ¥210 each way ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html), as of November 2026).
