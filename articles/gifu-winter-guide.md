Winter changes northern Gifu. From December to March, the Hida region is cold and snowy. Thatched farmhouses and old merchant streets sit under snow, and open-air hot spring baths look out on white mountains. It is also the season when the roads need the most care. This guide covers the main winter draws for first-time visitors: the Shirakawa-go light-up, Takayama's winter evenings, the Okuhida onsen villages with the Shinhotaka Ropeway, and Gero Onsen's winter fireworks. It ends with practical advice on getting around in snow.

All dates, prices and hours below come from official pages and are marked "as of October 2026". Many winter events announce their dates late in the year, so check the linked page before you book.

## Shirakawa-go winter light-up 2027

The Shirakawa-go light-up is the village's best-known winter event. For a few evenings each January, the gassho-zukuri farmhouses are lit up after dark. The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association lists the **41st Light-Up** on four days: **January 11 (a public holiday), January 17, January 24 and January 31, 2027** (as of October 2026, [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/)).

Visiting during the light-up takes planning:

- **It is a reservation-only event.** For the previous (2026) edition, Gifu Prefecture's tourism site described it as held under a fully advance-reservation system, with no same-day visits (as of October 2026, [Gifu Prefecture Tourism Federation](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1287.html)). Expect the same approach for 2027 and confirm the rules on the [association's website](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/).
- **Car parking:** The first round of regular car parking reservations (from September 10) is listed as **sold out** (as of October 2026, [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/)).
- **Accommodation:** Rooms handled by the Tourist Association are allocated **by lottery**, not first come, first served (as of October 2026, [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/)).
- **Tour buses:** A tour bus reservation system starts at Seseragi Park Oro Parking from December 1, 2026. This applies to buses, not private cars (as of October 2026, [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/)).

Start times, viewing-spot rules and any fees for 2027 were not set out on the pages checked. Look for them on the association's site, or call the association on +81-5769-6-1013 (9:00–17:00, as of October 2026).

The easiest way to see the light-up without a car is often a reserved light-up bus tour or a package that includes entry. Shirakawa-go is also lovely on ordinary winter days. ## Takayama in winter

For opening hours, every illumination and snow travel tips, see our full guide to [Takayama in winter](takayama-in-winter.html).

Takayama's old town stays open all winter. Its winter evenings also have their own events. Hida Takayama's official tourism site listed these for the 2025–26 season (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama event calendar, January](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000180.html)):

- **Winter Illumination in Takayama:** The snow-covered Nakabashi bridge and Miyagawa River area were lit from December 1, 2025 to March 15, 2026.
- **Winter Illumination of Hida-no-sato (Hida Folk Village):** Historic farmhouses, including steep-roofed ones, were lit from January 10 to February 28, 2026.
- **Nijuyokka-ichi (Fair on the 24th Day):** This is held every year on **January 24**. Local farmers sell handmade crafts such as bamboo baskets and wooden pails on a street closed to traffic. The listing says the fair has run since 1872.

The site's [February calendar](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000184.html) also lists **Setsubun** in early February. On this day, a costumed bean-throwing procession passes Takayama Station, Honmachi Street and Kokubunji Temple.

The 2026–27 dates had not been published when this guide was checked. The calendar is updated each year, so check the [Hida Takayama event calendar](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/index.html) before you go.

Takayama is a good winter base. The limited express trains from Nagoya and the buses to Shirakawa-go and Okuhida all start there. JR Central gives the trip as about 2 hours 20 minutes from Nagoya Station on the Limited Express Hida (as of October 2026, [JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/)). For step-by-step routes, see [Takayama from Nagoya](takayama-from-nagoya.html). Warm food matters on cold days, and our [Hida beef in Takayama](hida-beef-takayama.html) guide covers where to start.

## Okuhida Onsen and the Shinhotaka Ropeway in winter

Full details on tickets, buses from Takayama and temperatures are in our [Shinhotaka Ropeway guide](shinhotaka-ropeway.html).

Okuhida Onsengo is a group of five hot spring areas in the mountains east of Takayama: **Hirayu, Fukuchi, Shin-Hirayu, Tochio and Shinhotaka** ([Okuhida Onsengo Tourism Association](https://okuhida.or.jp/hot-springs)). The tourism association describes winter as the season to warm up in a snow-viewing bath ([Okuhida Onsengo](https://okuhida.or.jp/experience)). It also notes that the **Shinhotaka no Yu** day-use bath is closed in winter (as of October 2026, [Okuhida Onsengo](https://okuhida.or.jp/hot-springs)).

Winter night events in the area listed on Hida Takayama's calendar include:

- **Aodaru Illumination (Fukuchi Onsen):** Blue ice columns are lit from late December to late March, 5 p.m. to 10 p.m. Admission is free. In early to mid February, an "Ice Path Light-up" lets visitors walk behind the ice. The listing gives access as about 1 hour 10 minutes by bus from JR Takayama Station to the Fukuchi Onsen stop (2025–26 listing, as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000180.html)).
- **Frozen Hirayu Grand Waterfall Festival:** This February event has illumination and fireworks, with a free shuttle from Hirayu Bus Terminal. The official calendar listed past dates only ([Hida Takayama, February](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000184.html)). Dates are announced each year.

**Shinhotaka Ropeway** runs year-round. Departures run at :00 and :30 past the hour on No. 1 Ropeway and at :15 and :45 on No. 2 Ropeway. The adult round-trip fare between Shinhotaka Onsen and Nishi-Hotaka-guchi is **3,800 yen** (as of October 2026, [Shinhotaka Ropeway](https://shinhotaka-ropeway.jp/en/price/)). Things to know for winter, all from the ropeway's [FAQ](https://shinhotaka-ropeway.jp/en/faq/) (as of October 2026):

- The area gets heavy snow, usually from mid-November to early April. Winter equipment such as snow tires is required.
- Average December–March temperatures at Nishi-Hotaka-guchi Station are below freezing. Average highs range from about −3°C to −7°C.
- The ropeway may stop in strong winds. It also closes for inspection once or twice a year, and the FAQ gives no dates.
- Nabedaira Kogen car park is closed in winter.

No 2026–27 maintenance dates had been posted on the [ropeway's news page](https://shinhotaka-ropeway.jp/en/news-event/) when this guide was checked. Check the operating status on the [official site](https://shinhotaka-ropeway.jp/en/) on the morning you go.

## Gero Onsen winter fireworks (Hanabi Monogatari)

Gero Onsen is on the JR Takayama Line between Nagoya and Takayama, so it fits easily into a winter route. Its winter fireworks, **Fuyu no Gero Onsen Hanabi Monogatari**, ran **every Saturday from January 10 to March 28, 2026**. The 12 shows started at **8:30 p.m.** and lasted about 10 minutes. They were launched from the Hida River bank downstream of Gero Ohashi bridge. Admission was **free**, and shows could be canceled in bad weather or high water (2026 edition, as of October 2026, [Gifu Prefecture Tourism Federation](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3279.html)).

The 2027 schedule had not been published when this guide was checked; the Tourism Association describes the usual pattern as every Saturday from mid-January to late March. In December, Gero usually holds a separate winter fireworks musical (Hanabi Musical) every Saturday plus a special show on December 24, with regular shows of about 15 minutes ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/event/), as of October 2026). Dates are announced each year, so watch the [Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/event/) and [Gifu's tourism site](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/). For baths, ryokan and the walk through town, see our [Gero Onsen guide](gero-onsen-guide.html).

## Getting around in snow: bus or drive?

**Driving.** Hida Takayama's official site asks visitors not to drive rental cars with normal tires to Takayama in winter, and to take care on snow-covered roads ([Hida Takayama](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4001651.html)). The Shinhotaka Ropeway also says winter equipment such as snow tires is required ([FAQ](https://shinhotaka-ropeway.jp/en/faq/)). If you drive, confirm snow tires when you book the rental. Check closures on Gifu Prefecture's [road information site](https://douro.pref.gifu.lg.jp/), which the Okuhida association links to. The Okuhida association also posted a notice about partial restrictions and a detour on Route 158 between Takayama and Hirayu Onsen (July 2026, [Okuhida Onsengo](https://okuhida.or.jp/)).

**Buses and trains.** Public transport lets you skip snow driving, but expect delays. Nohi Bus warns that Takayama–Shirakawa-go buses can run **two hours or more late** because of parking-lot queues near Shirakawa-go, heavy snow or expressway closures. Some services do not run from December 29 to January 3. Some services need a reservation, and they can be booked through Japan Bus Online or by phone on (0577) 32-1688 (as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). The one-way fare from Takayama to Shirakawa-go is listed as 2,800 yen. However, Nohi Bus has announced a fare revision on this route from **December 1, 2026**, so check the current fare ([Nohi Bus news](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/news/)). Check live bus status on [Nohi Bus service information](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/bus-service-information/), and check JR Central's train service information on the day of travel.

Tips for a smooth trip:

- Build spare time into any day that includes Shirakawa-go or Okuhida, and avoid tight connections to the last train.
- Keep a data connection for checking live status. **Booking option:** [Japan eSIM plans](https://yesim.tpk.lu/T6xnCd9w) (Yesim). Check data allowance, validity and phone compatibility before buying.

## Can you ski in Gifu?

Yes. The Gujo area has 10 ski resorts about 1.5 hours from Nagoya by car, and the Hida area around Takayama has more ([Gujo Tourism Federation](https://en.tabitabigujo.com/appeal/ski/?c=th)). See [Skiing near Nagoya](skiing-near-nagoya.html) for the resorts and day packages from Nagoya.

## What to pack

Plan for below-freezing temperatures and snow underfoot. Useful items include:

- warm layers
- a waterproof outer layer
- gloves and a hat
- footwear with good grip

Higher spots such as the ropeway's upper station are colder than the valleys. Bring a small towel for onsen visits, and leave room in your schedule for weather changes.

## FAQ

### When is the Shirakawa-go light-up in 2027?

The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association lists the 41st Light-Up on January 11, January 17, January 24 and January 31, 2027 (as of October 2026, [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/)). The first round of regular car parking reservations is listed as sold out.

### Do you need a reservation for the Shirakawa-go light-up?

For the 2026 edition, Gifu Prefecture's tourism site described the light-up as held under a fully advance-reservation system, with no same-day visits (as of October 2026, [Gifu Prefecture Tourism Federation](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1287.html)). Confirm the 2027 rules on the [association's website](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/).

### Is the Shinhotaka Ropeway open in winter?

Yes, the Shinhotaka Ropeway runs year-round, and the adult round-trip fare between Shinhotaka Onsen and Nishi-Hotaka-guchi is 3,800 yen (as of October 2026, [Shinhotaka Ropeway](https://shinhotaka-ropeway.jp/en/price/)). It may stop in strong winds, so check the operating status on the morning you go.

### Can you drive to Takayama in winter?

Hida Takayama's official site asks visitors not to drive rental cars with normal tires to Takayama in winter ([Hida Takayama](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4001651.html)). If you drive, confirm snow tires when you book the rental.

### Do buses from Takayama to Shirakawa-go run on time in winter?

Nohi Bus warns that Takayama–Shirakawa-go buses can run two hours or more late because of parking-lot queues, heavy snow or expressway closures, and some services do not run from December 29 to January 3 (as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Build spare time into any day that includes Shirakawa-go.
