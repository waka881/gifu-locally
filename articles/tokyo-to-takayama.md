There is no direct train from Tokyo to Takayama: you change at Nagoya or Toyama, or take the highway bus from Shinjuku. This guide compares the three using Hida Takayama Tourism, Nohi Bus and JR West.

## Tokyo to Takayama at a glance

| Route | Time | Adult fare | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tokaido Shinkansen (Nozomi) to Nagoya + Limited Express Hida | About 100 min + 140 min, plus the change | Check JR; Japan Rail Pass holders ride Hikari or Kodama instead of Nozomi | Most travellers; Japan Rail Pass holders |
| Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama + Limited Express Hida | About 2 h 8 min + 90 min, plus the change | Check JR | Loops via Kanazawa, or returning a different way |
| Keio/Nohi highway bus, Shinjuku → Takayama | About 5 h 30 min | ¥8,000 (daytime) | Lowest fare; no change of vehicle |

## How do you get from Tokyo to Takayama by train?

**Via Nagoya (the standard route).** Take the Tokaido Shinkansen Nozomi from Tokyo to Nagoya, about 100 minutes, then the Limited Express Hida to Takayama, about 140 minutes ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)). Our [Takayama from Nagoya guide](takayama-from-nagoya.html) covers the Hida in detail.

**Via Toyama.** Take the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama (JR West lists 2 hours 8 minutes between Toyama and Tokyo), then the Limited Express Hida from Toyama to Takayama, about 90 minutes ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/shinkansen/hokuriku-shinkansen/); [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)). This works well for a loop: Tokyo → Toyama → Takayama → Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa, or out via Nagoya and back via Toyama.

**With a Japan Rail Pass:** the pass is not valid on Nozomi or Mizuho trains, so on the Tokaido Shinkansen you ride Hikari or Kodama, or buy the separate Nozomi ticket for pass holders ([Japan Rail Pass](https://japanrailpass.net/en/about_jrp/route/)). Whether a pass pays off depends on the rest of your week; see [Japan Rail Pass or Takayama-Hokuriku Pass?](jr-pass-vs-takayama-hokuriku-pass.html).

## What about the highway bus from Shinjuku?

Keio and Nohi Bus run the Takayama–Tokyo (Shinjuku) line, with all seats reserved:

| | Details |
|---|---|
| From | Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal (some services also from Bus Terminal Tokyo Yaesu) |
| To | Takayama Bus Terminal (next to JR Takayama Station), via Hirayu Onsen |
| Time | About 5 h 30 min |
| Adult fare | ¥8,000 daytime; ¥9,000 overnight (seasonal) |
| Shinjuku departures | 7:05, 8:15, 9:15, 11:05, 16:05; overnight 22:55 on set dates |
| Takayama departures | 6:30, 8:30, 10:30, 13:30, 15:00; 17:10 on set dates |
| Booking | Reservation required; opens one month before; online at highwaybus.com |
| On board | Toilet on all buses |

*Source: [Nohi Bus, Shinjuku line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/shinjuku_en/), as of October 2026. Seasonal services ran on set dates in spring and July–October 2026; check the page for your date.*

Nohi Bus lists a ¥500 web discount when booking through a site with Japanese-language support, and unpaid online reservations are cancelled automatically, so pay promptly after booking (same source). Keep mobile batteries with you: they may not go in the luggage bay.

The bus also stops at **Hirayu Onsen** in Okuhida before Takayama, so you can start a trip at the hot springs; see [Gero vs Okuhida](gero-vs-okuhida-onsen.html) and the [Shinhotaka Ropeway guide](shinhotaka-ropeway.html).

## Train or bus: which should you choose?

- **Take the train via Nagoya** if you have a Japan Rail Pass, want the shorter, more flexible trip, or are continuing to Kyoto or Osaka afterwards.
- **Take the bus** if price matters most: ¥8,000 with no change of vehicle, and you arrive next to the station. Book as soon as reservations open for busy dates.
- **Take the Toyama route** if you plan to go on to Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa: see the [Nagoya–Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa route](nagoya-takayama-shirakawa-go-kanazawa.html), which works in either direction.

Whichever way you go, the journey takes most of half a day, so plan at least one night in Takayama. See [How many days in Takayama?](how-many-days-in-takayama.html) and [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html).

## FAQ

### How long does it take from Tokyo to Takayama?

By train via Nagoya, about 100 minutes on the Nozomi plus about 140 minutes on the Limited Express Hida, plus the connection; by highway bus from Shinjuku, about 5 hours 30 minutes ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/shinjuku_en/)).

### How much is the bus from Tokyo to Takayama?

¥8,000 for adults on daytime buses and ¥9,000 on the seasonal overnight bus, from the Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal; seats must be reserved ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/shinjuku_en/), as of October 2026).

### Is there a direct train from Tokyo to Takayama?

No. Change at Nagoya to the Limited Express Hida, or take the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama and change there ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)).

### Does the Japan Rail Pass cover Tokyo to Takayama?

The pass covers JR trains with exceptions, including Nozomi and Mizuho on the Tokaido Shinkansen, so pass holders ride Hikari or Kodama to Nagoya or buy the separate Nozomi ticket ([Japan Rail Pass](https://japanrailpass.net/en/about_jrp/route/)).
