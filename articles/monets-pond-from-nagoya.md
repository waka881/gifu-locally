Monet's Pond is a small spring-fed pond in the mountains of Itadori, in Seki City, Gifu. Its clear water, carp and water lilies look like Monet's paintings, which is how it got its nickname; officially it is the "nameless pond" (Namonaki Ike). This guide uses Seki City's tourism site and the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO).

## How do you get to Monet's Pond from Nagoya by bus?

1. **Nagoya → Gifu:** JR train to JR Gifu Station, about 20 minutes ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya/)).
2. **Gifu Station → Horado Kiwi Plaza:** Gifu Bus Gifu–Itadori line. JNTO says to board at stand 2 (route N83), with about 10 buses a day; the ride is about 70 minutes ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/154/); [GLTJP](https://gltjp.com/en/directory/item/11989)).
3. **Horado Kiwi Plaza → Monet's Pond:** change to the Itadori Fureai Bus, which JNTO describes as free, and get off at "Monet-no-Ike-mae" after about 15 minutes. The pond is about 150 m from the stop ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/154/); [Seki City tourism](https://www.sekikanko.jp/spot_post/162)).

JNTO puts the trip from Gifu Station at about 90 minutes. The official pages don't list the Gifu Bus fare or the Fureai Bus timetable, so check both connections before you set off, especially the last bus back; with only about 10 buses a day, missing a connection can cost a lot of time.

## When is the best time to visit Monet's Pond?

- **Mid-June to early August:** water lilies in full bloom; the exact timing varies by year ([GLTJP](https://gltjp.com/en/directory/item/11989)). JNTO calls early summer the best time to visit ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/154/)).
- **Late June to early July:** hydrangeas along the Itadori area's Hydrangea Road (same GLTJP source).
- **November:** the trees around the pond turn red (same source).
- **Time of day:** Seki City says the light is best in the morning, from about 9:00 to 11:00 ([Seki City tourism](https://www.sekikanko.jp/spot_post/162)).

JNTO notes that the pond is popular with photographers and painters, so you may need patience to get a spot on the small bridge and viewing platform ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/154/)).

## What should you know before you go?

- **It is not a tourist facility.** Seki City says it was originally an agricultural irrigation pond ([Seki City tourism](https://www.sekikanko.jp/spot_post/162)). Treat it with care and stay on the paths.
- **Cash.** GLTJP lists credit cards as not accepted (source above), so carry cash for buses and the area.
- **Base yourself in Gifu City** if you want the morning light without an early start from Nagoya: the bus leaves from JR Gifu Station. See [where to stay in Gifu City](where-to-stay-gifu-city.html) and [Gifu City in one day](gifu-city-one-day.html).

## FAQ

### How do I get to Monet's Pond from Nagoya without a car?

Take the train to JR Gifu Station (about 20 minutes), the Gifu Bus Itadori line to Horado Kiwi Plaza (about 70 minutes), then the Itadori Fureai Bus to "Monet-no-Ike-mae" (about 15 minutes) ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya/); [GLTJP](https://gltjp.com/en/directory/item/11989)).

### Is Monet's Pond free?

Yes. Entry is free and it is open every day ([Seki City tourism](https://www.sekikanko.jp/spot_post/162); [GLTJP](https://gltjp.com/en/directory/item/11989), as of October 2026).

### When do the water lilies bloom at Monet's Pond?

Mostly from mid-June to early August, with exact timing varying each year ([GLTJP](https://gltjp.com/en/directory/item/11989)).

### Is there parking at Monet's Pond?

Yes, Seki City lists parking for 200 cars and for large buses ([Seki City tourism](https://www.sekikanko.jp/spot_post/162)).
