## Why do you need mobile data in Takayama and Shirakawa-go?

- **Bus reservations.** Most Takayama–Shirakawa-go buses need a reserved seat, booked online through Japan Bus Online ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Being able to check or change a booking on the go helps, especially in winter, when Nohi Bus warns of delays of two hours or more.
- **Timetables and last buses.** The last bus from Shirakawa-go to Takayama leaves at 17:30, and the Kamikochi bus runs only in season (see [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) and [Kamikochi from Takayama](kamikochi-from-takayama.html)).
- **Online train tickets.** Ticketless Limited Express Hida tickets and advance discounts are booked through JR West's E5489 online service ([JR Central](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044393.pdf)).
- **Maps in the old towns and villages.** Sanmachi, Shirakawa-go and Gero are all explored on foot.

## How much data do you need?

Yesim's FAQ gives a rough guide of about 1 hour of 1080p video streaming per 1 GB ([Yesim Japan](https://yesim.app/country/japan/)). Maps, messaging and booking sites use far less than video. As a rule of thumb:

| Trip and use | Suggested plan |
|---|---|
| A few days, maps and messages only | 1–5 GB |
| One week, maps, bookings, photos to social media | 5–10 GB |
| Two weeks, or sharing a hotspot with a travel partner | 20–30 GB |

*Our suggestion based on Yesim's usage guide; your use may differ.*

If you share your data with a partner's phone through the hotspot, the shared data counts against your plan ([Yesim](https://yesim.app/)).

## How does a Japan eSIM work?

1. **Check your phone.** eSIM works only on compatible Apple and Android devices. Yesim suggests dialling *#06# to check for eSIM support, or checking its compatible device list ([Yesim Japan](https://yesim.app/country/japan/)). Your phone also needs to be unlocked for other carriers.
2. **Buy before you fly.** You have 90 days from purchase to activate (same source).
3. **Install by QR code or in the app.** Each QR code works on one device only ([Yesim](https://yesim.app/)).
4. **Switch it on in Japan.** The validity period starts when the eSIM connects to a network, so the 30 days don't start until you arrive (same source).

Your usual number stays in your phone for messaging apps, and you avoid your home carrier's roaming charges by turning off data roaming on your home line. Check your home carrier's own rules before you travel.

## FAQ

### Do I need a SIM or eSIM for Takayama?

It isn't required, but it helps: most Takayama–Shirakawa-go buses need online reservations, and you'll want maps and timetables while walking around ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).

### How much does a Japan eSIM cost?

Yesim lists Japan plans from $4.44 for 1 GB up to $39.60 for 30 GB, each valid for 30 days, as of October 2026 ([Yesim Japan](https://yesim.app/country/japan/)). Prices change often.

### When does a Japan eSIM start counting days?

With Yesim, the validity period starts when the eSIM first connects to a network in Japan, and you must activate it within 90 days of buying (same source).

### Can I use an eSIM as a hotspot in Japan?

Yes. Yesim says hotspot sharing is available, and the shared data counts against your plan ([Yesim](https://yesim.app/)).
