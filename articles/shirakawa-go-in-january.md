January is when Shirakawa-go holds its winter light-up and gets the most snow of the year. This guide uses Nohi Bus, the Japan Meteorological Agency's averages for its Shirakawa station, the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association, Shirakawa Village and Klook.

## When is the Shirakawa-go light-up in January 2027?

On four evenings: Monday January 11 and Sundays January 17, 24 and 31, 2027, lit from 17:30 to 19:30 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)). For the 2026 event, the organisers did not let anyone in without a reservation, whatever the transport; our [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html) covers the ways in, including inn stays and parking.

## How do you see the light-up from Takayama?

On a Nohi Bus light-up tour. Nohi Bus says there are no route buses back to Takayama after the light-up, so the tour is the way to go by bus. All three courses leave from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center and include the return trip ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)):

| Course | Price (adult or child) | Leaves Takayama | Back in Takayama | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | ¥13,000 | 15:40 | 20:20 | Gassho-zukuri Minkaen ticket and zenzai (sweet red bean soup) |
| B | ¥16,000 | 15:10 | 20:45 | Ogimachi observation deck ticket and a souvenir |
| C | ¥14,000 | 14:40 | 20:20 | Hirase Onsen bath ticket and a souvenir |

*Source: [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/), as of November 2026.*

Bookings are online only, up to six people, paid by card. Sales for all four dates opened between October 13 and November 2, 2026, and there is no waiting list; cancelled seats go back on sale, so check again if your date is full. Only Course B includes the observation deck, which is about a 20-minute uphill walk each way, and no deck tickets are sold on the day (same source).

## How cold and snowy is Shirakawa-go in January?

Very snowy. The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages for Shirakawa give 340 cm of new snow in January, more than any other month, with the snow cover averaging 148 cm at its deepest (February is deeper, at 173 cm). January averages −1.0°C, with highs of 2.8°C, lows of −4.4°C and only 41.2 hours of sunshine ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=1306&year=&month=&day=&view=)). Bring waterproof boots with grip, a warm coat, gloves and a hat. The full year is in [Shirakawa-go weather by month](shirakawa-go-weather-by-month.html).

## What about January days without the light-up?

The village is open by day, but on other nights day visitors are not admitted after dark, to protect residents' lives and privacy ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/106/)). The route bus from Takayama takes about 50 minutes and costs ¥2,900 one way from December 1, 2026, and Nohi Bus warns of delays of two hours or more from heavy snow, expressway closures or traffic waiting to park ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). If you drive, Shirakawa Village says studless tyres are required and recommends chains and 4WD ([Shirakawa Village FAQ](https://www.vill.shirakawa.lg.jp/1466.htm)). See [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html), and for the next month, [Shirakawa-go in February](shirakawa-go-in-february.html).

## FAQ

### When is the Shirakawa-go light-up in 2027?

January 11, 17, 24 and 31, 2027, from 17:30 to 19:30 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)).

### How much is the Nohi Bus light-up tour from Takayama?

¥13,000 for Course A, ¥14,000 for Course C and ¥16,000 for Course B with the observation deck, the same for adults and children (same source).

### How much snow is there in Shirakawa-go in January?

The averages give 340 cm of snowfall in January, the most of any month, with the snow about 148 cm deep ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=1306&year=&month=&day=&view=)).

### Can I stay in Shirakawa-go after dark in January?

Only on light-up evenings with a reservation, or as an overnight guest; day visitors are not admitted after dark on other nights ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/106/)).
