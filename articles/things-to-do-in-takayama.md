Takayama is a mountain town in northern Gifu with a preserved merchant quarter, daily morning markets and easy day trips into the Japanese Alps. This page lists the main things to do with the key facts from official sources, and links to our full guide for each. ## What is there to do in Takayama's old town?

**Walk Sanmachi.** The old merchant quarter is about 12 minutes on foot from JR Takayama Station, and it is where most of the sights below are ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)). See [How many days in Takayama?](how-many-days-in-takayama.html) for how to pace it.

**Go to a morning market.** Miyagawa runs along the river and Jinya-mae is in front of Takayama Jinya; both open every morning and close at noon. See our [Takayama morning market guide](takayama-morning-market.html).

**Tour Takayama Jinya.** The former government office is ¥500, free for those 18 and under, and closed from December 29 to January 3 ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)). See our [Takayama Jinya guide](takayama-jinya.html) for the free English tour, and if you are visiting over the holidays, [Takayama at New Year](takayama-new-year.html).

**Taste sake.** JNTO says Takayama has seven sake breweries, all over 100 years old, marked by cedar balls over the door ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/153/)). See [Takayama sake breweries](takayama-sake-breweries.html).

[[cta|https://www.klook.com/activity/35490-sake-brewery-tour-takayama/|English-guided sake tasting|30 minutes, at least five sakes at Hirase Sake Brewery. Check dates on Klook.|See tasting]]

**Eat Hida beef.** In Takayama it comes as street food, such as nigiri, skewers and croquettes, or as sit-down meals; see [Hida beef in Takayama](hida-beef-takayama.html).

**See the festival floats.** The Takayama Festival runs on April 14–15 and October 9–10; the rest of the year, see floats at the Exhibition Hall. See our [Takayama Festival guide](takayama-festival.html).

## What can you do just outside the old town?

**Hida Folk Village.** An open-air museum of more than 30 traditional buildings, including thatched farmhouses, about 10 minutes by Sarubobo Bus from the Nohi Bus Center ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm)). See [Hida Folk Village](hida-folk-village.html).

## FAQ

### What is Takayama best known for?

Its preserved Sanmachi merchant quarter, daily morning markets, sake breweries, Hida beef and the Takayama Festival on April 14–15 and October 9–10 (sources above).

### Is Takayama Jinya worth visiting?

It is the town's main historic building and costs ¥500 (free for 18 and under); note it closes from December 29 to January 3 ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)).

### What can I do in Takayama in one day?

Walk Sanmachi, visit Takayama Jinya and either Hida Folk Village or a sake brewery; the morning markets close at noon, so a day trip from Nagoya usually misses them. See [How many days in Takayama?](how-many-days-in-takayama.html) and our [one day in Takayama](one-day-in-takayama.html) plan

### What is the best day trip from Takayama?

Shirakawa-go is the most popular, about 50 minutes by express bus for ¥2,800 one way ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).
