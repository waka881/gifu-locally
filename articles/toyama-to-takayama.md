Toyama is the Hokuriku Shinkansen station closest to Takayama, so it is the natural way in from Tokyo or Kanazawa. You can take the train straight there, or the bus with a stop at Shirakawa-go. This guide uses Hida Takayama Tourism, Nohi Bus, JR West and the pass's official site.

## How long is the train from Toyama to Takayama?

Hida Takayama Tourism gives the JR Limited Express Hida from Toyama to Takayama as 90 minutes one way ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)). It is the fastest option and arrives at JR Takayama Station, next to the bus center. Over the New Year, the pass site says all Hida seats are reserved-only from December 25, 2026 to January 5, 2027 ([Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/)), so get a seat reservation before you board.

Coming from Tokyo: JR West lists Tokyo to Toyama at 2 hours 8 minutes on the Hokuriku Shinkansen ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/shinkansen/hokuriku-shinkansen/)). See [Tokyo to Takayama](tokyo-to-takayama.html) to compare this with the Nagoya route and the Shinjuku bus.

## What about the bus from Toyama?

Nohi Bus and Toyama Chiho Railway run buses between Toyama Station and Takayama via Shirakawa-go:

| Toyama Station | Shirakawa-go | Takayama Bus Terminal |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | about 10:20 | 11:20 |
| 10:10 | about 11:30 | 12:30 |
| 12:00 | about 13:20 | 14:20 |

| Takayama Bus Terminal | Shirakawa-go | Toyama Station |
|---|---|---|
| 8:10 | 9:10 | about 10:30 |
| 13:10 | 14:10 | about 15:30 |
| 14:30 | 15:30 | about 16:50 |

*Source: [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026. "About" times are worked out from the listed Toyama–Shirakawa-go time of about 1 h 20 min. Nohi Bus says fares and timetables will be revised from December 1, 2026, so check the page for your date.*

Fares are ¥3,800 Toyama–Takayama and ¥2,400 Toyama–Shirakawa-go (adult, one way). Some departures need a reservation, through Japan Bus Online or the Toyama Chiho Railway highway bus reservation center, +81-76-433-4890 (8:30–19:00). Nohi Bus warns that delays of two hours or more can happen because of parking queues at Shirakawa-go, snow or expressway closures (same source).

**Stopping at Shirakawa-go:** you can get off, spend 2–3 hours in the village and take a later bus to Takayama, but plan both legs before you go; see [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) for the Takayama-side timetable and luggage tips. Want Gokayama too? It is reached from the Toyama side; see [Gokayama vs Shirakawa-go](gokayama-vs-shirakawa-go.html).

## Is the Takayama-Hokuriku pass worth it from Toyama?

The Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass costs ¥19,800 for adults for 5 consecutive days and covers JR between Nagoya and Toyama via Takayama (including the Hida), the Hokuriku Shinkansen between Toyama and Tsuruga, and the Nohi Bus via Shirakawa-go to Kanazawa or Toyama (reservations required), with up to 6 free reserved seats ([Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/)). It pays off on a loop such as Kanazawa → Toyama → Takayama → Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa, or onward to Nagoya and Kyoto; see our [Takayama-Hokuriku pass guide](takayama-hokuriku-pass.html) and the [Nagoya–Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa route](nagoya-takayama-shirakawa-go-kanazawa.html).

## FAQ

### How long does it take from Toyama to Takayama?

About 90 minutes by JR Limited Express Hida, or about 2 hours 20 minutes by bus via Shirakawa-go ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).

### How much is the bus from Toyama to Takayama?

¥3,800 for adults one way, or ¥2,400 to Shirakawa-go ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026; revision planned from December 1, 2026).

### Can I go from Toyama to Shirakawa-go by bus?

Yes. Buses leave Toyama Station at 9:00, 10:10 and 12:00 and take about 1 hour 20 minutes to Shirakawa-go (same source).

### Does the Takayama-Hokuriku pass cover Toyama to Takayama?

Yes. It covers the Limited Express Hida between Toyama and Takayama, and the Nohi Bus via Shirakawa-go with reservations ([Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/)).
