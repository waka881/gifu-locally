## How cold is Takayama in February?

Very. The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages give −0.6°C for February, with daily highs of 4.7°C and the lowest daily lows of the year, −4.9°C, and 90 cm of snowfall over the month ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)). See [Takayama weather by month](takayama-weather-by-month.html) for the whole year. Pack a warm coat, gloves, a hat and shoes with grip.

## What is on in Takayama in February 2027?

- **Hida Folk Village winter illumination:** the thatched farmhouses are lit every evening until February 28, 2027, 17:30–20:30 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3323.html)). Tickets and buses are in our [Hida Folk Village illumination guide](hida-folk-village-winter-illumination.html).
- **Setsubun:** Hida Takayama Tourism lists a bean-throwing parade in early February each year that starts at a temple gate and passes Takayama Station, Honmachi Street and Kokubunji Temple ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000184.html)).

The Shirakawa-go light-up is held only in January in 2027; see [Takayama in January](takayama-in-january.html).

## What ice and snow events are there in Okuhida?

Hida Takayama Tourism's February calendar lists several in the Okuhida hot spring area. The dates below are from the last season it showed, so check before you go ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000184.html), as of November 2026):

- **Aodaru Illumination, Fukuji Onsen:** lit ice columns, free, 17:00–22:00, December 23, 2024 to March 20, 2025; about 1 hour 10 minutes by bus from JR Takayama Station to the Fukuji Onsen stop.
- **Frozen Hirayu Great Falls Festival:** held every year, February 15–25 in 2024, with the illumination from 19:00 to 21:00 and fireworks from around 20:30, weather permitting; a free shuttle runs from the Hirayu Bus Terminal.
- **Nakao Kamakura Festival:** snow huts at Nakao Kogen, free, from 20:00, February 1–10 in 2024; about 1 hour 30 minutes by bus from JR Takayama Station to Nakao Kogen (Ashiyu-mae), then about 10 minutes on foot.

These evening events end late, so a night in Okuhida is easier than a return to Takayama; see [where to stay in Okuhida](where-to-stay-okuhida.html) and the [Okuhida bus pass](okuhida-bus-pass.html).

## What is open in Takayama in February?

The town runs on winter hours. Takayama Jinya is open 8:45–16:30 from November to March ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)), and the morning markets open at 8:00 from December to March ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)). ## FAQ

### Is February a good time to visit Takayama?

If you want snow, illuminations and ice events, yes: the Hida Folk Village illumination runs nightly until February 28, 2027, and Okuhida holds its ice events. It is also very cold, with daily lows of −4.9°C (sources above).

### How much snow does Takayama get in February?

The Japan Meteorological Agency's averages give 90 cm of snowfall in February, less than January's 117 cm ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)).

### Is the Shirakawa-go light-up in February?

Not in 2027; it is held on four evenings in January. The Hida Folk Village illumination in Takayama runs through February ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3323.html)).

### When is the frozen Hirayu Great Falls festival?

It is held every year in mid-February; it ran February 15–25 in 2024, the latest dates on Hida Takayama Tourism's calendar ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000184.html)).
