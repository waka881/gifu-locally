## Is there snow in Shirakawa-go in December?

Yes, and a lot of it. The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages for Shirakawa give 201 cm of new snow in December, with the snow cover reaching 84 cm on average, against 12 cm of snowfall and 5 cm of cover in November. December averages 1.7°C, with highs of 5.9°C and lows of −1.7°C, and it is the wettest month of the year (282.8 mm, mostly as snow), with only 47.0 hours of sunshine ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=1306&year=&month=&day=&view=)). Snow lies deeper still in January (148 cm) and February (173 cm); the full year is in [Shirakawa-go weather by month](shirakawa-go-weather-by-month.html).

## Is there a light-up in December?

No. The 2027 light-up evenings are January 11, 17, 24 and 31 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)); see [Shirakawa-go in January](shirakawa-go-in-january.html) and the [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html). On other nights, day visitors are not admitted to the village after dark, to protect residents' lives and privacy ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/106/)), so plan to leave before dusk unless you are staying overnight.

## How do you get to Shirakawa-go in December?

By bus or tour. From December 1, 2026 the Nohi Bus express from Takayama costs ¥2,900 one way for adults and takes about 50 minutes. Nohi Bus warns that winter delays of two hours or more can happen from heavy snow, expressway closures or traffic waiting to park, and some services do not run from December 29 to January 3 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). If you drive, Shirakawa Village says studless tyres are required and recommends chains and a 4WD vehicle ([Shirakawa Village FAQ](https://www.vill.shirakawa.lg.jp/1466.htm)).

## FAQ

### Is there snow in Shirakawa-go in December?

Yes. The averages give 201 cm of snowfall in December, with snow about 84 cm deep ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=1306&year=&month=&day=&view=)).

### Is the Shirakawa-go light-up in December?

No. In 2027 it is on January 11, 17, 24 and 31 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)).

### Do buses to Shirakawa-go run over New Year?

Mostly, but Nohi Bus says some services on the Takayama–Shirakawa-go line do not run from December 29 to January 3 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). See [Takayama at New Year](takayama-new-year.html).

### How much is the bus from Takayama to Shirakawa-go?

¥2,900 one way for adults from December 1, 2026, taking about 50 minutes (same source).
