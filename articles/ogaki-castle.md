Ogaki Castle stands in the middle of Ogaki, a short walk from the station, and is a quick stop for anyone interested in the Battle of Sekigahara. This guide uses the Ogaki tourism site and the Gifu Prefecture tourism site, which differ on closed days, so both are shown.

## What is Ogaki Castle's history?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site says that in the Battle of Sekigahara in 1600, Ogaki Castle was the base of Ishida Mitsunari's Western Army, and fierce fighting went on at the castle even after the main force moved to Sekigahara. From 1635 the Toda family ruled from the castle for 11 generations. The castle was lost to fire in war and rebuilt in 1959, and its four-storey keep is described as rare in Japan ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_894.html)).

## How much is it and when is it open?

Adult admission is ¥200, a combined ticket with the Ogaki City Local History Museum next door; high school students and younger are free, and groups of 20 or more get a discount. A four-venue combined ticket costs ¥600. It is open 9:00–17:00, with last entry at 16:30 ([Ogaki tourism](http://www.ogakikanko.jp/spot/ogakijyo/), as of November 2026). Some hands-on displays, including the experience corner and audio-visual guide corner, are suspended for now (same source).

## Is it open on Tuesdays?

The two official listings differ. Ogaki's tourism site lists closures on Tuesdays, the day after public holidays and over New Year ([Ogaki tourism](http://www.ogakikanko.jp/spot/ogakijyo/)); the Gifu Prefecture tourism site lists only the New Year break ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_894.html)), as of November 2026. To be safe, go on another day.

## How do you get there, and what else is nearby?

Walk about 7 minutes south from the south exit of JR Ogaki Station ([Ogaki tourism](http://www.ogakikanko.jp/spot/ogakijyo/)). Ogaki is also where you change to the Yoro Railway for the [Site of Reversible Destiny](yoro-park-site-of-reversible-destiny.html) in Yoro Park ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_902.html)). For Gifu's other castle on Mt. Kinka, closed for renovation until 2027, see [Is Gifu Castle open?](gifu-castle.html). More ideas are in [things to do in Gifu](things-to-do-in-gifu.html).
