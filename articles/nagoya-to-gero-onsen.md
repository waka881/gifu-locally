Gero Onsen is the easiest hot spring town to reach from Nagoya: one direct train on the JR Takayama Line, and the baths start a few minutes' walk from the station. This guide covers the train, what the ticket costs, how to save with advance discounts, and a day plan, using JR Central's fare release and the Gero Onsen Tourism Association.

## Nagoya to Gero Onsen at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Train | JR Limited Express Hida, Nagoya → Gero, direct |
| Time | About 1 h 30–40 min |
| Limited express charge (adult, reserved) | ¥2,390; Green Car ¥4,660 |
| Hayatoku 3 advance discount | ¥2,060 (book 3+ days ahead) |
| Base fare | Extra; can be paid with a TOICA IC card |
| Booking | Online through JR West's E5489, from one month ahead |
| From the station | Public baths 4–10 min on foot |

*Sources: [JR Central press release](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044393.pdf) (charges and booking, as of July 2025); [Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya) and the [Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/course/detail_4.html), checked October 2026.*

## How long is the train from Nagoya to Gero?

The Limited Express Hida runs direct from Nagoya Station to Gero Station. Visit Gifu, the prefecture's tourism site, gives the trip as 90 minutes in one place and about 1 hour 40 minutes in another ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya), as of October 2026). The Gero Onsen Tourism Association's JR model course uses a train leaving Nagoya at 12:48 and arriving in Gero at 14:22, about 1 hour 35 minutes, and it notes that every train on the line stops at Gero, including the Limited Express Hida ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/course/detail_4.html), checked October 2026). Times vary by train, so check the timetable on [JR Central's train information pages](https://traininfo.jr-central.co.jp/zairaisen/index.html?lang=en) for your date.

## How much is the ticket?

You pay two parts: a limited express charge for the reserved seat, and the base fare.

| Ticket (one way, Nagoya–Gero) | Adult | Child | How to buy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ticketless limited express ticket, reserved seat | ¥2,390 | ¥1,190 | E5489, from one month ahead until 2 min before departure |
| Ticketless limited express ticket, Green Car | ¥4,660 | ¥3,730 | E5489 |
| Tokkyu Ticketless Hayatoku 3 | ¥2,060 | ¥1,030 | E5489, until 3 days before; limited seats |
| Tokkyu Ticketless Hayatoku 7 | ¥1,510 | ¥760 | Only with a Shinkansen booking to or from Nagoya through EX; until 7 days before |

*Source: [JR Central press release](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044393.pdf), July 2025. The release listed the Hayatoku prices as provisional, so check the current price when you book.*

Key points from the same release:

- **Booking:** ticketless tickets are sold through E5489, JR West's online booking service, from 10:00 one month before travel. The ticketless reserved-seat price is the same as at the station counter.
- **Hayatoku discounts** are not sold for April 27–May 6, August 10–19 or December 28–January 6, and seats per train are limited.
- **Base fare:** ticketless tickets cover only the limited express charge. Since October 1, 2025, you can use a TOICA IC card at Gero Station for the base fare when riding the Hida.

If you have a Japan Rail Pass or a regional pass, check whether it covers the Hida before buying separate tickets. Klook sells the [JR Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass](https://www.klook.com/activity/2969-5-day-jr-takayama-hokuriku-area-tourist-pass-jr-pass/), which is worth comparing if you are continuing to Takayama.

## Is there a direct bus from Nagoya to Gero?

Nohi Bus's highway bus from Nagoya goes to Takayama via Gujo-Hachiman, not via Gero ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/english/highwaybus/), as of October 2026). The train is the straightforward option.

## What can you do on a day trip?

The tourism association's model course (same source) arrives at 14:22, picks up leaflets at the tourist information center at 14:30, takes a bus from the station at 14:40 to Gero Onsen Gassho Village at 14:45, and walks the hot spring town from 16:15. Using the association's spot pages (as of October 2026):

| Stop | From Gero Station | Adult price |
|---|---|---|
| Sachi-no-yu public bath | About 4 min on foot | ¥470 |
| Shirasagi-no-yu public bath | About 7 min on foot | ¥470 |
| Kur Garden Rotenburo (open-air bath by the river) | About 10 min on foot | ¥800 |
| Gero Onsen Gassho Village (open-air museum of thatched houses) | About 20 min on foot | ¥800 |
| Sagi no Ashiyu foot bath | About 8 min on foot | Free, 24 hours |

*Sources: Gero Onsen Tourism Association pages for [Sachi-no-yu](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_5.html), [Shirasagi-no-yu](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_3.html), [Kur Garden](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_4.html), [Gassho Village](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_12.html) and [Sagi no Ashiyu](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_6.html), as of October 2026. Each bath has its own closing day; check before you go.*

To try ryokan baths instead, the Yumeguri Tegata pass costs ¥2,500 for three day-use baths at member inns, valid for six months ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_50.html)). Our [Gero Onsen guide](gero-onsen-guide.html) has hours, closing days and bath etiquette.

## Should you stay the night?

A day trip works if you mainly want to try the water. Staying lets you bathe in the evening and the next morning, and makes it easy to continue to Takayama: JR Central gives the train from Takayama to Gero as about 45–60 minutes ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/itineraries/takayama-gero-craftsmanship-onsen/)), so the trip in the other direction is similar. ## FAQ

### How long does it take from Nagoya to Gero Onsen?

About 1 hour 30–40 minutes on the Limited Express Hida ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya)). The Gero Onsen Tourism Association's model course uses a train from Nagoya at 12:48 that reaches Gero at 14:22 ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/course/detail_4.html)). Check the timetable for your train.

### How much is the train from Nagoya to Gero?

The ticketless limited express charge for an adult reserved seat is ¥2,390, or ¥2,060 with the Hayatoku 3 advance discount, and the base fare is extra ([JR Central press release](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044393.pdf), July 2025).

### Can I use a TOICA card to Gero?

Yes, for the base fare. Since October 1, 2025, TOICA can be used at Gero Station when riding the Limited Express Hida, but you still need a separate limited express ticket (same source).

### Is Gero Onsen worth a day trip from Nagoya?

Yes, if you want an easy hot spring visit: the train is direct, and public baths such as Sachi-no-yu and Shirasagi-no-yu (¥470 for adults) are 4–7 minutes on foot from the station ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/), as of October 2026).
