Takayama is the closest major base for Shirakawa-go, and the bus makes it an easy half-day or day trip. This guide pulls the departures, fares and booking rules from the Nohi Bus timetable and adds a simple plan for the day.

## Takayama to Shirakawa-go at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| From | Takayama Nohi Bus Center, next to JR Takayama Station |
| Time | About 50 minutes (some buses about 1 hour 7 minutes) |
| Adult fare | ¥2,800 one way |
| First / last bus out | 7:20 / 17:50 |
| Last bus back | 17:30 from Shirakawa-go |
| Reservations | Needed on buses marked "R"; book through Japan Bus Online |
| Time in the village | About 2–3 hours to walk around |

*Sources: [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/); [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026.*

## How do you buy tickets?

- **Fare:** ¥2,800 one way for adults between Takayama and Shirakawa-go. No round-trip fare is listed. Children under 6 ride free when they don't take a seat (one per adult) ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).
- **Reserved buses ("R"):** book online through [Japan Bus Online](https://japanbusonline.com/), or by phone at the Nohi Bus Reservation Center, 9:00–17:00 (same source).
- **Non-reserved buses:** mobile tickets are sold for these only (same source). Seats are first come, first served.

**Our advice:** reserve both directions. The non-reserved buses are useful as a backup if you miss your booked bus, but you can't count on a seat on a busy day.

If you have the Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass, these buses are included, but you still need to reserve; see our [Takayama-Hokuriku pass guide](takayama-hokuriku-pass.html).

## A simple day trip plan

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 8:10 | Bus from Takayama (R) |
| 9:00 | Arrive Shirakawa-go. Walk to Wada House area |
| 10:00 | Shuttle up to the Shiroyama viewpoint (first run 10:00) |
| 11:00 | Wada House, Myozenji Temple Museum, village streets |
| 12:00 | Lunch |
| 13:30 | Bus back to Takayama (R), arriving 14:20 |
| Afternoon | Takayama's Sanmachi old town |

The viewpoint shuttle runs every 20 minutes from 10:00 to 14:40, with a break from 12:00 to 13:00, and costs ¥200 one way, cash only. Wada House charges ¥400 (cash only) and opens 9:00–17:00 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/); [Wada House](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/active/13/), as of October 2026). For more about each sight, see [Is Shirakawa-go worth visiting?](is-shirakawa-go-worth-visiting.html)

## Can you go on to Kanazawa instead of coming back?

Yes. The same line continues from Shirakawa-go to Kanazawa for ¥2,800, about 1 hour 20 minutes ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/); [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)). Coin lockers at the Shirakawago Bus Terminal cost ¥500 or ¥1,000 per use, 8:00–17:00 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)). See the [Japan Alps route guide](nagoya-takayama-shirakawa-go-kanazawa.html).

## Bus or guided tour?

A guided tour from Takayama saves you from booking buses and can add Gokayama, a second thatched village. One example on Klook is a [Shirakawa-go and Gokayama tour from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/90970-historic-villages-shirakawa-go-gokayama-join-bus-trip-takayama/), which lists about 2 hours 40 minutes in Shirakawa-go and a Japanese-speaking host; check the dates and language before booking. The bus is cheaper and lets you set your own pace.

**Winter:** Nohi Bus warns of delays of two hours or more on this line in winter ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). See [Shirakawa-go in winter](shirakawa-go-in-winter.html).

## FAQ

### How long is the bus from Takayama to Shirakawa-go?

About 50 minutes on most buses; some non-reserved buses take about 1 hour 7 minutes ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026).

### How much is the bus from Takayama to Shirakawa-go?

¥2,800 one way for adults. No round-trip fare is listed (same source).

### What time is the last bus from Shirakawa-go to Takayama?

17:30, arriving in Takayama at 18:35 (same source, checked October 2026). Timetables change, so confirm for your date.

### Do I need to book the bus from Takayama to Shirakawa-go?

For buses marked "R", yes; book on Japan Bus Online. Some departures are non-reserved, but booking a reserved bus is the safer choice (same source).
