Two days is enough to see Takayama properly and add Shirakawa-go without rushing. This itinerary starts and ends in Nagoya, uses public transport, and spends one night in Takayama so you can catch the morning markets. All times and fees come from official sources.

## The plan at a glance

| | Morning | Afternoon | Night |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Nagoya to Takayama by train or bus | Sanmachi old town, Takayama Jinya, Hida Folk Village | Stay in Takayama |
| **Day 2** | Miyagawa and Jinya-mae morning markets | Bus to Shirakawa-go, 2–3 hours in the village | Back to Nagoya, or onward |

## Before you go: what to book

- **Your Takayama hotel.** Book early for festival dates: the Spring Takayama Festival is on April 14–15 and the Autumn Festival on October 9–10 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/4000105.html), as of October 2026). See [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html).
- **Your bus seats.** The Nohi Bus between Nagoya and Takayama needs a reservation on every bus, with booking opening one month ahead ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/), as of October 2026).
- **Mobile data.** Bus times and maps are easier with a [Japan eSIM](https://yesim.tpk.lu/T6xnCd9w) set up before you fly.

## Day 1: Nagoya to Takayama and the old town

### Morning: get to Takayama

- **Train:** the Limited Express Hida runs from JR Nagoya Station to JR Takayama Station in approximately 2 hours 20 minutes ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/), as of October 2026).
- **Bus:** the Nohi Bus highway bus takes 2 hours 40 minutes and costs ¥3,600 one way, or ¥3,300 with the web discount ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/), as of October 2026).

Leave early so you arrive by late morning. Drop your bags at your hotel near the station; the main sights are 10 to 12 minutes away on foot. Full details are in our [Nagoya to Takayama guide](takayama-from-nagoya.html).

### Lunch: Hida beef

Takayama is known for Hida beef, eaten as street food such as nigiri and skewers, or at sit-down meals. See [Hida beef in Takayama](hida-beef-takayama.html) for what the name means and how to order it.

### Afternoon: Sanmachi and Takayama Jinya

- **Sanmachi** is the old merchant district, with lattice-fronted wooden townhouses, sake breweries and small shops, a 12-minute walk from JR Takayama Station ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)).
- **Takayama Jinya**, the former Edo-period government office, is open 8:45–17:00 from April to October and 8:45–16:30 from November to March, with the ticket window closing 30 minutes earlier. Admission is ¥500 ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/), as of October 2026).

### Late afternoon: Hida Folk Village

Hida Folk Village is an open-air museum of old Hida farmhouses, including thatched gassho-style houses. It is open 8:30–17:00 and costs ¥700 for adults. The Sarubobo Bus from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center to Hida-no-Sato-Shita takes about 10 minutes and costs ¥210 one way ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html), as of October 2026).

If you are visiting Shirakawa-go on day 2, you can skip the Folk Village and spend the time in Sanmachi instead, since both show gassho-style houses.

### Night: stay in Takayama

Staying near JR Takayama Station is the easiest choice for this plan, because the Nohi Bus Center, where buses to Shirakawa-go leave, is right by the station ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000219.html)).

## Day 2: morning markets and Shirakawa-go

### Early morning: the morning markets

Takayama has two morning markets, both open every day:

- **Miyagawa Morning Market**, along the Miyagawa River: 7:00–12:00 from April to November, 8:00–12:00 from December to March ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)).
- **Jinya-mae Morning Market**, in front of Takayama Jinya: same hours ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4001635.html)).

Go first thing, before you check out.

### Late morning: bus to Shirakawa-go

The express bus from Takayama to Shirakawago Bus Terminal takes about 50 minutes, and the village is a 1-minute walk from the terminal ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/), as of October 2026). The adult fare is ¥2,800 one way, and most departures need a reserved seat ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026). Check the current timetable and reserve your seats; our [Takayama to Shirakawa-go guide](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) lists the departures and last buses.

### Afternoon: 2–3 hours in Shirakawa-go

The Tourist Association estimates that walking around the village takes about 2–3 hours ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)). Priorities:

- **Shiroyama viewpoint** for the view over the whole village. The shuttle from in front of Wada House runs 10:00–14:40 every 20 minutes, with a break from 12:00 to 13:00, and costs ¥200 one way, cash only ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026). Go up early in your visit.
- **Wada House**, the largest remaining gassho-zukuri house in the village according to Visit Gifu. Admission is ¥400 for adults, cash only, 9:00–17:00 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/active/13/)).

More detail is in our [Shirakawa-go guide](shirakawa-go-from-nagoya.html).

### Evening: back to Nagoya, or on to Kanazawa

The highway bus between Shirakawa-go and the Meitetsu Bus Center in Nagoya takes about 2 hours 30 minutes, with reserved seats only and adult fares of ¥3,600–¥4,700 depending on the date ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/); [Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html), as of October 2026). Book this seat when you plan the trip; on a two-day trip, it sets how long you can stay in the village.

If you are continuing to Kanazawa instead, buses run on from Shirakawa-go; see our [Japan Alps route guide](nagoya-takayama-shirakawa-go-kanazawa.html).

## FAQ

### Is 2 days in Takayama enough?

Two days covers Takayama's main sights, the morning markets and a visit to Shirakawa-go. To compare one, two and three days, see [How many days in Takayama?](how-many-days-in-takayama.html) Stay longer if you want to add Hida Folk Village and Shirakawa-go at a slower pace, or an onsen night in Gero or Okuhida. From mid-April to mid-November, a third day could be a mountain walk in [Kamikochi](kamikochi-from-takayama.html).

### Should I stay in Takayama or Shirakawa-go?

Takayama is the easier base: it has trains to Nagoya, the Nohi Bus Center by the station, and more places to stay. Shirakawa-go is about 50 minutes away by express bus ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)).

### What time do the Takayama morning markets close?

Both the Miyagawa and Jinya-mae morning markets close at noon. They open at 7:00 from April to November and 8:00 from December to March ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html), as of October 2026).

### Do I need to book buses for this itinerary?

Yes. The Nagoya–Takayama Nohi Bus and the Shirakawa-go–Nagoya highway bus both use reserved seats, with booking opening one month ahead ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/); [Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html)).
