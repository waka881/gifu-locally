If your Japan trip runs from Nagoya into the Japanese Alps and out through Kanazawa to Kyoto or Osaka, the JR Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass is built for that route. This guide sets out exactly what it covers, using the official pass page run by JR Central and JR West, and gives a simple way to check whether it pays off for your plan.

## The pass at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Price | Adult ¥19,800; child (6–11) ¥9,900 |
| Validity | 5 consecutive days |
| JR trains | Nagoya–Takayama–Toyama (incl. Limited Express Hida); Tsuruga–Kyoto–Osaka; Osaka–Kansai Airport |
| Shinkansen | Hokuriku Shinkansen, Toyama–Tsuruga (incl. Kanazawa) |
| Buses | Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa/Toyama and Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Shin-Takaoka (reservations required) |
| Reserved seats | Free on JR limited express and Hokuriku Shinkansen, up to 6 times |
| Not covered | Tokaido Shinkansen Kyoto–Shin-Osaka |
| Where to buy | JR West online reservation, travel agents outside Japan, or station ticket offices |

*Source: [Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass, JR Central and JR West](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/), as of October 2026.*

## What does the pass cover?

The official page lists ([JR Central and JR West](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/), as of October 2026):

- **JR trains:** non-reserved seats in ordinary cars of limited express, express, rapid and local trains between Nagoya and Toyama on the Tokaido and Takayama lines, between Tsuruga and Kyoto and the Osaka city area, and between the Osaka city area and Kansai Airport.
- **Hokuriku Shinkansen:** non-reserved ordinary seats between Toyama and Tsuruga. Kanazawa is on this section.
- **Free reserved seats:** reserved seats on JR limited express trains and the Hokuriku Shinkansen are free up to 6 times.
- **Buses:** the Shirakawa-go/Kanazawa and Takayama/Toyama lines between the Takayama Nohi Bus Center, Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa or Toyama; the Takayama–Shirakawa-go/Takaoka line to Shin-Takaoka; and the World Heritage Bus between Shirakawa-go and Shin-Takaoka Station. Some exceptions apply, and the buses need reservations.
- **Not covered:** the Tokaido Shinkansen between Kyoto and Shin-Osaka.

There is also a 10% discount on the Kurobe Gorge Railway from Unazuki to Kanetsuri and Keyakidaira (same source).

## Is the Takayama-Hokuriku pass worth it?

It depends on how much of the route you use. These are fares on the route that we could confirm from official sources (adult, one way):

| Part of the route | Price without the pass | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Limited Express Hida, Nagoya–Takayama: reserved seat charge only | ¥2,730 (base fare extra) | [JR Central](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044393.pdf), July 2025 |
| Nohi Bus Takayama–Shirakawa-go | ¥2,800 | [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/) |
| Bus Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa | ¥2,800 | [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/) |
| **Subtotal before JR base fares and the trip on from Kanazawa** | **¥8,330** | |

The pass costs ¥19,800, so on the classic route the rest has to come from the JR base fare from Nagoya to Takayama and the trip from Kanazawa onward to Kyoto, Osaka or Kansai Airport. Check those two prices for your dates in a route search, add them to ¥8,330, and compare with ¥19,800.

**As a rule of thumb:**

- **Likely worth it:** the full loop Nagoya → Takayama → Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa → Kyoto/Osaka (or Kansai Airport) within 5 days, because that uses every part of the pass.
- **Likely not worth it:** only Nagoya to Takayama and back, or only Takayama and Shirakawa-go. Buy separate tickets and check the advance discounts in [Nagoya to Takayama](takayama-from-nagoya.html).
- **Check carefully:** if you have a Japan Rail Pass, compare what it already covers before buying a second pass; see [Japan Rail Pass or Takayama-Hokuriku pass?](jr-pass-vs-takayama-hokuriku-pass.html)

## How do you use it on the Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa bus?

The pass covers the Nohi Bus line between Takayama, Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa, but the official page says reservations are required on the bus lines ([JR Central and JR West](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/)). Nohi Bus marks reservation-only services with "R" on its timetable, and most Takayama–Shirakawa-go buses carry that mark ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026). Reserve your bus seats as soon as your dates are fixed, especially in the busy seasons. The full route, with times and luggage tips, is in our [Japan Alps route guide](nagoya-takayama-shirakawa-go-kanazawa.html).

## Tips for using the pass

- **Reserve the Hida.** The official page notes that from September 18 to 23, 2026, every seat on the Limited Express Hida was reserved-only, so a (free) reserved-seat ticket was needed. Using your free reservations on the Hida and the Shinkansen is the simplest habit.
- **Plan the 5 days.** The days are consecutive, so put the long travel legs inside the 5 days and the days you stay in one town outside them.
- **Winter:** Nohi Bus warns of delays of two hours or more on the Shirakawa-go line in winter ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Leave slack before any onward train. See [Takayama in winter](takayama-in-winter.html).

## FAQ

### How much is the Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass?

¥19,800 for adults and ¥9,900 for children aged 6 to 11, valid for 5 consecutive days ([JR Central and JR West](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/), as of October 2026).

### Does the Takayama-Hokuriku pass cover the bus to Shirakawa-go?

Yes. It covers the bus lines between the Takayama Nohi Bus Center, Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa or Toyama, and between Takayama, Shirakawa-go and Shin-Takaoka, but reservations are required and some exceptions apply (same source).

### Does the pass cover the Shinkansen?

It covers the Hokuriku Shinkansen between Toyama and Tsuruga, including Kanazawa, with up to 6 free reserved seats across JR limited express trains and the Hokuriku Shinkansen. It does not cover the Tokaido Shinkansen between Kyoto and Shin-Osaka (same source).

### Is the Takayama-Hokuriku pass worth it for Nagoya to Takayama only?

Usually not. The pass is designed for the longer loop through Takayama, Shirakawa-go, Kanazawa and Kyoto or Osaka. For a Takayama round trip, compare it with separate tickets and advance discounts in our [Nagoya to Takayama guide](takayama-from-nagoya.html).
