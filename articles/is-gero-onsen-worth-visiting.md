Gero Onsen sits on the Hida line between Nagoya and Takayama, so many travellers pass straight through it. This guide sets out what a stop actually costs, how long you need and who will get the most from it, using prices from the Gero Onsen Tourism Association and travel times from Visit Gifu and JR Central.

## Is Gero Onsen worth it? The short answer

| Worth it if you... | Maybe skip it if you... |
|---|---|
| Are travelling Nagoya ↔ Takayama by train and can break the journey | Have only one day for the whole region |
| Want to try onsen cheaply: public baths from ¥470, free foot baths | Want open-air baths with Northern Alps views (Okuhida suits you better) |
| Want a ryokan night in a compact town you can walk around | Are mainly after old streets and sightseeing (Takayama has more) |

*Our assessment, based on the facts and sources below.*

## What does a visit to Gero Onsen cost?

| Item | Adult price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shirasagi-no-yu public bath | ¥470 | About 7 min walk from the station; 10:00–20:45, closed Wednesdays |
| Yumeguri Tegata pass | ¥2,500 | 3 day-use baths at member ryokan, valid 6 months, towel included |
| Foot baths | Free | Nine around town |
| Gero Onsen Gassho Village | ¥800 | Thatched-farmhouse museum, 8:30–17:00 |
| Train from Nagoya | Base fare + ¥2,390 reserved-seat charge | Limited Express Hida, normal season |

*Sources: [Gero Onsen Tourism Association, Shirasagi-no-yu](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_3.html); [Yumeguri Tegata](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_50.html); [Gassho Village](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_12.html); [Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_9.html); [JR Central](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044393.pdf), as of October 2026.*

A day trip of a public bath, a few foot baths and the Gassho Village comes to well under ¥2,000 per adult in entry fees, plus the train. The Yumeguri Tegata is good value if you want to try several ryokan baths: three baths for ¥2,500 with a towel and drawstring bag included, sold at member ryokan, convenience stores and souvenir shops ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_50.html)). Bathing hours differ from inn to inn, so check before you walk over.

## What makes Gero Onsen special?

- **The water.** The Tourism Association describes it as an alkaline simple spring with a pH of 9.2, and counts Gero among Japan's "three famous hot springs" with Arima and Kusatsu ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/onsen/)).
- **A real onsen town.** The Gifu Prefecture tourism site says about 50 ryokan and hotels line the Hida River, with nine foot baths around town ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_9.html), as of October 2026).
- **Easy to reach.** Visit Gifu gives about 1 hour 30–40 minutes from Nagoya by Limited Express Hida ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya)); JR Central gives about 45–60 minutes from Takayama ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/itineraries/takayama-gero-craftsmanship-onsen/)).

**One thing to know:** older guides mention bathing in the open-air pool on the Hida riverbank. Bathing there has been prohibited since December 1, 2021 ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_2.html)).

## How long do you need in Gero Onsen?

| Time | What fits |
|---|---|
| Half day (stopover) | A public bath or two Yumeguri Tegata baths, foot baths, a walk through town |
| Full day | Add Gero Onsen Gassho Village (about 20 min on foot or 6 min by Nohi Bus from the station) |
| One night | A ryokan bath and dinner, an evening walk, a morning soak before moving on |

*Sources: [Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_12.html); our suggested timings.*

## Gero or Okuhida?

Gero is the easier, more walkable onsen town with a train station; Okuhida is a group of mountain hot spring villages with open-air baths and Northern Alps views, about 58 minutes by bus from Takayama to Hirayu ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shinhotaka-line-en/)). ## FAQ

### Is Gero Onsen worth a day trip from Nagoya?

Yes, if your main aim is to try hot springs: it is about 1 hour 30–40 minutes by Limited Express Hida ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya)), and public baths such as Shirasagi-no-yu cost ¥470 ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_3.html), as of October 2026).

### How much does it cost to bathe in Gero Onsen?

¥470 at Shirasagi-no-yu, ¥2,500 for the Yumeguri Tegata pass (three ryokan baths), and nothing at the foot baths (sources above).

### How many hours do you need in Gero Onsen?

A half day is enough for a bath or two, the foot baths and a walk; add a few hours for Gero Onsen Gassho Village, or stay the night for the ryokan experience.

### Can you swim in the river bath at Gero Onsen?

No. Bathing in the open-air pool on the Hida riverbank has been prohibited since December 1, 2021 ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_2.html)).
