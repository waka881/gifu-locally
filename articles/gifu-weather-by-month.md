Gifu City is in the south of Gifu Prefecture, and its weather is very different from the mountain towns in the north. The figures below are the Japan Meteorological Agency's official 30-year averages (1991–2020) for its Gifu weather station.

## How hot is Gifu City in summer?

Hot. August averages 28.3°C, with daily highs of 33.4°C and lows of 24.6°C, and July averages 27.0°C with highs of 31.6°C. Summer is also the wet season: July averages 270.9 mm of rain and September 242.7 mm, the two wettest months of the year ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47632&year=&month=&day=&view=)).

## Does it snow in Gifu City?

Only a little. The averages give 34 cm of snowfall a year: 9 cm in December, 14 cm in January, 10 cm in February and 1 cm in March. Winter is cool and mostly dry, with January averaging 4.6°C (highs 9.1°C, lows 0.7°C) and only 65.9 mm of rain or snow, and it is sunny, with about 155–166 hours of sunshine a month from December to February (same source).

## What should you pack?

- **June to September:** light clothes, sun protection and an umbrella; highs are 28–33°C and these are the wettest months.
- **December to February:** a warm jacket and layers; daily lows are around 1–3°C.
- **March to May and October to November:** a light jacket for mornings and evenings.

## FAQ

### What is the hottest month in Gifu City?

August, averaging 28.3°C with daily highs of 33.4°C ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47632&year=&month=&day=&view=)).

### Does Gifu City get snow?

A little: about 34 cm of snowfall a year on average, mostly in January and February (same source).

### What is the rainiest month in Gifu City?

July, with 270.9 mm on average, followed by September with 242.7 mm (same source).

### Is Gifu City warmer than Takayama?

Yes. January averages 4.6°C in Gifu City against −1.2°C in Takayama, and Gifu gets about 34 cm of snow a year against Takayama's 305 cm (JMA normals above).
