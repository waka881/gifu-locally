Magome and Tsumago are two old post towns on the Nakasendo, about 9 km apart over a mountain pass. You can see both from Nagoya in a day by tour bus or by train and bus. This guide compares the tours listed on Klook with doing it yourself, using the tour pages and the official tourist association sites.

## What do the tours include?

All three leave from Nagoya Station in the morning and are back by about 18:00, and none includes meals ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/175724-inuyama-castle-magome-juku-ena-gorge-day-tour-nagoya/), as of November 22, 2026).

- **Tour A** stops at Inuyama Castle and Sankoinari Shrine, the Enakyo observatory (30 minutes), Magome (1 hour 30 minutes) and Tsumago (1 hour). The guide speaks English, Chinese and Japanese, entry to the Inuyama Castle keep is not included, and the tour runs only with at least 4 people ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/175724-inuyama-castle-magome-juku-ena-gorge-day-tour-nagoya/)).
- **Tour B** has a similar route: Inuyama Castle (pay on site), Ena Valley, Magome and Tsumago, with a Chinese and English guide, in a large group of 4–48 ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/101855/)).
- **Tour C** swaps Inuyama for Kokeizan Eihoji Temple and gives the longest stop in Magome, 2 hours, then 1 hour 10 minutes in Tsumago, in a group of 10–49 ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/24904/)).

None of the three itineraries includes the walk between Magome and Tsumago, so you see each town but not the trail.

## How do you go on your own?

Take the JR Limited Express Shinano from Nagoya to Nakatsugawa, about 50 minutes ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/kisoji-magome-tsumago/)), then the Kita-Ena Kotsu bus from stop 3, 25 minutes to Magome ([Kita-Ena Kotsu](https://kitaena.co.jp/timetable/)). The Tsumago Tourist Association gives the trail to Tsumago as 9 km, 2.5 to 3 hours, over Magome Pass ([Tsumago Tourist Association](https://tsumago.jp/walk/)). From Tsumago, the town bus goes to JR Nagiso Station for ¥300, and the Shinano takes about an hour back to Nagoya ([Nagiso Town](https://www.town.nagiso.nagano.jp/0000000015.html); [Nagiso Town access](https://en.nagiso.jp/access/), as of October 2026). Every step, with luggage forwarding and bear bells, is in our [Magome to Tsumago walk guide](magome-tsumago-walk.html).

## Which should you choose?

- **Choose a tour** if you want both towns and Ena Gorge without planning train and bus connections, or if you are travelling in winter: the Magome Tourist Association says snow may lie from about December to March, and the luggage forwarding service for walkers ends on November 30 ([Magome Tourist Association](https://kiso-magome.com/q_a); [Tsumago Tourist Association](https://tsumago.jp/walk/)).
- **Go on your own** if the walk over Magome Pass is the point of the trip, or if you want more than 1–2 hours in each town.

## FAQ

### Is there a day tour from Nagoya to Magome and Tsumago?

Yes. Klook lists several, such as a tour to Inuyama Castle, Ena Gorge, Magome and Tsumago for US$54.39, 8:30–18:20, rated 4.8 ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/175724-inuyama-castle-magome-juku-ena-gorge-day-tour-nagoya/), as of November 22, 2026).

### Do the tours walk the trail from Magome to Tsumago?

None of the three tours lists the walk; they drive between the towns and give 1–2 hours in each (same sources).

### How long is the walk from Magome to Tsumago?

9 km, taking 2.5 to 3 hours, according to the Tsumago Tourist Association ([Tsumago Tourist Association](https://tsumago.jp/walk/)).

### How do I get to Magome from Nagoya without a tour?

Limited Express Shinano to Nakatsugawa (about 50 minutes), then the 25-minute bus to Magome ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/kisoji-magome-tsumago/); [Kita-Ena Kotsu](https://kitaena.co.jp/timetable/)).
