Takayama is a mountain town in northern Gifu Prefecture. It has a preserved merchant district, two daily morning markets, and an Edo-period government office you can walk through. From Nagoya you can get there by train or by highway bus. This guide covers both, what to see once you arrive, and whether to go for the day or stay a night.

### By train: Limited Express Hida

The Limited Express Hida runs from JR Nagoya Station to JR Takayama Station. According to JR Central, the trip takes **approximately 2 hours and 20 minutes** (as of October 2026, [JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/)).

**Cost.** JR Central's ticketless service lists a Nagoya–Takayama limited express charge of **¥2,730 for an adult reserved seat** and **¥5,000 for the Green Car** in the regular season. The charge is ¥200 lower in off-peak periods and up to ¥400 higher in the busiest periods (as of October 2026, [JR Central ticketless service, Japanese](https://railway.jr-central.co.jp/ticketless/)). That page notes that **you also need a separate basic fare ticket**, and it does not list the basic fare. Check the total at the ticket office or with JR Central's own tools before you travel.

**Save by booking ahead.** JR Central's July 2025 release lists the Tokkyu Ticketless Hayatoku 3 for Nagoya–Takayama at ¥2,400 for an adult reserved seat if you book at least 3 days ahead through JR West's E5489, with limited seats and blackout periods around Golden Week, Obon and New Year. It listed that price as provisional, so check it when you book ([JR Central press release](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044393.pdf)). The same release says TOICA IC cards can be used for the basic fare at Takayama Station on the Hida from October 1, 2025.

### By highway bus

Nohi Bus runs a Takayama–Nagoya express bus line. Its official page lists (as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/)):

- **Travel time:** 2 hours 40 minutes
- **One-way fare, Nagoya to Takayama Bus Terminal:** ¥3,600, or ¥3,300 with the web discount
- **Boarding points in Nagoya:** Meitetsu Bus Center and JR Nagoya Station
- **Reservations:** required on every bus, and all seats are reserved. Booking opens one month ahead. Online bookings are cancelled if you do not pay within 3 days.
- **Round-trip tickets** cannot be bought on the bus

The bus takes about 20 minutes longer than the train. Its one-way fare is a single fixed amount, which makes the cost easy to work out.

## What to see in Takayama

The town center is walkable. The main sights below are 10 to 12 minutes on foot from JR Takayama Station. Hida Folk Village is a short bus ride away.

### Sanmachi old town

The Sanmachi Traditional Buildings Preservation Area is the old merchant district. Its streets are lined with wooden townhouses that have lattice fronts, and water channels run along the edges of the lanes. It is home to several sake breweries, which hang balls of cedar leaves over their doors. You will also find small restaurants that use local ingredients, and souvenir shops. The district is a **12-minute walk from JR Takayama Station** (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)).

Shops and breweries set their own hours, so check individual opening times if a particular stop matters to you.

### Takayama Jinya

Takayama Jinya is the former local government office of the Edo period. Visitors can tour its offices, meeting rooms and storehouse. Details from the official site (as of October 2026, [Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)):

- **Hours:** 8:45 a.m. to 5 p.m. from April 1 to October 31, and 8:45 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. from November 1 to March 31. The ticket window closes 30 minutes before closing time.
- **Closed:** December 29 to January 3. It may also close temporarily for facility inspections or stormy weather.
- **Admission:** ¥500 for individuals. Free for visitors aged 18 and under, who may be asked to show a student ID.

The official site has a notices section where closures are announced. The information page had no renovation notice when checked.

### The morning markets

Takayama has two morning markets, and both are open every day of the year.

- **Miyagawa Morning Market** runs along the Miyagawa River between Kaji-bashi and Yayoi-bashi bridges. Hours are **7 a.m. to noon from April to November** and **8 a.m. to noon from December to March** (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)).
- **Jinya-mae Morning Market** is held in front of Takayama Jinya, a 10-minute walk from the station. It keeps the same hours: **7 a.m. to noon from April to November** and **8 a.m. to noon from December to March** (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4001635.html)).

Both markets close at noon. On a day trip from Nagoya, you will only catch them if you take an early train or bus. If you stay overnight, you can see them easily the next morning.

### Hida Folk Village (Hida no Sato)

Hida Folk Village is an open-air museum of old farmhouses from the Hida region, including thatched gassho-style houses. Details from the official pages (as of October 2026):

- **Hours:** 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., open all year ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html); [Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm))
- **Admission:** ¥700 for adults. ¥200 for children, defined as elementary and junior high school students ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm))
- **Getting there:** About 10 minutes by bus from JR Takayama Station. Take the Sarubobo Bus from Takayama Nohi Bus Center to Hida-no-Sato-Shita. The one-way fare is ¥210 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html)).
- The on-site craft experience center is closed on Thursdays. Artisan demonstrations are scheduled from 10:00 to 15:00, but the times may change ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm)).

## Takayama Festival dates

The Takayama Festival takes place twice a year. Each festival belongs to a different shrine, and the parades use different areas of town (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/4000105.html)):

- **Spring Takayama Festival (Sanno Matsuri):** April 14 and 15, held by Hie Jinja Shrine
- **Autumn Takayama Festival (Hachiman Matsuri):** October 9 and 10, held by Sakurayama Hachimangu Shrine

The 2026 autumn festival has already taken place. The next festival is the spring festival on April 14–15. Expect large crowds on festival days. If you want to stay overnight then, book your train seats and your room well in advance.

## Day trip or overnight?

**A day trip works** if you leave Nagoya early. With about 2 hours 20 minutes each way by train, or 2 hours 40 minutes by bus, you can still have a full afternoon in town. A realistic plan covers Sanmachi, Takayama Jinya, and either Hida Folk Village or a sake brewery. The morning markets close at noon, so on a day trip you will likely see them only if you arrive early.

**An overnight stay** adds the evening, when the old town is quieter, and the morning markets the next day. It also lets you continue to another destination, such as Shirakawa-go or Gero, instead of going back to Nagoya.

A simple overnight plan:

- **Day 1:** Arrive late morning. See Sanmachi and Takayama Jinya, then Hida Folk Village in the afternoon.
- **Day 2:** Visit the Miyagawa and Jinya-mae morning markets. Then go back to Nagoya, or continue to Shirakawa-go or Gero.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to get from Nagoya to Takayama?

The Limited Express Hida takes approximately 2 hours and 20 minutes from JR Nagoya Station to JR Takayama Station (as of October 2026, [JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/)). The Nohi Bus highway bus takes 2 hours 40 minutes (as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/)).

### How much is the train or bus from Nagoya to Takayama?

The limited express charge is ¥2,730 for an adult reserved seat and ¥5,000 for the Green Car in the regular season, and a separate basic fare ticket is also needed (as of October 2026, [JR Central ticketless service, Japanese](https://railway.jr-central.co.jp/ticketless/)). The one-way bus fare to Takayama Bus Terminal is ¥3,600, or ¥3,300 with the web discount (as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/)).

### Do I need to reserve the Nagoya–Takayama highway bus?

Yes. Reservations are required on every bus and all seats are reserved, with booking opening one month ahead (as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/)). Online bookings are cancelled if you do not pay within 3 days.

### What time do the Takayama morning markets open?

Both the Miyagawa and Jinya-mae morning markets are open every day, from 7 a.m. to noon from April to November and 8 a.m. to noon from December to March (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)). On a day trip from Nagoya, you will only catch them with an early train or bus.

### When is the Takayama Festival?

The Spring Takayama Festival is on April 14 and 15, and the Autumn Takayama Festival is on October 9 and 10 (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/4000105.html)). Book train seats and rooms well in advance if you plan to stay overnight then.
