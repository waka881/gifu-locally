## How cold and snowy is Takayama in December?

Cold, and getting snowier. The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages give 1.6°C for December, with daily highs of 6.2°C and lows of −1.7°C, and 66 cm of snowfall over the month, against only 2 cm in November ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)). See [Takayama weather by month](takayama-weather-by-month.html) for the full year, and pack a warm coat, gloves and shoes with grip.

## What is on in Takayama in December?

- **Winter illumination:** Hida Takayama Tourism lists the Nakabashi Bridge and Miyagawa River area as lit from December 1 to March 15, 16:30–21:30 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000181.html), page updated November 2025).
- **Okuhida ice illuminations:** the free Aodaru Illumination of ice columns near the Fukuji Onsen bus stop is listed from late December to late March, 17:00–22:00, about 1 hour 10 minutes by bus from JR Takayama Station, and the Taruma Kanekori illumination at Shin-Hirayu Onsen from late December to February 28, 17:00–21:30 (same source). See [where to stay in Okuhida](where-to-stay-okuhida.html).
- **Gero Onsen fireworks:** the Fireworks Musical is on December 5, 12 and 19, 2026 (20:00–20:15), with a Christmas show on December 24 (20:00–20:30) ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1279.html)). Details are in our [Gero Onsen winter fireworks guide](gero-onsen-winter-fireworks.html), and the trains in [Takayama to Gero Onsen](takayama-to-gero-onsen.html).

The [Hida Folk Village illumination](hida-folk-village-winter-illumination.html) and the Shirakawa-go light-up start in January; see [Takayama in January](takayama-in-january.html).

## What changes at the end of December?

Takayama Jinya is closed from December 29 to January 3 ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)), while the morning markets run all year, from 8:00 to 12:00 from December to March ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)). For the rest of the year-end closures and bus changes, see [Takayama at New Year](takayama-new-year.html).

## What changes for buses from December 1, 2026?

Nohi Bus revises fares and timetables from December 1, 2026: Takayama to Shirakawa-go goes from ¥2,800 to ¥2,900, Takayama to Kanazawa from ¥4,200 to ¥4,400 and Takayama to Toyama from ¥3,800 to ¥3,900 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026). See [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) and [Takayama to Kanazawa by bus](takayama-to-kanazawa-bus.html).

## FAQ

### Does it snow in Takayama in December?

Yes. The Japan Meteorological Agency's averages give 66 cm of snowfall in December, with daily lows of −1.7°C ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)).

### Is Takayama open between Christmas and New Year?

Mostly. Takayama Jinya closes from December 29 to January 3, but the morning markets run all year ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/); [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)).

### When does the Takayama winter illumination start?

Hida Takayama Tourism lists it from December 1 to March 15, 16:30–21:30, around Nakabashi Bridge and the Miyagawa River ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000181.html)).

### How much is the bus from Takayama to Shirakawa-go in December 2026?

¥2,900 one way for adults from December 1, 2026 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).
