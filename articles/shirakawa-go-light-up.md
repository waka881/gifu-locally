On a few winter evenings each year, the thatched gassho-zukuri farmhouses of Shirakawa-go's Ogimachi village are lit up against the snow. You can't just turn up: the event is reservation-only, and most of the ways in sell out weeks or months ahead. This guide covers the confirmed 2027 dates, each way to secure a place, and how to get there from Nagoya or Takayama.

## Shirakawa-go light-up 2027 dates and times

The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association has confirmed the **41st Shirakawa-go Light-Up Event** for these dates ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/), as of October 2026):

| Date | Day |
|---|---|
| January 11, 2027 | Monday (national holiday) |
| January 17, 2027 | Sunday |
| January 24, 2027 | Sunday |
| January 31, 2027 | Sunday |

The lights are on from **5:30 pm to 7:30 pm** ([Nohi Bus light-up tour page](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/), as of October 2026). The same four dates appear in Nohi Bus's [2027 schedule notice](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/20260702-lu/).

The association published the schedule on July 1, 2026, and an event overview on September 2, 2026 ([announcements list](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/)). The final 2027 rules are on the [full 2027 overview page](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/142/), so read it before you book.

## The key rule: reservation-only, no walk-ins

For the 2026 (40th) event, the organisers said the light-up was "strictly reservation-based (entry ticket required)." Arriving the same day without a reservation was not allowed, whether by route bus, private car, rental car, taxi or chartered bus ([2026 notice, published July 1, 2025](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/datas/files/2025/06/27/1dde4eb133e0d6ab4d776ad54ec807c1961b7b14.pdf)). The village also described the 2026 event as run on a "complete advance reservation system" with tickets ([Shirakawa village, last updated July 3, 2025](https://www.vill.shirakawa.lg.jp/2768.htm)).

Expect the same approach in 2027, but check the [2027 overview](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/142/) to confirm the exact wording.

## Four ways to get a spot in 2027

### 1. Nohi Bus light-up tour from Takayama (best for most visitors)

Nohi Bus runs official light-up bus tours from the **Takayama Nohi Bus Center** on all four dates. There are three courses. All prices and times below come from the [Nohi Bus English page](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/), as of October 2026:

| Course | Fare (adult or child 6–12) | Departs Takayama | Back in Takayama | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: Gassho-zukuri Minkaen | 13,000 yen | 3:40 pm | 8:20 pm | Round-trip bus, open-air museum entry with zenzai (sweet red-bean soup) |
| B: Observation deck | 16,000 yen | 3:10 pm | 8:45 pm | Round-trip bus, Ogimachi Castle observation deck ticket, souvenir |
| C: Hirase Onsen | 14,000 yen | 2:40 pm | 8:20 pm | Round-trip bus, Shiramizu-no-yu bath ticket with towel, souvenir |

**Sales dates.** Sales open in stages at 2:00 pm (Japan time) ([Nohi Bus Japanese page](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027/), as of October 2026):

- January 11 tour: from October 13, 2026
- January 17 tour: from October 19, 2026
- January 24 tour: from October 26, 2026
- January 31 tour: from November 2, 2026

**Booking rules** (as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)):

- You can only book online, through the Japanese "Hassha Oline Net" site ([secure.j-bus.co.jp](https://secure.j-bus.co.jp/hon)). Phone bookings aren't accepted.
- Each booking can include up to 6 people. You pay by credit card when you book.
- You can't make changes after payment. Cancelling before departure costs a 100-yen refund fee, and you can't cancel after departure.
- You must swap your booking for a ticket at the Takayama Nohi Bus Center counter before you board. The counter opens at 7:00 am on the day.
- You can't get on or off at stops along the way, and you can't book only one direction. Nohi Bus notes that no route bus runs back to Takayama after the light-up.
- Course C's bath ticket is valid from the day after the tour. To bathe on tour day, you need to take the 5:04 pm local bus from Hirase Onsen.

### 2. Stay overnight in the village (lottery)

Inns booked through the Tourist Association are allocated **by lottery**, not first come, first served. Applications opened on October 1, 2026 ([Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/), as of October 2026). Check the 2027 deadlines on the association's [lottery page](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/145/).

Here is how it worked for the **2026 event** (Tourist Association, [published November 26, 2025](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/130/)):

- The lottery closed on October 31, and results went out on November 10. Rooms that winners declined were then released first come, first served.
- Each stay included an observation deck ticket, handed out at the inn.
- Booking pages were in Japanese only, and payment was by credit card.
- Hirase Onsen guests needed a separate 6,000-yen light-up parking permit to drive to the venue.

### 3. Drive and reserve parking

Regular-car parking for 2027 is sold online only, through the Tourist Association website, and paid by credit card. The **first round (from September 10, 2026) is sold out**. The **second round goes on sale on December 1, 2026, at 1:00 pm** ([Tourist Association notice, September 10, 2026](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/events/443/)). The notice gives no price.

Winter roads to Shirakawa-go can be snowy and icy. If you aren't used to driving in snow, a bus tour is the simpler choice.

## The observation deck: book it in advance

The well-known overhead view comes from the **Ogimachi Castle Ruins observation deck**. According to Nohi Bus (as of October 2026, [source](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)):

- Entry is strictly controlled, and **tickets are not sold on site**.
- You walk up from the village, about 20 minutes each way, on roads that can be slippery with snow.
- Guests who have trouble walking can request a shuttle through the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association (+81-5769-6-1013).
- Only Course B on the Nohi tour includes the deck. Courses A and C don't give access.

For the 2026 event, inn stays also came with a deck ticket ([Tourist Association, November 26, 2025](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/130/)).

## How to get there from Takayama

Takayama is the departure point for the official light-up tours, so it is the easiest base. Outside light-up evenings, Nohi Bus route buses take **about 50 minutes** from Takayama to Shirakawa-go and cost **2,800 yen one way** (as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Some buses are reservation-only. Nohi Bus warns that delays of two hours or more can happen. On light-up dates, book the tour rather than relying on route buses, because the tour includes your return.

## How to get there from Nagoya

There's no official Nohi light-up tour leaving from Nagoya, since its tours start only in Takayama. You have two practical options, or you can book a day tour from Nagoya (see option 4 above):

**Option A: Train to Takayama, then the Nohi tour.** The Limited Express Hida takes about 2 hours 20 minutes from Nagoya Station to Takayama ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/), as of October 2026). Arrive with time to collect your ticket before the tour leaves between 2:40 pm and 3:40 pm. Tours return to Takayama between 8:20 pm and 8:45 pm, so plan to stay the night in Takayama. Our [Takayama from Nagoya guide](takayama-from-nagoya.html) covers the train in more detail.

Bookings open one month before departure. In 2026, arriving by route bus without a light-up reservation wasn't allowed, so only use this option if you already have a reservation, such as an inn stay. ## Practical tips for the night

- **Dress for cold and snow.** Nohi Bus warns that evenings are very cold and recommends warm clothing and non-slip, snow-ready shoes ([source](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)).
- **Allow for delays.** Snow and traffic can shorten your time in the village (same source).
- **Watch for cancellations.** The organisers said the 2026 event could be cancelled if a nationwide cold wave seriously disrupted transport ([2026 notice](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/datas/files/2025/06/27/1dde4eb133e0d6ab4d776ad54ec807c1961b7b14.pdf)).
- **Stay connected.** Booking emails and bus updates come by email and online, so data on your phone helps. A [Japan eSIM](https://yesim.tpk.lu/T6xnCd9w) is one option.

## FAQ

### When is the Shirakawa-go light-up in 2027?

It takes place on January 11 (a national holiday), 17, 24 and 31, 2027, with lights on from 5:30 to 7:30 pm, according to the [Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/) and [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/), as of October 2026.

### Can I go to the light-up without a reservation?

Probably not. For 2026, the organisers didn't allow same-day entry without a reservation by any form of transport ([2026 notice](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/datas/files/2025/06/27/1dde4eb133e0d6ab4d776ad54ec807c1961b7b14.pdf)). Check the [2027 overview](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/142/) for this year's rules.

### How do I get an observation deck ticket?

Tickets aren't sold on site. In 2027 you can get one through Nohi Bus Course B (16,000 yen as of October 2026, [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)). In 2026, inn stays booked through the Tourist Association also included one.

### Is there a light-up tour from Nagoya?

Nohi Bus's official tours leave only from Takayama. From Nagoya, take the Limited Express Hida to Takayama (about 2 hours 20 minutes, [JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/)), join the tour, and stay the night in Takayama.
