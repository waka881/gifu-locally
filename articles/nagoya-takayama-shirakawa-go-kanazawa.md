One of the most popular routes through central Japan runs from Nagoya up into the mountains to Takayama, across to the thatched village of Shirakawa-go, and down to Kanazawa on the Sea of Japan coast. This guide shows how each leg works, what it costs and what to book, using figures from JR Central, Nohi Bus and the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association.

## The route at a glance

| Leg | How | Time | Adult fare (one way) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nagoya → Takayama | JR Limited Express Hida | About 2 h 20 min | Base fare + limited express charge |
| Nagoya → Takayama (alternative) | Nohi Bus highway bus | 2 h 40 min | ¥3,600 (¥3,300 web discount) |
| Takayama → Shirakawa-go | Nohi Bus | About 50 min | ¥2,800 |
| Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa | Bus | About 1 h 20 min | ¥2,800 |
| Takayama → Kanazawa (riding through) | Nohi Bus | 2 h 5 min–2 h 20 min | ¥4,200 |

*Sources: [JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/); [Nohi Bus, Nagoya line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/); [Nohi Bus, Shirakawa-go line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/); [Nohi Bus express bus list](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/english/highwaybus/); [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/), as of October 2026.*

## How do you get from Nagoya to Takayama?

- **Train:** the Limited Express Hida runs from JR Nagoya Station to JR Takayama Station in approximately 2 hours 20 minutes ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/), as of October 2026). You pay the base fare plus a limited express charge.
- **Bus:** the Nohi Bus highway bus takes 2 hours 40 minutes and costs ¥3,600 one way, or ¥3,300 with the web discount. Every bus needs a reservation, and booking opens one month ahead ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/), as of October 2026).

The train suits rail-pass holders and anyone who wants to stop at Gero Onsen on the way; see [Gero Onsen vs Okuhida](gero-vs-okuhida-onsen.html). Full details for this leg are in [Nagoya to Takayama](takayama-from-nagoya.html).

## How do you get from Takayama to Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa?

Nohi Bus runs a line from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center, next to JR Takayama Station, to Shirakawa-go and on to Kanazawa Station, Takaoka or Toyama Station ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/english/highwaybus/), as of October 2026). Key points from the operator's route page ([Nohi Bus, Shirakawa-go line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026):

- **Fares (adult, one way):** Takayama–Shirakawa-go ¥2,800; Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa ¥2,800; Takayama–Kanazawa ¥4,200; Shirakawa-go–Toyama ¥2,400.
- **Time:** about 50 minutes from Takayama to Shirakawa-go.
- **Frequency:** the page lists 16 round trips a day, but some services do not run on weekends, national holidays, August 13–15 or December 29–January 3.
- **Reservations:** services marked "R" on the timetable need a reserved seat, and most Takayama–Shirakawa-go services carry that mark. Reserved buses can be booked online through Japan Bus Online. Mobile tickets are only for non-reserved buses.

The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association gives the express bus time between Kanazawa Station and Shirakawago Bus Terminal as about 1 hour 20 minutes ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)). For a day trip the other way, see [Shirakawa-go from Kanazawa](shirakawa-go-from-kanazawa.html).

### Should you buy Takayama–Kanazawa or two separate tickets?

A through ticket from Takayama to Kanazawa costs ¥4,200, but you stay on the bus. To spend time in Shirakawa-go, you buy two tickets (¥2,800 + ¥2,800 = ¥5,600) and reserve two separate buses. Book the second bus with enough time in the village: the Tourist Association says walking around takes about 2–3 hours ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)).

## What about luggage?

This route usually means travelling with suitcases between hotels.

- **On the bus:** Nohi Bus says large items may not fit in the trunk, and you handle your own bags. Keep power banks with you in the cabin, not in the luggage hold ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).
- **In Shirakawa-go:** coin lockers at Shirakawago Bus Terminal cost ¥500 (small or medium) or ¥1,000 (large) per use, from 8:00 to 17:00, and a luggage storage service next to the terminal is open until 17:00 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026).

Travelling light makes every bus leg easier. If you are starting in Nagoya, you can also leave bags there for a day trip with [Radical Storage](https://radicalstorage.com/luggage-storage/nagoya).

## Is this route possible in winter?

Yes, but plan for delays. Nohi Bus warns that in winter, delays of two hours or more can occur, because of traffic waiting for parking at Shirakawa-go, heavy snow or expressway closures ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026). Don't book a tight onward train or flight from Kanazawa on the same day.

The Shirakawa-go light-up on January 11, 17, 24 and 31, 2027 is the busiest time, and the 2026 event was reservation-only. See our [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html) and [best time to visit Shirakawa-go](best-time-to-visit-shirakawa-go.html).

## FAQ

### How long does it take from Takayama to Kanazawa by bus?

Nohi Bus lists 2 hours 5 minutes to 2 hours 20 minutes for its line from Takayama via Shirakawa-go to Kanazawa, Takaoka and Toyama, and the adult fare from Takayama to Kanazawa is ¥4,200 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026).

### How much is the bus from Takayama to Shirakawa-go?

¥2,800 one way for adults, and the trip takes about 50 minutes ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026).

### Do I need to reserve the bus from Takayama to Shirakawa-go?

Usually yes. Nohi Bus marks reservation-required services with "R" on the timetable, and most Takayama–Shirakawa-go services carry that mark. Check the mark for your departure and book through Japan Bus Online ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).

### Can I stop in Shirakawa-go with my luggage?

Yes. Coin lockers at Shirakawago Bus Terminal cost ¥500 or ¥1,000 per use, and a luggage storage service next to the terminal is open until 17:00 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026).
