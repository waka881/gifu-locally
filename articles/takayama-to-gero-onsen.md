Takayama and Gero Onsen are both on the JR Takayama Line, so the Limited Express Hida links them directly. This guide gives the journey time, example trains and the bus ticket option, using JR Central and JR West's timetable.

## Takayama to Gero at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Train | JR Limited Express Hida, JR Takayama Station ↔ JR Gero Station, direct |
| Journey time | About 45 min on the Hida; JR Central gives 45–60 min by train |
| Example, Takayama → Gero | Hida 16: Takayama 15:34 → Gero 16:17 |
| Example, Gero → Takayama | Hida 5: Gero 11:30 → Takayama 12:14 |
| Onward from Gero | Same trains continue to Nagoya, and Hida 36 to Kyoto and Osaka |
| Fare | Check JR for your date |

*Sources: [JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/itineraries/takayama-gero-craftsmanship-onsen/); [JR West timetable, Hida 16/36](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/77031?date=20261027) and [Hida 5](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027), timetables for October 27, 2026.*

## How long is the train from Takayama to Gero?

About 45 minutes on the Limited Express Hida. JR Central's suggested itinerary gives Takayama to Gero as about 45–60 minutes by train ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/itineraries/takayama-gero-craftsmanship-onsen/)). On JR West's timetable, Hida 16 leaves Takayama at 15:34 and arrives in Gero at 16:17, and in the other direction Hida 5 leaves Gero at 11:30 and reaches Takayama at 12:14 ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/77031?date=20261027); [JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027)).

Seat reservations change over the New Year: JR West's timetable notes changes from December 25 to January 5 (same source), and the Takayama-Hokuriku pass site says all Hida seats are reserved in that period ([Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass](https://touristpass.jp/en/takayama_hokuriku/)). Book a seat before you board then.

## Can you go on from Gero to Nagoya, Kyoto or Osaka?

Yes, on the same train. Hida 16 continues from Gero to Gifu and Nagoya (arriving 18:06), and the Osaka-bound section, Hida 36, splits at Gifu and reaches Kyoto at 19:17 and Osaka at 19:50 ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/77031?date=20261027), October 2026). So a night in Takayama, then an afternoon in Gero before heading home, works without extra changes. Going the other way, see [Osaka or Kyoto to Gero Onsen](osaka-kyoto-to-gero-onsen.html) and [Nagoya to Gero Onsen](nagoya-to-gero-onsen.html).

## Should you stay a night in Gero?

If you have time, yes: a night lets you have an evening bath and a ryokan dinner, then continue in the morning. Gero has public baths and foot baths near the station; see the [Gero Onsen guide](gero-onsen-guide.html), [where to stay in Gero Onsen](where-to-stay-gero-onsen.html) and [is Gero Onsen worth visiting?](is-gero-onsen-worth-visiting.html). ## FAQ

### How far is Gero Onsen from Takayama?

About 45 minutes by Limited Express Hida; JR Central gives 45–60 minutes by train ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/itineraries/takayama-gero-craftsmanship-onsen/)).

### Can I do Gero Onsen as a day trip from Takayama?

Yes, it is close enough, but it fits better as a stop on the way to or from Nagoya, Kyoto or Osaka, since the same Hida trains continue there (JR West timetable, October 2026).

### Is there a direct train from Gero to Osaka?

Yes. Hida 36 leaves Gero at 16:18 (as part of Hida 16) and reaches Kyoto at 19:17 and Osaka at 19:50 ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/77031?date=20261027), October 2026).

### Is there a bus from Takayama to Gero?

Klook lists a 2-day bus ticket for unlimited rides between the Takayama Nohi Bus Center and Gero Onsen ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/activity/179451-hida-takayama-gero-onsen-2-day-free-ticket/)).
