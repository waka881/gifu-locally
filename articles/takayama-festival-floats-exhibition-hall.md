The Takayama Festival happens only four days a year, but some of its floats can be seen every day at the Takayama Festival Floats Exhibition Hall (Takayama Matsuri Yatai Kaikan). This guide uses the Gifu Prefecture tourism site, Hida Takayama Tourism and Centrip Japan.

## Floats Exhibition Hall at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| What you see | 4 of the 11 autumn-festival floats at a time |
| Rotation | Three times a year (March, July and November) |
| Adult admission | ¥1,000 (Gifu Prefecture tourism); ¥900 on Hida Takayama Tourism's English page |
| High school / elementary and junior high | ¥600 / ¥500 |
| Hours | 9:00–17:00 (March–November); 9:00–16:30 (December–February) |
| Closed | Open every day |
| Getting there | About 20 minutes on foot from JR Takayama Station, next to Sakurayama Hachimangu Shrine |

*Sources: [Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1244.html); [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000170.html); [Centrip Japan](https://centrip-japan.com/node/1403), as of November 2026. The sources differ on the adult price, so check at the ticket counter.*

## What can you see at the Floats Exhibition Hall?

The hall keeps the floats used in the autumn Takayama Festival. The Gifu Prefecture tourism site says four of the 11 are on display at any time and that they are swapped three times a year ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1244.html)); Hida Takayama Tourism gives the rotation months as March, July and November ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000170.html)). The prefecture site also describes the swaps as a rare chance to see floats moved through town outside the festival days ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_4807.html)).

## How much is the ticket and when is it open?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site lists adult admission at ¥1,000 (¥880 for groups of 20 or more), ¥600 for high school students and ¥500 for elementary and junior high students, open every day from 9:00 to 17:00 from March to November and 9:00 to 16:30 from December to February ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1244.html), as of November 2026). Centrip Japan gives the same prices and hours ([Centrip Japan](https://centrip-japan.com/node/1403)). Hida Takayama Tourism's English page still shows ¥900 for adults ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000170.html)), so bring a little extra cash.

## How do you get there?

Walk about 20 minutes from JR Takayama Station ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1244.html)). Centrip Japan places it next to Sakurayama Hachimangu Shrine ([Centrip Japan](https://centrip-japan.com/node/1403)). It fits well after the [Miyagawa morning market](takayama-morning-market.html) and the [Sanmachi old town and sake breweries](takayama-sake-breweries.html). See our [one day in Takayama](one-day-in-takayama.html) plan for the rest of the day.

## Is it worth it if you miss the festival?

Yes, if you want to see the floats themselves. The Takayama Festival is held on April 14–15 (spring) and October 9–10 (autumn); see our [Takayama Festival guide](takayama-festival.html) for the schedule. On any other day, the hall is the place to see the real floats up close. If you only have a few hours in Takayama, [Takayama Jinya](takayama-jinya.html) and the old town may come first; [things to do in Takayama](things-to-do-in-takayama.html) compares the main sights.

## FAQ

### How much is the Takayama Festival Floats Exhibition Hall?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site lists ¥1,000 for adults, ¥600 for high school students and ¥500 for elementary and junior high students; Hida Takayama Tourism's English page shows ¥900 for adults ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1244.html); [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000170.html), as of November 2026).

### What are the opening hours?

9:00–17:00 from March to November and 9:00–16:30 from December to February, every day (same sources).

### How many floats are on display?

Four of the 11 autumn-festival floats at a time, rotated three times a year ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1244.html)).

### How far is it from Takayama Station?

About 20 minutes on foot (same source).
