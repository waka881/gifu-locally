Shirakawa-go and Gokayama are listed together by UNESCO as the "Historic Villages of Shirakawa-go and Gokayama" (inscribed 1995): three villages of thatched gassho-style farmhouses along the Sho River in Gifu and Toyama prefectures ([UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/734/)). Ogimachi is in Shirakawa-go, Gifu; Ainokura and Suganuma are in Gokayama, in Nanto City, Toyama. This guide compares them using UNESCO, the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association, the Toyama Tourism Organization and JR West.

## What is the difference between Gokayama and Shirakawa-go?

They are the same kind of village and part of the same World Heritage site; the differences are size and access. Shirakawa-go's Ogimachi is the village most people mean when they talk about Japan's thatched farmhouses, and it is on the express bus route between Takayama and Kanazawa. The Toyama Tourism Organization describes Ainokura as a village of about 20 gassho houses aged 100 to 350 years, the largest in Gokayama ([Visit Toyama](https://www.visit-toyama-japan.com/en/places-to-go/41003)). Gokayama is reached from the Toyama side, from JR Takaoka or Shin-Takaoka stations ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/ticket/pass/hokuriku-arch-pass/travel-information/toyama_004.html)).

## Which should you choose?

- **Choose Shirakawa-go** if you are based in Takayama or Kanazawa, have half a day, and want the easiest bus trip. See [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) and [Shirakawa-go from Kanazawa](shirakawa-go-from-kanazawa.html).
- **Choose Gokayama** if you are travelling on the Hokuriku side (Takaoka, Toyama or Kanazawa) or driving, and want a smaller village. Visit Toyama lists a volunteer guide for ¥2,000 at Ainokura ([Visit Toyama](https://www.visit-toyama-japan.com/en/places-to-go/41003)).
- **Do both** on a day tour from Takayama (below), or by car.

## How do you get to Gokayama?

**By World Heritage Bus.** JR West lists the World Heritage Bus to Ainokura-guchi stop at about 80 minutes from JR Takaoka Station, 65 minutes from Shin-Takaoka (Hokuriku Shinkansen) and 25 minutes from Johana, then about 5 minutes on foot; Suganuma is about 95, 80 and 40 minutes from the same stops, then about 1 minute on foot ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/ticket/pass/hokuriku-arch-pass/travel-information/toyama_004.html)). The bus is run by Kaetsuno Bus; check its timetable and fares before you travel.

**By car.** Ainokura is about 20 minutes from Gokayama IC via Route 156, with parking at ¥1,000 per car ([Visit Toyama](https://www.visit-toyama-japan.com/en/places-to-go/41003)). If you drive in winter, read our [Gifu winter guide](gifu-winter-guide.html) first.

For help in English, the Gokayama Tourist Information Center is on +81-763-66-2468 ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/ticket/pass/hokuriku-arch-pass/travel-information/toyama_004.html)).

## FAQ

### Is Gokayama part of the Shirakawa-go World Heritage site?

Yes. UNESCO lists them together as the "Historic Villages of Shirakawa-go and Gokayama", inscribed in 1995 ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/734/)).

### How long does it take to get to Gokayama?

By World Heritage Bus, about 80 minutes from JR Takaoka Station to Ainokura-guchi, plus a 5-minute walk; by car, about 20 minutes from Gokayama IC ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/ticket/pass/hokuriku-arch-pass/travel-information/toyama_004.html); [Visit Toyama](https://www.visit-toyama-japan.com/en/places-to-go/41003)).

### Is Ainokura free to visit?

Visit Toyama lists free admission, 8:30–17:00, with parking at ¥1,000 per car ([Visit Toyama](https://www.visit-toyama-japan.com/en/places-to-go/41003), as of November 2026).

### Can I see Gokayama and Shirakawa-go in one day from Takayama?

Yes. Klook lists a full-day tour from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center that stops at both, from about US$90 ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/90970-historic-villages-shirakawa-go-gokayama-join-bus-trip-takayama/), as of November 2026).
