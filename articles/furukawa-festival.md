The Furukawa Festival takes over the small canal town of Hida-Furukawa, north of Takayama, every April. This guide uses the Gifu Prefecture tourism site's 2026 listing and Hida City's pages.

## Furukawa Festival at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Dates | April 19–20 every year |
| April 19 (night) | Okoshi Daiko drum procession, into the small hours; held even in rain |
| April 20 | Festival floats (yatai) through town, lit by lanterns at night; cancelled in rain |
| Also | Karakuri puppet and children's kabuki offerings |

## When is the Furukawa Festival?

On April 19 and 20 every year ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1313.html); [Hida City](https://city.hida.gifu.jp/site/kamiokalab-en/12041.html)). That is a few days after the [Spring Takayama Festival](takayama-festival.html) on April 14–15, so one trip can take in both. The prefecture's page links a pamphlet with each year's timetable and the drum's route, so check it closer to the date.

## What is the Okoshi Daiko?

On the night of April 19, men dressed in white sarashi carry a large drum on a wooden platform through the town until late at night, while groups of young men with smaller "tsuke-daiko" drums take turns charging at it. The Gifu Prefecture tourism site says the Okoshi Daiko goes ahead even in rain ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1313.html)). Together with the floats, it is designated a national Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property ([Hida City](https://city.hida.gifu.jp/site/kamiokalab-en/12041.html)), and the prefecture notes it is also on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list.

## What happens on April 20?

The festival floats are pulled through town, and on the night of the 20th they move through the streets lit by lanterns. In rain, the float procession is cancelled. The festival also includes karakuri puppet and children's kabuki performances; the prefecture's page gives a map of where they take place in fine weather, with times marked as approximate ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1313.html)).

If you can't be there in April, the Okoshi-Daiko no Sato Festival Exhibition Hall shows three of the festival floats all year, about 7 minutes on foot from JR Hida-Furukawa Station ([Hida City](https://city.hida.gifu.jp/site/kamiokalab-en/12041.html)).

## How do you get there from Takayama?

Hida-Furukawa is about 15 minutes by train from Takayama ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/features/132)), and the festival area is within walking distance of JR Hida-Furukawa Station ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1313.html)). The Okoshi Daiko runs late into the night, so check the last train back before you go. For the rest of the town, see [Hida-Furukawa or Takayama](hida-furukawa-or-takayama.html).

## FAQ

### When is the Furukawa Festival?

Every year on April 19 and 20 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1313.html)).

### What is the Okoshi Daiko?

A large drum carried through Hida-Furukawa by men in white sarashi on the night of April 19, challenged by smaller drums; it is held even in rain (same source).

### Is the Furukawa Festival cancelled in rain?

The Okoshi Daiko goes ahead in rain; the float procession on April 20 is cancelled (same source).

### How far is Hida-Furukawa from Takayama?

About 15 minutes by train ([Hida City tourism](https://www.hida-kankou.jp/features/132)).
