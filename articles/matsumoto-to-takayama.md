Matsumoto and Takayama sit on either side of the Northern Alps, and the bus crosses between them through the Okuhida hot spring area. It is a popular link for travellers going between the Nagano and Gifu sides of the Japanese Alps. This guide uses Nohi Bus's own timetable and fare table.

## Matsumoto to Takayama at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Operators | Nohi Bus and Alpico (jointly) |
| Route | Matsumoto Bus Terminal → Shin-Shimashima Station → Nakanoyu → Hirayu Onsen → Takayama Bus Terminal |
| Time | 2 h 20 min listed; 2 h 33 min to 2 h 48 min on the timetable |
| Adult fare | ¥4,400 Matsumoto–Takayama; ¥3,000 Matsumoto–Hirayu Onsen |
| Buses | 6 a day each way, plus one seasonal bus via the Shinhotaka Ropeway |
| Reservations | Required (since April 1, 2024); online at highwaybus.com or by phone |
| Children | Half fare, ages 1–12 |

*Source: [Nohi Bus, Takayama–Matsumoto line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/matsumoto_en/), as of October 2026.*

**Stop at Hirayu Onsen.** Hirayu is a hot spring village on the route, and it is where the shuttle to Kamikochi leaves in season. You could leave Matsumoto in the morning, spend a night in the Okuhida hot springs, and continue to Takayama the next day. See [Gero vs Okuhida](gero-vs-okuhida-onsen.html), [Kamikochi from Takayama](kamikochi-from-takayama.html) and the [Shinhotaka Ropeway guide](shinhotaka-ropeway.html).

## How do you book the bus?

- **Reservations are required** on every bus. Book online through highwaybus.com, linked from [the Nohi Bus page](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/matsumoto_en/), or by phone at the Nohi Bus Reservation Center (+81-577-32-1688, 9:00–17:00).
- Round-trip tickets can't be bought on the bus.
- Nohi Bus warns that traffic delays may cause missed connections, so leave a margin before an onward train (same source).
- Stops at Sawando and Nakanoyu change location by season; check Nohi Bus's PDF linked from the page.

## FAQ

### How long is the bus from Matsumoto to Takayama?

Nohi Bus lists 2 hours 20 minutes; on the timetable, buses take about 2 hours 33 minutes to 2 hours 48 minutes from the Matsumoto Bus Terminal to the Takayama Bus Terminal ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/matsumoto_en/), as of October 2026).

### How much is the bus from Matsumoto to Takayama?

¥4,400 for adults and half fare for children aged 1–12 (same source).

### Do I need to reserve the Matsumoto–Takayama bus?

Yes. Nohi Bus says reservations have been required since April 1, 2024; book on highwaybus.com or by phone (same source).

### Can I stop at Hirayu Onsen on the way?

Yes. Hirayu Onsen is a stop on every bus; the adult fare from Matsumoto to Hirayu is ¥3,000 (same source).
