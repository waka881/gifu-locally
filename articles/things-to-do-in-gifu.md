Gifu Prefecture sits just north of Nagoya and stretches up into the Japanese Alps. The south, around Gifu City, is an easy day trip from Nagoya; the north, Hida, is where Takayama, Shirakawa-go and the mountain hot springs are. This page sums up the main things to do with the key numbers, each taken from the official sources cited in our full guides.

## What is there to do in northern Gifu (Hida)?

**Takayama** is the base for the north: walk the Sanmachi merchant quarter, see a morning market (closed by noon), tour [Takayama Jinya](takayama-jinya.html), taste sake and eat [Hida beef](hida-beef-takayama.html). See [things to do in Takayama](things-to-do-in-takayama.html) and [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html).

**Shirakawa-go** is part of the UNESCO World Heritage site "Historic Villages of Shirakawa-go and Gokayama" ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/734/)). Most visitors spend 2–3 hours walking the village ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)). See [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html), [Gokayama vs Shirakawa-go](gokayama-vs-shirakawa-go.html) and, in winter, the [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html).

**Hot springs:** Gero Onsen is the walkable onsen town on the train line; Okuhida's villages are in the mountains above Takayama. Compare them in [Gero vs Okuhida](gero-vs-okuhida-onsen.html), and see [where to stay in Gero Onsen](where-to-stay-gero-onsen.html).

## What can you do in southern Gifu on a day trip from Nagoya?

Gifu City is the easiest: about 20 minutes by JR rapid train from Nagoya ([Gifu City in one day](gifu-city-one-day.html)). Start with a [morning service breakfast](gifu-morning-service.html), a local coffee-shop custom. Further out are Gujo Hachiman's castle town and [food-sample workshops](gujo-hachiman-food-samples.html), Seki's blades, Mino's paper, and the Nakasendo from Magome, which is in Nakatsugawa, Gifu ([Magome to Tsumago walk](magome-tsumago-walk.html)). For a ranked list by effort, see [Nagoya day trips](nagoya-day-trips.html). In winter, Gujo has the ski resorts closest to Nagoya: see [skiing near Nagoya](skiing-near-nagoya.html).

## FAQ

### What is Gifu Prefecture best known for?

Takayama's old town, the thatched farmhouses of Shirakawa-go (a UNESCO World Heritage site), Gero Onsen, Hida beef, Seki knives and Mino washi paper (sources in each linked guide).

### Is Gifu worth visiting?

Yes, if you want old towns, onsen and mountain scenery within reach of Nagoya: Gifu City is about 20 minutes away by train and Takayama about 2 hours 20 minutes by Limited Express Hida (sources in the linked guides).

### How do I get to Gifu from Nagoya?

JR trains reach Gifu City in about 20 minutes; the Limited Express Hida continues to Gero and Takayama; highway buses from Nagoya serve Gujo Hachiman, Mino and Shirakawa-go. See [Nagoya day trips](nagoya-day-trips.html).

### How many days should I spend in Gifu?

Three days covers Gero Onsen, Takayama and Shirakawa-go; see our [3-day Gifu itinerary](gifu-3-day-itinerary.html).
