Gero Onsen is a compact hot spring town on the Hida River, on the JR Takayama Line between Nagoya and Takayama. This guide uses the Gero Onsen Tourism Association's accommodation list to explain where the inns are and how to choose. We don't rank individual inns, and we haven't stayed at them.

## Where to stay in Gero Onsen at a glance

| Where | Good for | Examples from the official list |
|---|---|---|
| By the Hida River | The classic ryokan night with river views | Suimeikan, Kawakamiya Kasuitei, Ogawaya and Hyokin are described as beside or overlooking the river |
| Near Gero Station | Late arrivals, early trains, short stays | Mutsumikan ("the closest inn to the station"); Kissenkan Chikusuitei (5 min on foot); Minshuku Kappo Shoen (2 min) |
| On the hill above town | Quieter stays and views over town | Sasara, Kaiseki-yado Suihoen and Hotel Kusakabe Armeria are described as on the hill |
| Historic | A building with character | Yunoshimakan is a registered tangible cultural property |

*Source: [Gero Onsen Tourism Association, accommodation list](https://www.gero-spa.com/shukuhaku/) (Japanese), as of November 2026. Descriptions are the association's, not ours.*

## How many places are there to stay in Gero?

The tourism association lists 42 ryokan and hotels under "Ryokan and hotels around Gero Station" ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/shukuhaku/)). By address, 15 are in Yunoshima, 14 in Kohda, 9 in Mori and 4 elsewhere (our count from the list, November 2026). Central Japan's regional tourism site says Gero has more than 50 ryokan and hotels in all ([Centrip Japan](https://centrip-japan.com/spot/gerospa.html)).

## Riverside, station or hillside: which area should you choose?

- **Riverside** if the ryokan is the point of the trip. Several inns on the list are described as beside or overlooking the Hida River, and the Kur Garden Rotenburo public bath has an open-air bath by the river too ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_4.html)).
- **Near the station** if you are stopping for one night between Nagoya and Takayama. Sachi-no-yu public bath is about 4 minutes on foot from the station and Shirasagi-no-yu about 7 minutes ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_5.html); [Shirasagi-no-yu](https://www.gero-spa.com/spot/detail_3.html)), so you can bathe even if your hotel is simple.
- **On the hill** if you want quiet and views, and don't mind a climb or a pick-up. Check with the inn whether it offers a station shuttle before you book.

## Ryokan, hotel or minshuku?

| Type | What to expect | On the official list |
|---|---|---|
| Ryokan | Japanese-style rooms, often with dinner and breakfast included | Most of the list |
| Hotel | Western-style rooms; some have onsen baths | Several, including onsen business hotels |
| Minshuku | Small, family-run guesthouse | A few, such as Minshuku Kappo Shoen |
| Room only (sudomari) | No meals; eat in town | For example, Toraya |

*Source: [Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/shukuhaku/), as of November 2026.*

Features the association mentions include private open-air baths (Bosenkan) and free-flowing spring water, *gensen kakenagashi* (Kikuhan Ryokan). For what to ask about meals, dinner times and tattoos, see our [Gero Onsen guide](gero-onsen-guide.html).

## How do you book a ryokan in Gero?

On the association's list, 35 of the 42 places have a booking button that goes to the Gero Onsen Ryokan Cooperative site; the other seven, including some larger hotels, have only a map link, so book those directly or through a booking site ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/shukuhaku/)). ## FAQ

### How many ryokan are there in Gero Onsen?

The Gero Onsen Tourism Association lists 42 ryokan and hotels around Gero Station, and Centrip Japan says there are more than 50 in Gero ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/shukuhaku/); [Centrip Japan](https://centrip-japan.com/spot/gerospa.html)).

### Is it better to stay by the river or near the station in Gero?

Stay by the Hida River for a classic ryokan with views; stay near the station for late arrivals or early trains. Public baths are about 4–7 minutes' walk from the station either way (sources above).

### Can I stay in Gero without meals?

Yes. The association's list includes room-only (sudomari) places and hotels, and you can eat in town ([Gero Onsen Tourism Association](https://www.gero-spa.com/shukuhaku/)).

### How do I book a Gero ryokan in English?

Most inns on the official list book through the Japanese-language Gero Onsen Ryokan Cooperative site; for English, compare them on a booking site such as Klook.
