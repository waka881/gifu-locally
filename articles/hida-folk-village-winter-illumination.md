In winter, Hida Folk Village (Hida no Sato), the open-air museum of old farmhouses on a hill above Takayama, lights up its buildings after dark. This guide uses the Gifu Prefecture tourism site, the village's own pages and the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association.

## Hida Folk Village winter illumination at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Dates | January 9 – February 28, 2027 |
| Hours | 17:30–20:30 |
| Evening admission | ¥300 adults, ¥100 children |
| Daytime admission | ¥700 adults, ¥200 children (ages 6–15), 8:30–17:00 |
| From Takayama | About 10 minutes by bus from Takayama Bus Center, or 20–30 minutes on foot from JR Takayama Station |
| Parking | Available (free during illumination hours last season) |

*Sources: [Gifu Prefecture tourism, Hida no Sato winter light-up](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3323.html); [Hida Folk Village (English)](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [Hida Folk Village, 2026 winter illumination](https://hidanosato.com/event/winter_illumination2026/), as of November 2026. The village's own event page still shows the 2026 season, so check it again closer to your trip.*

## When is the 2027 winter illumination?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site lists January 9 (Saturday) to February 28 (Sunday), 2027, lit from 17:30 to 20:30, with an evening admission of ¥300 for adults and ¥100 for children. It describes snow-covered gassho-style houses lit up after dark ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3323.html), as of November 2026).

The village's own page for the previous season (January 10 – February 28, 2026) had the same hours and prices, and said that it ran every day, that four of the old houses were open inside during the illumination, that amazake and shiitake tea were served free, and that parking was free in the evening ([Hida Folk Village](https://hidanosato.com/event/winter_illumination2026/)). Those details are from 2026; the 2027 details had not been posted on the village's page when we checked.

## How do you get there from Takayama Station?

The village says it is about 10 minutes by bus from the Takayama Bus Center next to JR Takayama Station, or about 20–30 minutes on foot ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm)). The daytime Sarubobo Bus to Hida-no-Sato-Shita costs ¥210 each way ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html)). Check the last bus back at the bus center before you go, since the illumination runs until 20:30. For daytime hours and what's inside, see our [Hida Folk Village guide](hida-folk-village.html).

## Is it a good alternative to the Shirakawa-go light-up?

For many visitors, yes. The 2027 Shirakawa-go Light-Up is on only four evenings (January 11, 17, 24 and 31) and needs an advance reservation for a tour, an inn or parking ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/)); see our [Shirakawa-go light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html). Hida Folk Village is lit every evening for seven weeks, from a Takayama hotel by bus or on foot.

| | Hida Folk Village | Shirakawa-go Light-Up |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 dates | Jan 9 – Feb 28, nightly | Jan 11, 17, 24 and 31 |
| Hours | 17:30–20:30 | 17:30–19:30 |
| Cost | ¥300 evening admission | Tour, inn or parking booking required |
| From Takayama | About 10 minutes by bus | About 50 minutes by bus |

*Sources: as above; [Nohi Bus light-up tours](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/); [Nohi Bus, Shirakawa-go route](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026.*

The Shirakawa-go light-up is the [UNESCO World Heritage](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/734/) village itself, so if you can get a booking, it is the bigger sight. If you can't, or your dates don't match, the folk village is the easy way to see lit-up thatched houses.

## FAQ

### When is the Hida Folk Village winter illumination in 2027?

January 9 to February 28, 2027, from 17:30 to 20:30 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3323.html), as of November 2026).

### How much does it cost?

Evening admission is ¥300 for adults and ¥100 for children (same source).

### Do you need a reservation?

Neither the prefecture's listing nor the village's page mentions one, unlike the Shirakawa-go light-up, which needs a booked tour, inn or parking space.

### How do I get there from Takayama Station?

About 10 minutes by bus from Takayama Bus Center, or 20–30 minutes on foot ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm)).
