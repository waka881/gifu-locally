Takayama changes a lot through the year, from deep snow in January to festival crowds in April and October. This guide puts the official weather averages and event dates side by side, using the Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA), Hida Takayama Tourism, Takayama Jinya, the Gifu Prefecture tourism site and Nohi Bus.

## When is the Takayama Festival?

Twice a year, on fixed dates: the Spring Festival on April 14–15 and the Autumn Festival on October 9–10 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/4000105.html)). These are the busiest days in town, so book a room as soon as you know your dates. The timetable is in our [Takayama Festival guide](takayama-festival.html); on other days, see the floats at the [Floats Exhibition Hall](takayama-festival-floats-exhibition-hall.html).

## When is Takayama best for snow?

From December to March. The JMA averages give 66 cm of snowfall in December, 117 cm in January, 90 cm in February and 29 cm in March, with average daily lows below freezing in all four months ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)). Winter also brings the illuminations: the Nakabashi Bridge and Miyagawa River area is lit from December 1 to March 15 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000181.html)), and Hida Folk Village every evening from January 9 to February 28, 2027 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3323.html)). Month guides: [December](takayama-in-december.html), [January](takayama-in-january.html), [February](takayama-in-february.html) and [March](takayama-in-march.html).

## When is the weather nicest?

By the averages, May: 15.6°C, highs of 22.9°C and the most sunshine of the year, 189.2 hours. October (13.5°C) is also mild and drier than September. July is the wettest month (260.9 mm), and August is the warmest, with highs of 31.0°C ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)). The full figures are in [Takayama weather by month](takayama-weather-by-month.html).

## When can you reach Kamikochi and the Alpine Route?

Only from spring to autumn. In 2026 the Nohi Bus Kamikochi line ran from April 17 to November 15 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/kamikochi-line-en/)), and the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route was open from April 15 to November 30 ([Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route](https://alpen-route.com/en/transport/opening.html)). See [Kamikochi from Takayama](kamikochi-from-takayama.html), the [Alpine Route from Takayama](tateyama-kurobe-alpine-route-from-takayama.html) and [Takayama in spring](takayama-in-spring.html).

## FAQ

### What is the best month to visit Takayama?

It depends on your goal: April or October for the Takayama Festival (April 14–15, October 9–10), January or February for snow, and May for mild, sunny weather (sources above).

### When is the Takayama Festival?

April 14–15 (spring) and October 9–10 (autumn) every year ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/4000105.html)).

### Does it snow in Takayama?

Yes, about 305 cm a year on the JMA averages, mostly from December to February ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)).

### What is the rainiest month in Takayama?

July, with 260.9 mm on average, followed by September with 225.9 mm (same source).
