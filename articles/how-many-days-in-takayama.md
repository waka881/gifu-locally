Takayama is a small town, but it is also the base for Shirakawa-go, the Okuhida hot springs, Kamikochi and Gero Onsen. How many days you need depends on how many of those you want to add. This guide compares one, two and three days, using travel times and opening hours from the operators and official tourism sites.

## How many days in Takayama at a glance

| Stay | What fits | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 day (day trip from Nagoya) | Sanmachi old town, Takayama Jinya, and Hida Folk Village or a sake brewery | Travellers short on time |
| 2 days (1 night) | Day 1 in town; day 2 morning market and Shirakawa-go | Most first-time visitors |
| 3 days (2 nights) | The above plus Okuhida and the Shinhotaka Ropeway, Kamikochi (in season) or Gero Onsen | Hot springs, mountains, a slower pace |

*Our recommendation, based on the travel times and hours below.*

## What can you see in one day?

From Nagoya, the Limited Express Hida reaches Takayama in about 2 hours 20 minutes ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/), as of October 2026), so a day trip gives you a long afternoon. The main sights are close together:

| Sight | Getting there | Hours | Adult price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sanmachi old town | About 12 min on foot from JR Takayama Station | Streets always open | Free |
| Takayama Jinya | About 10 min on foot from the station | 8:45–17:00 (to 16:30 Nov–Mar) | ¥500 |
| Hida Folk Village | About 10 min by bus | 8:30–17:00 | ¥700 |

*Sources: [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html); [Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/); [Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm), as of October 2026.*

The morning markets close at noon, so a day trip from Nagoya usually misses them. See [Nagoya to Takayama](takayama-from-nagoya.html) for trains, buses and advance discounts.

## Why are two days the sweet spot?

One night lets you see the town on day 1 and add a morning market and Shirakawa-go on day 2:

- **Morning markets:** the Miyagawa and Jinya-mae markets open 7:00–12:00 from April to November and 8:00–12:00 from December to March ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html), as of October 2026).
- **Shirakawa-go:** about 50 minutes by bus from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center, ¥2,800 one way, with the last bus back at 17:30 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), checked October 2026). The Tourist Association estimates 2–3 hours to walk around the village ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)).

## FAQ

### Is one day enough for Takayama?

One day covers Sanmachi, Takayama Jinya and Hida Folk Village or a sake brewery, but not the morning markets (they close at noon) or Shirakawa-go. With about 2 hours 20 minutes each way from Nagoya by train, it is a long day ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/)).

### Is two days in Takayama enough?

For most first visits, yes: one day in town and one for a morning market and Shirakawa-go, which is about 50 minutes away by bus ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of October 2026).

### What can I do with three days in Takayama?

Add a day trip to Okuhida and the Shinhotaka Ropeway, to Kamikochi between mid-April and mid-November, or to Gero Onsen, about 45–60 minutes away by train (sources above).

### Can I visit Shirakawa-go and Takayama in one day?

Yes, but it is rushed. A guided tour from Nagoya is the simplest way; on your own, you need an early train or bus and reserved seats on the Shirakawa-go buses.
