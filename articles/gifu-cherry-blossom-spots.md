Gifu stretches from the Nobi plain to the Japan Alps, so cherry trees bloom at different times across the prefecture. This guide picks the spots that are easiest to combine with a trip, using the Gifu Prefecture tourism site's 2026 cherry blossom feature, which lists 59 spots.

## When do cherry blossoms bloom in Gifu?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site gives the usual peak at Gifu Park and the Nagara River bank as late March to early April, and several spots in the Chuno area, such as Yamakusu Park, as late March to early April too. Up in the mountains it is later: the site says cherry blossoms in Okuhida bloom about a month after the lowlands, from around late April, and Asahi in Takayama, known for weeping cherries, blooms in the second half of April ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_60.html), as of November 2026). So a trip in early April may catch the end of the season in Gifu City, and the start in Takayama.

## Where can you see cherry blossoms near Gifu City and Ogaki?

- **Gifu Park and the Nagara River bank:** about 400 Somei-yoshino, yamazakura and edo-higan trees along about 1 km, below Mt. Kinka ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_3361.html)). Gifu Park is 15 minutes by bus from JR Gifu Station; see [Is Gifu Castle open?](gifu-castle.html) for the bus and the Kinka Ropeway.
- **The Usuzumi cherry:** a single tree over 1,500 years old whose blossoms fade from white to a pale ink colour, reached by the Tarumi Railway from Ogaki. Full details are in our [Usuzumi cherry guide](usuzumi-cherry-blossom.html).
- **Yoro Park:** about 3,000 Somei-yoshino, higan-zakura and yae-zakura over about 2 km between Yoro Falls and Yoro Station on the Yoro Railway ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_3366.html)). The park is also home to the [Site of Reversible Destiny](yoro-park-site-of-reversible-destiny.html).
- **Sunomata:** about 800 trees over 3.7 km along the Saigawa river bank by Sunomata Ichiya Castle in Ogaki ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_3367.html)).

## Where can you see cherry blossoms in Takayama and Hida?

- **Shiroyama Park:** about 1,000 Somei-yoshino on the site of Takayama Castle ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_3382.html)).
- **Nakabashi Bridge:** the vermilion bridge over the Miyagawa in the old town has a spring light-up ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_60.html)). The [Takayama Festival](takayama-festival.html) on April 14–15 also starts its night procession near here.
- **Shokawa-zakura:** two giant Azuma-higan cherry trees, over 500 years old, beside Lake Miboro ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1249.html)).
- **Garyu-zakura:** a National Natural Monument about 1,100 years old, 20 m tall with branches spreading 30 m ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_1239.html)).
- **Gero:** trees line the platform at JR Gero Station, and a night light-up is held at Shirasagi Ryokuchi Park, 3 minutes on foot from the station ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_60.html)). See [Gero Onsen guide](gero-onsen-guide.html) for a ryokan night.

## FAQ

### When is cherry blossom season in Gifu?

Around Gifu City it usually peaks from late March to early April; in Okuhida in the mountains it starts around late April, about a month later ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_60.html), as of November 2026).

### Where can I see cherry blossoms in Takayama?

Shiroyama Park has about 1,000 Somei-yoshino on the old castle site, and Nakabashi Bridge in the old town is lit up in spring (same source).

### What is the most famous cherry tree in Gifu?

The Neodani Usuzumi-zakura in Motosu, over 1,500 years old and one of Japan's Three Great Cherry Trees ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_921.html)).

### Are there cherry blossoms near Gifu Station?

Yes. Gifu Park and the Nagara River bank, about 15 minutes by bus from JR Gifu Station, have about 400 trees along about 1 km ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_3361.html); [Gifu CVB](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php)).
