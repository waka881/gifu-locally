Most visitors to Gifu Prefecture start from Nagoya, but Gifu City is only one stop up the line. This guide compares the two as a base, using Visit Gifu, JR timetables, Nohi Bus, Gifu Bus and the Gifu Prefecture tourism site.

## Nagoya or Gifu City at a glance

| | Nagoya | Gifu City |
|---|---|---|
| Between the two | About 20 min by JR | About 20 min by JR |
| Shinkansen | Yes (Tokaido Shinkansen) | No; change at Nagoya |
| Limited Express Hida to Takayama | About 2 h 20 min | About 2 h (e.g. 10:12 → 12:14) |
| Bus to Takayama | Nagoya–Takayama line | Meitetsu Gifu, about 2 h, ¥2,900, via Gujo Hachiman IC |
| Bus to Shirakawa-go | Meitetsu Bus Center, about 2 h 30 min, ¥3,600–¥4,700 by date | Via Nagoya or Takayama |
| Bus to Gujo Hachiman | JR Nagoya Station, about 1 h 20 min | Meitetsu Gifu bus to Gujo Hachiman IC |
| Evenings | A big city | Tamamiya (over 400 restaurants and bars), Nagaragawa Onsen |

## When is Nagoya the better base?

When you arrive or leave by Shinkansen, or want the highway buses that start there. The Nagoya–Shirakawa-go bus leaves from the Meitetsu Bus Center, about 2 hours 30 minutes to the village, with fares of ¥3,600–¥4,700 depending on the date; see [Shirakawa-go from Nagoya](shirakawa-go-from-nagoya.html). The Gujo Hachiman bus leaves from JR Nagoya Station and takes about 1 hour 20 minutes ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_370.html)); see the [Gujo Hachiman day trip](gujo-hachiman-day-trip.html). The guided day tours we compare also leave from Nagoya Station; see [Shirakawa-go tours from Nagoya](shirakawa-go-tours-from-nagoya.html).

## When is Gifu City the better base?

When you want a smaller city with the same trains north. JR Nagoya is around 20 minutes away ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya/)), and the Limited Express Hida stops at Gifu: on JR West's timetable, one leaves Gifu at 10:12 and reaches Takayama at 12:14 ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027), timetable for October 27, 2026). From Meitetsu Gifu, a reserved express bus runs to Takayama in about 2 hours for ¥2,900, stopping at Gujo Hachiman IC ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/)); see [Gifu to Takayama](gifu-to-takayama.html).

In the evening, JR Central describes the Tamamiya district by JR Gifu Station as having over 400 restaurants and bars ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/onlinebooking/contents/hello_gifu/)), and Nagaragawa Onsen by the river has seven member ryokan and hotels with hot-spring water ([Gifu Nagaragawa Onsen Hotel Cooperative](https://nagaragawa.org/en/about)). See [where to stay in Gifu City](where-to-stay-gifu-city.html) and [Gifu City in one day](gifu-city-one-day.html).

## Should you just stay in Takayama instead?

If Takayama and Shirakawa-go are the main goal, yes. Both are far from Nagoya and Gifu (about 2 hours or more), and Takayama's morning markets close at noon, so a night there saves a long return trip. See [where to stay for Shirakawa-go](where-to-stay-for-shirakawa-go.html) and [how many days in Takayama](how-many-days-in-takayama.html).

## FAQ

### How far is Gifu City from Nagoya?

Around 20 minutes by JR train from JR Nagoya Station to JR Gifu Station ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/how-to-get-to/nagoya/)).

### Does the Limited Express Hida stop at Gifu?

Yes. For example, a Hida leaves Gifu at 10:12 and reaches Takayama at 12:14 ([JR West](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/76841?date=20261027), October 2026 timetable).

### Is there a bus from Gifu City to Takayama?

Yes, a reserved express bus from Meitetsu Gifu (stop No. 6), about 2 hours for ¥2,900, via Gujo Hachiman IC ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/), as of November 2026).

### Where does the Shirakawa-go bus from Nagoya leave from?

The Meitetsu Bus Center, about a 5-minute walk from Nagoya Station (see [Shirakawa-go from Nagoya](shirakawa-go-from-nagoya.html)).
