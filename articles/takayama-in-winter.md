Winter is a good time to see Takayama: the old town is quieter than in autumn, the mountains are white, and evenings bring illuminations along the river and at the folk village. This guide covers what changes in winter, the main seasonal events, and how to travel safely in snow, using information from Hida Takayama Tourism, the attractions themselves and the bus operators.

## Takayama in winter at a glance

| | Winter details |
|---|---|
| Morning markets | Open daily, 8:00–12:00 from December to March |
| Takayama Jinya | 8:45–16:30 from November to March (ticket window closes 30 min earlier) |
| Hida Folk Village | 8:30–17:00; winter illumination in January–February |
| Evening illumination | Nakabashi and Miyagawa River area, December to mid-March |
| Shirakawa-go light-up | January 11, 17, 24 and 31, 2027 |

## What is open in Takayama in winter?

The main sights stay open, some with shorter hours:

- **Morning markets:** both the Miyagawa and Jinya-mae markets open every day, 8:00–12:00 from December to March, an hour later than the rest of the year ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html), as of October 2026).
- **Takayama Jinya:** 8:45–16:30 from November to March, with the ticket window closing 30 minutes earlier; admission ¥500 ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/), as of October 2026).
- **Hida Folk Village:** 8:30–17:00, ¥700 for adults ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm), as of October 2026).
- **Sanmachi old town:** the streets are always open; it is a 12-minute walk from JR Takayama Station ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)).

Days are short, so start early and save the illuminations for the evening. For a two-day plan, see our [Takayama 2-day itinerary](takayama-2-day-itinerary.html).

## Can you add the Shirakawa-go light-up?

The 41st Shirakawa-go Light-Up is on **January 11, 17, 24 and 31, 2027** ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/), as of October 2026). It is about 50 minutes from Takayama by express bus ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)), but the 2026 event was reservation-only, and on normal nights day visitors are not admitted to the village after dark ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/106/)). Read our [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html) and [Shirakawa-go in winter](shirakawa-go-in-winter.html) before you plan. Many visitors go on an organised tour that handles the reservations.

## How do you get to Takayama in winter?

- **Train:** the Limited Express Hida runs from JR Nagoya Station to Takayama in approximately 2 hours 20 minutes ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/), as of October 2026).
- **Highway bus:** the Nohi Bus from Nagoya takes 2 hours 40 minutes and costs ¥3,600 one way, or ¥3,300 with the web discount ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/), as of October 2026). Highway buses can be delayed by snow.
- **Driving:** Hida Takayama Tourism asks visitors not to drive rental cars with normal tyres to Takayama in winter ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000219.html)).

More options are in [Nagoya to Takayama](takayama-from-nagoya.html).

## Tips for travelling in snow

- **Leave buffers.** On the Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa bus line, Nohi Bus warns that winter delays of two hours or more can occur ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Avoid tight connections to trains or flights.
- **Don't count on a quick taxi.** Hida Takayama Tourism says taxis are busiest on weekends, at year end and on snowy days, and you sometimes have to wait more than an hour ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000219.html)).
- **Store big bags.** At the Nohi Bus Center next to JR Takayama Station, bags that don't fit in a coin locker can be checked for ¥500 per day (same source).
- **Stay near the station.** It keeps you close to trains and the bus center when roads are snowy. See [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html).

## FAQ

### Is Takayama worth visiting in winter?

Yes, if you want snowy scenery and fewer crowds. The main sights stay open with shorter hours, and the evening illuminations along the Miyagawa River and at Hida Folk Village are winter-only ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000181.html)).

### What time do the Takayama morning markets open in winter?

From December to March, the Miyagawa and Jinya-mae morning markets open 8:00–12:00 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html), as of October 2026).

### Can I drive to Takayama in winter?

Hida Takayama Tourism asks visitors not to drive rental cars with normal tyres to Takayama in winter. The Limited Express Hida from Nagoya is the simpler option ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000219.html)).

### When is the Takayama winter illumination?

Hida Takayama Tourism listed the Winter Illumination in the Nakabashi and Miyagawa River area for December 1 to March 15 in the 2025–26 season, 16:30–21:30. Check the [official event calendar](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000181.html) for this winter's dates.
