Nagoya has no mountains of its own, but the snow is close: the Gujo area of Gifu, north of Gujo Hachiman, has a cluster of ski resorts within a day trip. This guide uses the Gujo Tourism Federation's resort data and the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO).

*Source: [Gujo Tourism Federation (TABITABI Gujo)](https://en.tabitabigujo.com/appeal/ski/?c=th), as of October 2026. Percentages are as published.*

The Federation gives the drive from Nagoya as about 1.5 hours, and notes that the Snowboard World Cup has been held in Gujo twice (same source). JNTO says all the Gujo resorts can be reached by bus from Nagoya and Gifu, and describes Dynaland as about two hours from Nagoya and very busy on weekends ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/tokai/gifu/gifu-ski-resorts)).

## Which Gujo ski resort should you choose?

- **First time on skis:** the small resorts with mostly beginner slopes, such as Outdoor In Motai or Gujo Vacation Mura (80% beginner), or Hirugano Kogen, which the Federation says is 5 minutes from the Hirugano-kogen SA smart interchange (same source).
- **Snowboarders:** JNTO says Takasu's main draw is its snowboard park with rails, pipes and walls ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/tokai/gifu/gifu-ski-resorts)).
- **Long runs:** Meiho (5,000 m) and Takasu Snow Park (4,900 m) have the longest runs on the Federation's list.
- **An onsen after skiing:** JNTO lists an onsen at Washigatake (same source).
- **Easy access without a car:** Dynaland, because day packages with a bus and lift ticket leave from Nagoya (below).

## What should you know before you go?

- **Rental sizes:** JNTO notes that some resorts stock boots only up to 28 or 29 cm, so check sizes in advance if you have large feet (same source).
- **Season and opening dates:** neither source gives dates; check each resort's own website before you book.
- **Weekends are busy:** JNTO says Dynaland gets very crowded on weekends (same source).
- **Add Gujo Hachiman:** the old castle town is the gateway to the Gujo resorts; see [Gujo Hachiman day trip](gujo-hachiman-day-trip.html) and our [Nagoya day trips](nagoya-day-trips.html).

## FAQ

### What is the closest ski resort to Nagoya?

The nearest cluster is in Gujo, Gifu: the Gujo Tourism Federation lists 10 resorts about 1.5 hours from Nagoya by car, and JNTO says Dynaland is about two hours away ([Gujo Tourism Federation](https://en.tabitabigujo.com/appeal/ski/?c=th); [JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/tokai/gifu/gifu-ski-resorts)).

### Can I go skiing from Nagoya without a car?

Yes. JNTO says the Gujo resorts are all reachable by bus from Nagoya and Gifu, and Klook sells a Dynaland day package with round-trip bus and lift ticket from Nagoya Station (sources above).

### Which Gujo ski resort is best for beginners?

On the Federation's figures, Outdoor In Motai and Gujo Vacation Mura are 80% beginner slopes; Dynaland (49%) and Takasu Snow Park (47%) also have many beginner trails ([Gujo Tourism Federation](https://en.tabitabigujo.com/appeal/ski/?c=th)).

### Are there ski resorts near Takayama?

Yes, including Hounokidaira and Hirayu Onsen; JNTO says Hirayu's small resort is a short walk from the bus terminal ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/tokai/gifu/gifu-ski-resorts)).
