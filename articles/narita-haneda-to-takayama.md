Many international flights to Japan land at Narita or Haneda, and neither has a direct link to Takayama. You first get into Tokyo, then take the train via Nagoya or the highway bus from Shinjuku. This guide uses Hida Takayama Tourism, Nohi Bus and JR West.

## Narita or Haneda to Takayama at a glance

| Route | Airport leg | Then | Adult fare | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Train via Nagoya | Haneda → Tokyo Station 35 min (bus); Narita → Tokyo Station 53 min (Narita Express) | Nozomi to Nagoya 100 min + Hida 140 min | Check JR | Fastest |
| Train via Toyama | Same as above | Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama 2 h 8 min + Hida 90 min | Check JR | Going on to Shirakawa-go or Kanazawa |
| Highway bus from Shinjuku | Haneda → Busta Shinjuku 35 min (bus); Narita → Shinjuku 90 min (Narita Express) | Keio/Nohi bus about 5 h 30 min | ¥8,000 (daytime) | No train changes; one seat all the way |

## What is the fastest way from Haneda or Narita to Takayama?

Hida Takayama Tourism lists the airport shuttle bus from Haneda to JR Tokyo Station (35 minutes) or the Narita Express from Narita to Tokyo Station (53 minutes), then the Nozomi Shinkansen to Nagoya (100 minutes) and the Limited Express Hida to Takayama (140 minutes) ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)). That is about 4 hours of riding from Tokyo Station, plus the airport leg and two changes.

For the Nagoya leg and Hida tickets, see [Takayama from Nagoya](takayama-from-nagoya.html); for all Tokyo options, see [Tokyo to Takayama](tokyo-to-takayama.html).

## Can you take a bus from the airport to Takayama?

Not directly. Get to Shinjuku first: Hida Takayama Tourism lists the airport shuttle bus from Haneda to Busta Shinjuku at 35 minutes and the Narita Express from Narita at 90 minutes (same source). From the Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal, Keio and Nohi Bus run reserved buses to Takayama:

| Departs Shinjuku | Arrives Takayama Bus Terminal |
|---|---|
| 7:05 | 12:50 |
| 8:15 | 14:00 |
| 9:15 | 15:15 |
| 11:05 | 16:50 |
| 16:05 | 21:50 |

*Source: [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/shinjuku_en/), as of November 2026. Daytime fare ¥8,000 from Shinjuku (¥8,500 from Bus Terminal Tokyo Yaesu, which has an 8:30 departure); an overnight bus ran on set dates in 2026.*

All seats are reserved and bookings open one month ahead, online through a booking site or by phone (same source). The buses stop at Hirayu Onsen on the way, about an hour before Takayama, which suits a night in [Okuhida](where-to-stay-okuhida.html).

## Should you stay a night in Tokyo first?

If you land in the afternoon or evening, usually yes. The last daytime bus leaves Shinjuku at 16:05 and arrives at 21:50 (same source), and the train route still takes about 4 hours from Tokyo Station once you are in the city. A night in Tokyo lets you take a morning train or bus and arrive in Takayama by early afternoon. Flying into Osaka instead? See [Kansai Airport to Takayama](kansai-airport-to-takayama.html). Closest of all is Nagoya's airport: see [Centrair Airport to Gifu](centrair-airport-to-gifu.html).

## Is a rail pass worth it from Tokyo?

The regional Takayama-Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass does not cover Tokyo, so the Shinkansen from Tokyo is extra; the 7-day Japan Rail Pass covers more but costs ¥50,000 online. ## FAQ

### How long does it take from Narita Airport to Takayama?

About 53 minutes on the Narita Express to Tokyo Station, then about 4 hours by Shinkansen and Limited Express Hida via Nagoya, plus connections ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)).

### How do I get from Haneda to Takayama?

Take the airport bus to Tokyo Station (35 minutes) for the train via Nagoya, or to Busta Shinjuku (35 minutes) for the Keio/Nohi highway bus to Takayama (same source; [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/shinjuku_en/)).

### Is there a direct bus from Narita or Haneda to Takayama?

Not on the official routes. The buses to Takayama leave from Shinjuku, and one from Bus Terminal Tokyo Yaesu ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/shinjuku_en/), as of November 2026).

### Can I reach Takayama on the day I land?

If you land in the morning, yes; the last daytime bus leaves Shinjuku at 16:05. With a later landing, a night in Tokyo is easier (same source).
