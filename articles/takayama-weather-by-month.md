## How cold is Takayama in winter?

Cold. January averages −1.2°C and February −0.6°C, with average daily lows of about −5°C and highs of only 3–5°C. December averages 1.6°C. The averages give 66 cm of snowfall in December, 117 cm in January and 90 cm in February, about 305 cm over the year ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)). For what is open and how to get around in snow, see [Takayama in winter](takayama-in-winter.html) and the [Gifu winter guide](gifu-winter-guide.html).

## Does it snow in Takayama in November or April?

A little. The averages show 2 cm of snowfall in November and 3 cm in April, against 29 cm in March. November averages 7.1°C, with lows of 2.9°C; April averages 9.7°C, with lows of 3.4°C (same source). For the [Spring Takayama Festival](takayama-festival.html) on April 14–15, pack for cool evenings; see [Takayama in spring](takayama-in-spring.html).

## How hot and rainy is Takayama in summer?

August is the warmest month, averaging 24.4°C with daily highs of 31.0°C. Summer is also the wet season: July averages 260.9 mm of rain, September 225.9 mm and August 197.9 mm, the three wettest months of the year (same source). Nights stay mild, with August lows around 20°C.

## What should you pack?

- **December to March:** a warm coat, gloves and shoes with grip for icy streets; daily lows are below freezing from December to March.
- **April and November:** layers and a warm jacket for evenings, when lows are around 3°C.
- **June to September:** an umbrella or rain jacket, and light clothes for highs of 26–31°C.

## FAQ

### Does Takayama get snow?

Yes. The Japan Meteorological Agency's averages give about 305 cm of snowfall a year, most of it from December to February ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)).

### What is the coldest month in Takayama?

January, averaging −1.2°C with daily lows of −4.8°C; February's lows are similar, at −4.9°C (same source).

### What is the weather like in Takayama in December?

December averages 1.6°C, with highs of 6.2°C and lows of −1.7°C, and 66 cm of snowfall over the month (same source).

### What is the rainiest month in Takayama?

July, with 260.9 mm on average (same source).
