Seki, a quiet city about 40 minutes by bus from Gifu Station, has been making blades for more than 700 years and is often counted among the world's great cutlery towns ([Go Central Japan](https://shoryudo.go-centraljapan.jp/en/sekiknifemuseum/)). If you want a Japanese kitchen knife, or want to watch a swordsmith at work, this is the place.

## Important for 2026–27: the sword museum is under renovation

The **Seki Kaji Denshokan** (Seki Swordsmith Museum) is closed for renovation from September 7, 2026 to March 31, 2027. The **forging workshop stays open** during this period, and the first-floor exhibition room is expected to reopen first. Dates may change ([Gifu tourism, Japanese](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/1179/), as of October 2026).

## Watch traditional sword forging

Swordsmiths give public demonstrations of traditional Japanese sword forging at the Seki Kaji Denshokan:

- **When:** set Sundays, usually the first of the month, plus the New Year forging ceremony on January 2 and the October Blade Festival. Dates listed by Seki City: November 1 and December 6, 2026; January 2 (morning only), February 7 and March 7, 2027
- **Times:** forging 11:00–12:00 and 13:30–14:30 on regular days; doors open 30 minutes earlier. Tickets for the afternoon session go on sale at 11:00 on the day
- **Cost:** adults ¥300, high school students ¥200, younger students ¥100
- **Wear dark clothes:** the forge produces soot
- **Next dates:** Sunday, November 1 and Sunday, December 6, 2026. Because of the renovation, these two days show sword forging only; the sword polishing and fittings demonstrations are not held ([Seki City, Japanese](https://www.city.seki.lg.jp/kanko/0000017993.html), as of October 2026)

([Gifu tourism, Japanese](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_4962.html), as of October 2026. Times may change, so check before you go, especially while the museum is being renovated.)

**Getting there:** 3-minute walk from Seki Terrace-mae Station on the Nagaragawa Railway.

## Buy a knife: Seki Hamono Museum (Cutler SANSYU)

A shop and museum where you can buy the in-house Seki Yoshihide kitchen knives and scissors, and watch traditional swordsmithing at the on-site forge. Trying it yourself costs extra and needs a reservation ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/see-do/cutler-sansyu-seki-hamono-museum/), as of October 2026). Entry is free, and it's open 9:00 to 16:30 every day, with irregular closures during the New Year holidays (January 1–3) ([Seki Hamono Museum, official](https://www.hamonoyasan.com/sekihamonomuseum/), as of October 2026).

**Getting there:** From JR Gifu Station, take Gifu Bus B81 (Gifu Seki Line, toward Seki Higashiyama) from bus zone 14 to the **Oyana** stop (about 35–40 minutes) ([Gifu Bus timetable, Japanese PDF](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/2610_gifu-seki.pdf), as of October 2026). From the stop it's about a 14-minute walk ([Seki Hamono Museum access guide, PDF](https://www.hamonoyasan.com/data/public-transport-en.pdf)).

## Tips for buying a knife in Japan

- **Carry it home in checked baggage.** Narita Airport says you basically cannot carry any knives on board, but you can put them in checked baggage, with possible limits on blade length ([Narita Airport](https://www.narita-airport.jp/en/airportguide/security/master-sheet/), as of October 2026).
- **Ask about the steel.** Stainless steel is easier to care for; carbon steel holds a sharper edge but can rust.

## FAQ

### How do I get to Seki from Gifu Station?

From JR Gifu Station, take Gifu Bus B81 (Gifu Seki Line, toward Seki Higashiyama) from bus zone 14 to the Oyana stop, which takes about 35–40 minutes ([Gifu Bus timetable, Japanese PDF](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/2610_gifu-seki.pdf), as of October 2026). The Seki Hamono Museum is about a 14-minute walk from the stop ([Seki Hamono Museum access guide, PDF](https://www.hamonoyasan.com/data/public-transport-en.pdf)).

### Is the Seki Swordsmith Museum open in 2026?

The Seki Kaji Denshokan is closed for renovation from September 7, 2026 to March 31, 2027, but the forging workshop stays open during this period. Dates may change ([Gifu tourism, Japanese](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/1179/), as of October 2026).

### When can I watch sword forging in Seki?

Demonstrations are held on set Sundays, usually the first of the month, plus January 2 and the October Blade Festival, with forging at 11:00–12:00 and 13:30–14:30 on regular days (¥300 for adults). The next dates are Sunday, November 1 and Sunday, December 6, 2026, which show sword forging only because of the renovation ([Seki City, Japanese](https://www.city.seki.lg.jp/kanko/0000017993.html), as of October 2026).

### Where can I buy a Japanese kitchen knife in Seki?

The Seki Hamono Museum (Cutler SANSYU) sells its in-house Seki Yoshihide kitchen knives and scissors. Entry is free, and it's open 9:00 to 16:30 every day, with irregular closures during the New Year holidays (January 1–3) ([Seki Hamono Museum, official](https://www.hamonoyasan.com/sekihamonomuseum/), as of October 2026).

### Can I take a knife bought in Japan on the plane?

Narita Airport says you basically cannot carry knives on board, but you can put them in checked baggage, with possible limits on blade length ([Narita Airport](https://www.narita-airport.jp/en/airportguide/security/master-sheet/)).
