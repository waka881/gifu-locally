## Which tour should you choose?

- **You want Hida beef for lunch without searching for a restaurant:** the [Hida beef lunch tour](https://www.klook.com/activity/14117-takayama-shirakawa-go-day-trip-nagoya/). You choose the lunch when booking (Hida beef, Kobe wagyu, Hida pork, vegetarian oden or no meal). Its page says that if roads close, Shirakawa-go may be replaced with Gujo Hachiman and the lunch refunded.
- **You want the most free time in each place:** the [Takayama & Shirakawa-go tour](https://www.klook.com/activity/15800-takayama-shirakawa-go-day-trip-nagoya/) lists about 2 hours of free time in each. It is the most-booked of the three, has the widest choice of languages, and also sells one-way options (for example Nagoya to Takayama) if you want to stay on.
- **You want to see a third town:** the [Gujo Hachiman version](https://www.klook.com/activity/121048-shirakawa-go-and-gujo-hachiman-and-hida-day-tour-from-nagoya/) adds Gujo Hachiman, a castle town known for its waterways, for an 11-hour day. Expect less time at each stop.

Two hours is enough for the main sights: the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association estimates 2–3 hours to walk around the village ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026). See [Is Shirakawa-go worth visiting?](is-shirakawa-go-worth-visiting.html) for what to prioritise.

*Sources: [Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html); [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/); tour prices from Klook, as of October 2026.*

**Book a tour** if you want Takayama and Shirakawa-go in one day from Nagoya: by public transport that means a chain of reserved buses and a long train ride back. **Take the bus** if you only want Shirakawa-go, want more than two hours there, or are travelling on to Takayama or Kanazawa. ## Tours from Takayama instead

If you are staying in Takayama, a shorter tour works better. Klook lists a [Shirakawa-go and Gokayama tour from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/90970-historic-villages-shirakawa-go-gokayama-join-bus-trip-takayama/), which adds Gokayama, a second thatched village; its page lists about 2 hours 40 minutes in Shirakawa-go and a Japanese-speaking host. Or take the bus, about 50 minutes each way; see [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html).

## Winter tours

In winter, Nohi Bus warns of delays of two hours or more on the Shirakawa-go line because of snow and traffic ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)), and tour times can change too. The 2027 light-up evenings are January 11, 17, 24 and 31; Klook also lists [light-up tours from Nagoya](https://www.klook.com/activity/175324-shirakawa-go-light-up-2026-day-tour-nagoya-departure-via-takayama/), which sell out early. ## FAQ

### Can you do Shirakawa-go as a day trip from Nagoya?

Yes. The highway bus takes about 2 hours 50 minutes from Nagoya Station ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/), as of October 2026), and day tours from Nagoya run about 10–11 hours including Takayama.

### How much is a Shirakawa-go tour from Nagoya?

Day tours on Klook listed prices from about US$45–50 per person in October 2026, with lunch extra on most tours. Prices change daily, so check the tour page for your date.

### Is a tour better than the bus to Shirakawa-go?

A tour is easier if you want both Takayama and Shirakawa-go in one day. The bus (¥3,600–¥4,700 one way, [Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html)) is better if you want more time in the village or are travelling on.

### How long do tours spend in Shirakawa-go?

About 2 hours on the Takayama & Shirakawa-go tour, according to its Klook page. The Tourist Association suggests 2–3 hours to walk around the village.
