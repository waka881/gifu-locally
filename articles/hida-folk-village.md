Hida Folk Village is a hillside open-air museum just outside central Takayama, with farmhouses moved from across the Hida region. This guide covers tickets, the bus and how it compares with Shirakawa-go, using the village's own site, Hida Takayama Tourism and Nohi Bus.

## Hida Folk Village at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| What it is | Open-air village of 30+ traditional buildings: thatched and shingled houses, shrines and sheds, on about 99,000 m² |
| Hours | 8:30–17:00, open all year |
| Admission | Adults ¥700; ages 6–15 ¥200 (card accepted) |
| Getting there | Sarubobo Bus from Takayama Nohi Bus Center to "Hida-no-Sato-Shita", about 10 min, ¥210 one way; or 20–30 min on foot |
| Combination ticket | Nohi Bus: round-trip bus + admission, ¥1,000 (adults) |
| Craft demonstrations | 10:00–15:00 (may change) |

*Sources: [Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html); [Nohi Bus economical tickets](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/english/ticket/), as of October 2026.*

## What is there to see?

The village has more than 30 buildings that recreate how Hida's mountain farming villages looked, including houses designated as National Important Folk Cultural Assets. You can go inside the houses, which display everyday tools and household items ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html)).

- **Craft demonstrations:** artisans work in several houses on Hida quilting, straw crafts, wood carving, weaving, ceramics and roof-shingle making, 10:00–15:00 (same sources).
- **Goami Pond** and a free telescope for mountain views ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm)).
- **Hands-on crafts nearby:** the Hida Takayama Craft Experience Center (10:00–15:30, closed Thursdays) offers over 10 activities, with a discount if you show your Folk Village ticket (same source).

*Sources: [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html); [Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/english/ticket/), as of October 2026.*

The bus leaves from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center next to JR Takayama Station; get off at "Hida-no-Sato-Shita". If you plan to ride the city buses several times, Nohi Bus also sells a Takayama City Two-Day Open Ticket for ¥1,000, valid on all Takayama city route buses ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/english/ticket/)).

| | Hida Folk Village | Shirakawa-go |

| Entry | ¥700 | Some houses charge (e.g. Wada House ¥400) |
| Time needed | Not stated; about 1–2 hours (our estimate) | 2–3 hours suggested |

*Sources: as above; [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/); [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/); [Wada House](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/active/13/), as of October 2026.*

If you have time for both, do both: they show different things. If you only have one day in Takayama and won't reach Shirakawa-go, the Folk Village is the easy way to see thatched houses. ## Visiting in winter

The village is open all year, and lists illuminations in autumn, at Christmas and in January–February among its annual events ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm)). Hida Takayama Tourism's event calendar listed the 2026 winter illumination for January 10 to February 28, 2026 ([Hida Takayama event calendar](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000180.html)); check for the 2027 dates. ## FAQ

### How much is Hida Folk Village?

¥700 for adults and ¥200 for ages 6–15. Nohi Bus's combination ticket with the round-trip bus costs ¥1,000 for adults ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/english/ticket/), as of October 2026).

### How do I get to Hida Folk Village from Takayama Station?

Take the Sarubobo Bus from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center to "Hida-no-Sato-Shita", about 10 minutes, ¥210 one way, or walk 20–30 minutes ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000165.html)).

### What are Hida Folk Village's opening hours?

8:30–17:00, open all year ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm)).

### Is Hida Folk Village worth it if I'm going to Shirakawa-go?

Both are worth it, as one is a museum of houses moved from around Hida and the other a living World Heritage village. If you can only do one, Shirakawa-go is the bigger trip; the Folk Village is the easy option from Takayama.
