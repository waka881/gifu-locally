The year-end holidays (roughly December 29 to January 3) are a popular time to travel in Japan, and some sights close. This guide lists what the official pages say is open or closed in and around Takayama, as of November 2026.

## What's open in Takayama over New Year?

| Sight | Year-end and New Year | Winter hours | Adult price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Takayama Jinya | **Closed December 29–January 3** | 8:45–16:30 (Nov–Mar) | ¥500 |
| Miyagawa and Jinya-mae morning markets | Listed as open all year | 8:00–12:00 (Dec–Mar) | Free |
| Takayama Festival Floats Exhibition Hall | Listed as open daily; call to confirm holiday hours | 9:00–16:30 (Dec–Feb) | ¥900 |
| Hida Folk Village | No closed days listed | 8:30–17:00 | ¥700 |
| Hida Furukawa Festival Hall | **Closed December 28–January 2** | 9:00–16:30 (Dec–Feb) | ¥500 |

*Sources: [Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/); [Hida Takayama Tourism, morning markets](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html); [Floats Exhibition Hall](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000170.html); [Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [JR West, Hida Furukawa Festival Hall](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/ticket/hokuriku-arch-pass/Explore_Japan/spot/gifu/hida-furukawa-festival-hall/index.html), as of November 2026.*

**Takayama Jinya** is the main closure: the former government office shuts for the year-end and New Year holidays from December 29 to January 3 ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)). If it's on your list, visit on December 28 or from January 4.

**The morning markets** are listed as open all year, from 8:00 to noon from December to March, but Hida Takayama Tourism notes the number of stalls can drop to about 10 in winter or rain ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)). See our [morning market guide](takayama-morning-market.html).

**The Floats Exhibition Hall** is a good indoor option. Hida Takayama Tourism lists it as open daily, but suggests calling +81-577-32-5100 to confirm holiday hours ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000170.html)). See our [Takayama Festival guide](takayama-festival.html).

**Hida Folk Village** lists winter illuminations in January and February among its events; the 2026 winter illumination ran from January 10 to February 28 ([Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm); [Hida Takayama event calendar](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000180.html)). See our [Hida Folk Village guide](hida-folk-village.html).

## Do buses run normally over New Year?

Not all of them. On the Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa line, Nohi Bus says some services do not run from December 29 to January 3 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)), and it warns that winter delays of two hours or more can occur (same source). Check the timetable for your exact date and reserve seats.

## Where should you stay over New Year?

Book as early as you can, and stay near JR Takayama Station so you can walk to the bus center if snow slows things down. Takayama adds an accommodation tax of ¥100–¥300 per person per night ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4001680.html)). ## FAQ

### Is Takayama Jinya open on New Year's Day?

No. Takayama Jinya is closed from December 29 to January 3 ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/), as of November 2026).

### Are the Takayama morning markets open at New Year?

Hida Takayama Tourism lists both markets as open all year, from 8:00 to noon in winter, with fewer stalls in winter or rain ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)).

### Do the buses to Shirakawa-go run over New Year?

Yes, but Nohi Bus says some services do not run from December 29 to January 3, so check your date and reserve ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).

### What is open in Takayama on January 1?

Hida Takayama Tourism lists the morning markets as open all year and the Floats Exhibition Hall as open daily (call to confirm holiday hours); Takayama Jinya is closed (sources above).
