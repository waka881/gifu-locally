## What is the Site of Reversible Destiny?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site describes it as a theme park that realised a project by the contemporary artist Shusaku Arakawa and his partner, the poet Madeline Gins. It has nine pavilions and paths set in a bowl-shaped landscape, with maps of the Japanese islands built at different scales ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_902.html)). Yoro Park gives the opening year as 1995 and the area as about 18,000 m² ([Yoro Park](https://www.yoro-park.com/facility-map/hantenchi/)).

The ground is uneven on purpose. The park asks visitors to watch out for hollows and slippery slopes and to keep children in sight, and strollers and wheelchairs can be used only in limited areas; ask at the museum shop. Coin lockers cost ¥100 and the coin isn't returned (same source). Wear shoes you can scramble in.

## How much is it and when is it open?

¥850 for adults, ¥550 for high school students and ¥350 for elementary and junior high students; children under school age are free. It is open 9:00–17:00 with last entry at 16:30, and closed on Tuesdays (the next day if Tuesday is a holiday) and from December 29 to January 3; it may also close in bad weather ([Yoro Park](https://www.yoro-park.com/guide/information/), as of November 2026).

## How do you get there?

Take the Yoro Railway to Yoro Station; the park is about 10 minutes on foot from there ([Yoro Park](https://www.yoro-park.com/guide/access/)). The Gifu Prefecture tourism site gives the route as JR Ogaki Station, then the Yoro Railway to Yoro ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_902.html)). By car it is about 20 minutes from Ogaki IC, and about an hour from Nagoya by expressway; park parking is free, and lot No. 2 is closest to the site ([Yoro Park](https://www.yoro-park.com/guide/access/)).

## FAQ

### How much is the Site of Reversible Destiny?

¥850 for adults, ¥550 for high school students and ¥350 for elementary and junior high students; children under school age are free ([Yoro Park](https://www.yoro-park.com/guide/information/), as of November 2026).

### What are the opening hours?

9:00–17:00, with last entry at 16:30 (same source).

### Is it open on Tuesdays?

No. It closes every Tuesday (the next day if Tuesday is a public holiday) and from December 29 to January 3 (same source).

### How do I get there by train?

Change at JR Ogaki to the Yoro Railway and get off at Yoro Station, about 10 minutes on foot from the park ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_902.html); [Yoro Park](https://www.yoro-park.com/guide/access/)).
