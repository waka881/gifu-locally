Gujo Hachiman, the castle town of water channels and food samples, sits on the expressway between Takayama and Gifu or Nagoya. Express buses from Takayama stop at Gujo Hachiman IC, so it is easy to reach without changing. This guide uses the Nohi Bus timetables and the Gujo Hachiman Tourism Association.

## Takayama to Gujo Hachiman at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Bus | Reserved express buses from Takayama Bus Terminal (Gifu line and Nagoya line) |
| Stop | Gujo Hachiman IC, on the expressway outside town |
| Time | About 1 h 5 min on the Gifu line |
| Adult fare | ¥2,400 one way (Nagoya line ¥2,100 with web booking) |
| Into town | Shared taxi, ¥350 per person, reservation only |

*Sources: [Nohi Bus, Gifu–Takayama](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/); [Nohi Bus, Nagoya–Takayama](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/); [Gujo Hachiman Tourism Association](https://www.gujohachiman.com/kanko/), as of November 2026.*

## When are the buses from Takayama to Gujo Hachiman?

On the Gifu line, the bus to Gifu lets passengers off at Gujo Hachiman IC ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/)):

| Takayama Bus Terminal | Gujo Hachiman IC |
|---|---|
| 6:50 | 7:55 |
| 9:00 | 10:05 |
| 11:00 | 12:05 |
| 14:10 | 15:15 |
| 16:10 | 17:15 |
| 19:00 | 20:05 |

*Source: [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/), as of November 2026. ¥2,400 Takayama–Gujo Hachiman IC.*

Some Nagoya-line buses also stop at Gujo Hachiman IC, at 7:51, 9:51, 13:21, 16:21, 18:21 and 20:21; the fare is ¥2,400, or ¥2,100 when booked online through a Japanese-language site ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/), as of November 2026). All seats on both lines are reserved, and bookings open one month ahead.

## How do you get from Gujo Hachiman IC into town?

The IC stop is on the expressway, not in the old town. The Gujo Hachiman Tourism Association lists a shared taxi into the center for ¥350 per person, by reservation only, so book it before you set off ([Gujo Hachiman Tourism Association](https://www.gujohachiman.com/kanko/)). For the castle, the water channels and the food-sample workshops, see our [Gujo Hachiman day trip guide](gujo-hachiman-day-trip.html) and [Gujo Hachiman food samples](gujo-hachiman-food-samples.html).

## How do you get back to Takayama, or on to Gifu and Nagoya?

Back to Takayama, the Gifu-line buses pick up at Gujo Hachiman IC at 8:25, 11:15, 13:15, 16:05, 17:50 and 19:55, arriving in Takayama at 9:43, 12:33, 14:31, 17:21, 19:08 and 21:13 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/)). Nagoya-line buses towards Takayama pick up at 9:06, 11:06, 14:06, 18:06, 20:06 and 22:06 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/)).

Leaving Takayama for good? Continue on the same Gifu-line bus to Meitetsu Gifu (¥2,900 from Takayama), or take a Nagoya-line bus to Nagoya. See [Gifu to Takayama](gifu-to-takayama.html) and [Takayama from Nagoya](takayama-from-nagoya.html) for those routes.

## Should you stay a night in Gujo Hachiman?

A night lets you see the town early and in the evening, without planning around the last bus and the shared taxi. Gujo Hachiman is also known for its summer dance festival; see the [Gujo Hachiman day trip guide](gujo-hachiman-day-trip.html) for the town's sights and seasons.

## FAQ

### How long is the bus from Takayama to Gujo Hachiman?

About 1 hour 5 minutes on the Gifu-line bus to Gujo Hachiman IC, for ¥2,400 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/gifu_en/), as of November 2026).

### Do I need to reserve the bus?

Yes. All seats on the Gifu and Nagoya lines are reserved, and bookings open one month ahead (sources above).

### How do I get from Gujo Hachiman IC to the town center?

By the shared taxi, ¥350 per person, which runs by reservation only ([Gujo Hachiman Tourism Association](https://www.gujohachiman.com/kanko/)).

### Can I visit Gujo Hachiman on the way from Takayama to Nagoya or Gifu?

Yes. Get off at Gujo Hachiman IC, then take a later Gifu-line or Nagoya-line bus onward; book both legs (sources above).
