Gifu Castle sits on top of Mt. Kinka, above Gifu City, and is usually reached by ropeway. Until late 2027, both the castle and the ropeway have closures you should know about before you go. This guide uses the Gifu Convention & Visitors Bureau and the Gifu Prefecture tourism site.

## Gifu Castle and the Kinka Ropeway at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Castle keep | Closed from May 19, 2026 to around late October 2027; reopening planned for November 2027 |
| Castle archives museum | Closed from April 1, 2026 to around late October 2027 |
| Summit area in front of the keep | Closed from November 25, 2025 to around the end of September 2027 |
| Kinka Ropeway | Running, except December 1–28, 2026 and January 7–February 16, 2027 (planned inspection breaks) |

*Sources: [Gifu Convention & Visitors Bureau, Gifu Castle](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php); [Gifu Prefecture tourism, Kinka Ropeway](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/923/), as of November 2026. Closure dates are the operators' plans and may change.*

## Is Gifu Castle open right now?

No. The Gifu Convention & Visitors Bureau says the keep closed for renovation on May 19, 2026 and will stay closed until around late October 2027, with a renewal opening planned for November 2027. The castle's archives museum closed on April 1, 2026 for the same period, and the summit area in front of the keep has been closed since November 25, 2025, with reopening planned for around the end of September 2027 ([Gifu CVB](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php), as of November 2026).

Hikers are affected too: the Mizunote and Hanataka trails stop short of the summit, so you can't walk up to the castle or the summit toilets ([Gifu CVB](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php)).

## Can you still ride the Kinka Ropeway?

Yes, on most days. It runs every 15 minutes (every 10 minutes when busy), takes about 4 minutes from Gifu Park to the summit, and costs ¥1,300 return for adults. Standard hours are 9:00–18:00 all year. It runs until 22:00 from July 10 to September 30, and until 20:30 on Fridays, weekends and holidays from October 1 to November 30 and from March 19 to 28 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/923/), as of November 2026).

It is closed for inspection on **December 1–28, 2026** and **January 7–February 16, 2027** (planned), so a winter trip may find it not running ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/923/)). On the open days, the Gifu CVB says you can still take in the view from the Mt. Kinka observation deck on the roof of the summit restaurant while the castle is closed ([Gifu CVB](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php)).

## How do you get there from JR Gifu Station?

From stops 11, 12 or 13 at JR Gifu Station, take a Gifu Bus on the N line toward Nagarabashi or the City Loop counter-clockwise. Buses run about every 5 minutes, take 15 minutes and cost ¥250. Get off at "Gifu Koen / Gifu-jo" (Gifu Park / Gifu Castle) and walk 3 minutes to the ropeway in Gifu Park. You can't drive to the summit ([Gifu CVB](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php), as of November 2026). JR Gifu is around 20 minutes from Nagoya by JR; see [Nagoya or Gifu City as a base](nagoya-or-gifu-city-base.html).

## What is the history of Gifu Castle?

The Gifu CVB says the first fort was built on the mountain in the early 1200s, and the castle later belonged to Saito Dosan. Oda Nobunaga took it in 1567, and he is said to have renamed the town and castle "Gifu". The castle fell in 1600 in a battle before Sekigahara. Today's keep is a reinforced-concrete reconstruction from 1956 ([Gifu CVB](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php)).

## FAQ

### When will Gifu Castle reopen?

A renewal opening is planned for November 2027. The keep is closed from May 19, 2026 to around late October 2027 ([Gifu CVB](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php), as of November 2026).

### Is the Kinka Ropeway running in December?

Not from December 1 to 28, 2026, and not from January 7 to February 16, 2027, because of planned inspections ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/923/), as of November 2026).

### How much is the Kinka Ropeway?

¥1,300 return or ¥800 one way for adults (12 and over), and ¥650 return or ¥400 one way for children aged 4 to 11 (same source).

### How do I get to Gifu Castle from Gifu Station?

Take a Gifu Bus from stop 11, 12 or 13 to "Gifu Koen / Gifu-jo", about 15 minutes for ¥250, then walk 3 minutes to the ropeway ([Gifu CVB](https://www.gifucvb.or.jp/sightseeing/gifu-castle.php)).
