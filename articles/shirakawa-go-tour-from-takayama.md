Shirakawa-go is only about 50 minutes from Takayama by bus, so many travellers go on their own. A guided day tour costs more but takes care of the seats, the timing and the commentary. This guide compares the two using Nohi Bus and the tour's Klook listing.

## How much does it cost to go to Shirakawa-go on your own?

Nohi Bus lists ¥2,800 one way between the Takayama Bus Terminal and Shirakawa-go, rising to ¥2,900 from December 1, 2026, so a round trip is ¥5,600 now (¥5,800 from December) ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Most departures need a reserved seat. The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association suggests about 2–3 hours in the village ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)). Our [Takayama to Shirakawa-go guide](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) has the full timetable and a sample day.

## Which should you choose?

- **Take the bus** if you are on a budget, travelling alone, happy to book seats and want to set your own pace.
- **Take the tour** if you want Takayama's main sights and Shirakawa-go covered in one day with a guide, or don't want to plan bus times.

## FAQ

### Is a Shirakawa-go tour from Takayama worth it?

It suits travellers who want a guide and Takayama's sights in the same day; the bus is far cheaper at ¥2,800 each way (¥2,900 from December 1, 2026) ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/); [Klook](https://www.klook.com/activity/91141-takayama-shirakawa-go-one-day-bus-tour-takayama/)).

### How long is the Shirakawa-go tour from Takayama?

About 9 hours 10–40 minutes, according to the Klook listing (as of November 2026).

### Can I visit Shirakawa-go from Takayama without a tour?

Yes. The Nohi Bus takes about 50 minutes; book reserved seats on buses marked "R" ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).

### How long should I spend in Shirakawa-go?

About 2–3 hours, according to the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association ([FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)).
