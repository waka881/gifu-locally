Shirakawa-go has no station. From Tokyo you take the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama or Kanazawa and change to an express bus, or go via Takayama. This guide compares the options using the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association, JR West and Nohi Bus.

## Tokyo to Shirakawa-go at a glance

| Route | Time | Adult fare | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama + bus | About 4 h in total (Shinkansen 2 h 8–15 min, bus about 1 h 20 min) | Shinkansen (check JR) + bus ¥2,400 | Fastest |
| Hokuriku Shinkansen to Kanazawa + bus | 2 h 28 min + about 1 h 20 min | Shinkansen (check JR) + bus ¥2,800 | Staying a night in Kanazawa |
| Shinjuku bus to Takayama + bus | About 5 h 30 min, then 50 min next day | ¥8,000 + ¥2,800 | Lowest fare; seeing Takayama too |

*Sources: [Shirakawa-go Tourist Association, access](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/); [JR West, Hokuriku Shinkansen](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/shinkansen/hokuriku-shinkansen/); [Nohi Bus, Shirakawa-go line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/); [Nohi Bus, Shinjuku line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/shinjuku_en/), as of November 2026.*

## What is the fastest way from Tokyo to Shirakawa-go?

Via Toyama. The Tourist Association lists the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama (2 hours 15 minutes) and the express bus to the Shirakawago Bus Terminal (1 hour 20 minutes), about 4 hours in total ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)); JR West gives Tokyo–Toyama as 2 hours 8 minutes ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/shinkansen/hokuriku-shinkansen/)).

The catch is that there are only a few buses from Toyama: Nohi Bus lists departures from Toyama Station at **9:00, 10:10 and 12:00**, and buses back from Shirakawa-go to Toyama at **9:10, 14:10 and 15:30** ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026). Some need a reservation. Nohi Bus plans a fare and timetable revision from December 1, 2026, so check the page for your date. See [Toyama to Takayama](toyama-to-takayama.html) for this bus in detail.

## Is it better to go via Kanazawa?

Often, yes. JR West lists Tokyo–Kanazawa at 2 hours 28 minutes ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/shinkansen/hokuriku-shinkansen/)), and the bus from Kanazawa Station takes about 1 hour 20 minutes for ¥2,800 ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Kanazawa is also worth a night of its own; see [Shirakawa-go from Kanazawa](shirakawa-go-from-kanazawa.html) and [Takayama or Kanazawa?](takayama-or-kanazawa.html).

## Can you get to Shirakawa-go from Tokyo by bus?

Not directly on the routes the official sources list, but you can take the Keio/Nohi highway bus from the Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal to Takayama, about 5 hours 30 minutes for ¥8,000 (daytime, reservation required), then the 50-minute bus to Shirakawa-go for ¥2,800 ([Nohi Bus, Shinjuku](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/shinjuku_en/); [Nohi Bus, Shirakawa-go](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). Plan a night in Takayama; see [Tokyo to Takayama](tokyo-to-takayama.html) and [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html).

## Can you do Shirakawa-go as a day trip from Tokyo?

It's possible via Toyama or Kanazawa but makes a very long day: about 4 hours each way for the 2–3 hours most people spend in the village ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)). A night in Kanazawa, Toyama or Takayama, or [in Shirakawa-go itself](shirakawa-go-overnight-stay.html), makes it easier. A loop works well: Tokyo → Kanazawa → Shirakawa-go → Takayama → Nagoya, or the reverse; see the [Nagoya–Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa route](nagoya-takayama-shirakawa-go-kanazawa.html).

## What should you know before you go?

- **Winter:** Nohi Bus warns of delays of two hours or more because of snow, expressway closures or parking queues ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). See [Shirakawa-go in winter](shirakawa-go-in-winter.html).
- **Luggage:** coin lockers at the Shirakawago Bus Terminal cost ¥500 or ¥1,000 per use ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)).
- **Coming from Osaka or Kyoto later?** See [Osaka or Kyoto to Shirakawa-go](osaka-kyoto-to-shirakawa-go.html).

## FAQ

### How long does it take from Tokyo to Shirakawa-go?

About 4 hours via the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Toyama and the express bus ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/)).

### Is there a direct bus from Tokyo to Shirakawa-go?

Not on the official routes. The nearest is the Shinjuku–Takayama highway bus, about 5 hours 30 minutes for ¥8,000, then a 50-minute bus from Takayama (sources above).

### Should I go via Toyama or Kanazawa?

Toyama is quicker by train, but the bus from Toyama runs only a few times a day (9:00, 10:10 and 12:00 as of November 2026); Kanazawa is 20 minutes further by Shinkansen and a good place to stay ([JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/shinkansen/hokuriku-shinkansen/); [Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)).

### Can I do a day trip to Shirakawa-go from Tokyo?

Yes, but it means about 8 hours of travel for 2–3 hours in the village; an overnight stop is easier.
