The Usuzumi cherry is a single, very old tree in the mountains north of Ogaki. This guide uses the Gifu Prefecture tourism site, Motosu City's own pages and the Japan National Tourism Organization.

## What is the Usuzumi cherry?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site describes the Neodani Usuzumi-zakura as a cherry tree more than 1,500 years old, 17.3 m tall with a trunk 9.4 m around. It is a National Natural Monument and one of Japan's Three Great Cherry Trees, with the Yamataka Jindai-zakura in Yamanashi and the Miharu Takizakura in Fukushima. Its buds are pale pink, the flowers are white in full bloom, and they turn a pale ink colour ("usuzumi") just before they fall, which is said to be how the tree got its name ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_921.html)). The tree stands in Usuzumi Park, Neo-Itadokoro, Motosu City ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1248/)).

## How do you get there by train?

Take the JR Tokaido Line from Nagoya to Ogaki, about 30 minutes, then the Tarumi Railway to its last stop, Tarumi Station ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1248/)). Motosu City says Ogaki to Tarumi takes about an hour and warns that trains are infrequent, so check the timetable before you set out. From Tarumi Station it is about 15 minutes on foot to Usuzumi Park ([Motosu City](https://www.city.motosu.lg.jp/0000000221.html), as of November 2026).

Ogaki is also where you change for the [Site of Reversible Destiny](yoro-park-site-of-reversible-destiny.html), and [Ogaki Castle](ogaki-castle.html) is 7 minutes on foot from JR Ogaki Station, so you can pair either with the morning train north.

## Can you drive, and is there parking?

Yes. Motosu City gives about 30 minutes from Motosu IC on the Tokai-Kanjo Expressway, heading north on Route 157, and the prefecture adds about 40 minutes from Ono-Godo IC. The city's temporary parking holds about 700 cars and is open 24 hours; the prefecture also lists space for 30 large buses. The city warns that nearby roads get very crowded in the season, and that the Neo area has one petrol station, closed on Sundays ([Motosu City](https://www.city.motosu.lg.jp/0000000221.html); [Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_921.html), as of November 2026).

## When does it bloom, and is there a light-up?

Neither official source gives a fixed bloom date; Motosu City updates a bloom-status page through the season, with a live camera and a phone line (+81-58-323-0880). In 2026 its status on April 8 was already "fallen" ([Motosu City](https://www.city.motosu.lg.jp/0000000038.html)). The 2026 light-up ran from March 13 to April 19, 18:40–21:00 ([Motosu City](https://www.city.motosu.lg.jp/0000000258.html)), and food and souvenir stalls open in the park while the tree is in flower ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_921.html)). Dates for 2027 had not been announced as of November 2026.

## FAQ

### How old is the Usuzumi cherry tree?

More than 1,500 years old, according to the Gifu Prefecture tourism site; it is 17.3 m tall and a National Natural Monument ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/spot/detail_921.html)).

### How do I get to the Usuzumi cherry from Nagoya?

Take the JR Tokaido Line to Ogaki (about 30 minutes), then the Tarumi Railway to Tarumi Station (about 1 hour), and walk about 15 minutes ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1248/); [Motosu City](https://www.city.motosu.lg.jp/0000000221.html)).

### When is the Usuzumi cherry in bloom?

There is no fixed date; check Motosu City's bloom-status page or live camera. In 2026 the blossoms had fallen by April 8, and the light-up ran March 13–April 19 ([Motosu City](https://www.city.motosu.lg.jp/0000000038.html)).

### Is there parking at Usuzumi Park?

Yes, city temporary parking for about 700 cars, open 24 hours; roads nearby get crowded in the season ([Motosu City](https://www.city.motosu.lg.jp/0000000221.html), as of November 2026).
