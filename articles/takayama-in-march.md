March is the month when Takayama moves from winter to spring: the snow tails off, the last winter events finish and spring hours begin on April 1. This guide uses the Japan Meteorological Agency's averages, Hida Takayama Tourism, Takayama Jinya and the Gifu Prefecture tourism site.

## How cold and snowy is Takayama in March?

Much milder than February, but still wintry. The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages give 3.4°C for March, with daily highs of 9.8°C and lows of −1.6°C, and 29 cm of snowfall over the month, against 90 cm in February and 3 cm in April ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)). See [Takayama weather by month](takayama-weather-by-month.html). Pack a warm jacket and shoes that can handle slush.

## What winter events are still on in March?

- **Winter illumination in town:** Hida Takayama Tourism lists the Nakabashi Bridge and Miyagawa River area as lit from December 1 to March 15, 16:30–21:30 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000181.html)).
- **Aodaru Illumination:** the free ice-column display near the Fukuji Onsen bus stop in Okuhida runs from late December to late March, 17:00–22:00, about 1 hour 10 minutes by bus from JR Takayama Station (same source). See [where to stay in Okuhida](where-to-stay-okuhida.html).
- **Gero Onsen fireworks:** the free Hanabi Monogatari ran on Saturday nights from January 10 to March 28 in 2026, at 20:30 for about 10 minutes; the 2027 dates had not been posted ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3279.html), as of November 2026). See our [Gero Onsen winter fireworks guide](gero-onsen-winter-fireworks.html).

The Hida Folk Village illumination ends on February 28; see [Takayama in February](takayama-in-february.html).

## What changes between March and April?

March still runs on winter hours: the morning markets open at 8:00 from December to March and at 7:00 from April ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)), and Takayama Jinya closes at 16:30 from November to March and at 17:00 from April to October ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)). The mountain routes reopen in mid-April, and the Spring Takayama Festival is on April 14–15; see [Takayama in spring](takayama-in-spring.html).

## FAQ

### Is there snow in Takayama in March?

Some. The Japan Meteorological Agency's averages give 29 cm of snowfall in March, against 90 cm in February ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=47617)).

### How cold is Takayama in March?

March averages 3.4°C, with daily highs of 9.8°C and lows of −1.6°C (same source).

### When does the Takayama winter illumination end?

Hida Takayama Tourism lists it until March 15, 16:30–21:30, around Nakabashi Bridge and the Miyagawa River ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/eventcalendar/4000181.html)).

### What time do the Takayama morning markets open in March?

At 8:00, until 12:00; from April they open at 7:00 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)).
