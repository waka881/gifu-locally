The Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route crosses the Northern Alps between Toyama and Nagano by cable car, bus, ropeway and trolleybus. It is not in Gifu, but you can start it from Takayama by train. This guide uses the route's official site, Toyama City's tourism site and Hida Takayama Tourism.

## Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route at a glance

| | Details |
|---|---|
| Season | April 15–November 30 in 2026; 2027 dates not yet announced (as of November 2026) |
| Length | 37.2 km, with a maximum elevation difference of 1,975 m |
| Takayama → Toyama | Limited Express Hida, about 90 minutes |
| Dentetsu-Toyama → Tateyama | Toyama Chiho Railway, about 1 hour (next to JR Toyama Station) |
| Tateyama → Murodo | Tateyama Cable Car and Highland Bus, about 50 minutes |
| Snow wall (Yuki-no-Otani) | Murodo area, 2,450 m; mid-April to mid-June |
| Tickets | Web Ticket (buy and reserve a boarding time together) or same-day ticket |

## How do you get to the Alpine Route from Takayama?

Take the JR Limited Express Hida from Takayama to Toyama, about 90 minutes ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html)). From Dentetsu-Toyama Station, next to JR Toyama Station, the Toyama Chiho Railway Tateyama Line takes about an hour to Tateyama Station, where the route begins; the Tateyama Cable Car and Highland Bus then take about 50 minutes up to Murodo ([Toyama City tourism](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/en/spot/alpineroute/), as of November 2026). The route ends at Ogizawa on the Nagano side.

For other ways between the two cities, including the bus via Shirakawa-go, see [Toyama to Takayama](toyama-to-takayama.html).

## How much does the Alpine Route cost?

The official fare page gives these adult and child fares, current as of February 27, 2026 ([Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route, fares](https://www.alpen-route.com/access_new/fare/person/index.php)):

| Section | Adult one way | Child one way | Adult round trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tateyama – Ogizawa (whole mountain section) | ¥10,940 | ¥5,480 | ¥19,680 |
| Tateyama – Kurobeko (Kurobe Lake) | ¥9,140 | ¥4,580 | ¥16,480 |
| Ogizawa – Murodo | ¥6,850 | ¥3,430 | ¥12,300 |

Children are 6 to 11; under-6s ride free. The train from Dentetsu-Toyama to Tateyama is extra. The official site sells Web Tickets that reserve your boarding time, and same-day tickets that you can buy even when advance tickets are sold out ([Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route](https://alpen-route.com/en/fare)). It also lists the JR Alpine-Takayama-Matsumoto Area Tourist Pass, which combines JR trains and the Alpine Route; for 2026 Klook sold it for use between April 15 and November 10, and the listing showed as unavailable in November 2026 ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/2973-jr-alpine-takayama-matsumoto-area-pass/)).

## When can you see the snow wall?

Toyama City's tourism site says the snow wall in the Murodo area, at 2,450 m, can be seen from mid-April to mid-June, with some sections open for walking between the walls ([Toyama City tourism](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/en/spot/alpineroute/)). The route's own page for the 2026 Snow Wall Walk advises winter clothes, because it may snow and highs stay below 10°C, and trekking shoes or snow boots ([Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route](https://www.alpen-route.com/en/sww2026)).

## What do you do with your luggage?

The route runs a cash-only baggage forwarding service: ¥4,000 per item between Dentetsu-Toyama Station and Shinano-Omachi Station, with same-day delivery. No reservation is needed, but drop bags 20–30 minutes before you leave; at Dentetsu-Toyama it takes bags from 5:00 to 10:00. In 2026 it ran from April 15 to November 8 ([Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route, baggage](https://alpen-route.com/en/baggage/)).

## FAQ

### How do I get from Takayama to the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route?

Take the Limited Express Hida to Toyama (about 90 minutes), then the Toyama Chiho Railway from Dentetsu-Toyama to Tateyama (about 1 hour) ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/traveltips/practicalguide/4000220.html); [Toyama City tourism](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/en/spot/alpineroute/)).

### When is the Alpine Route open?

In 2026 it ran from April 15 to November 30; the 2027 dates had not been announced as of November 2026 ([Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route](https://alpen-route.com/en/transport/opening.html)).

### How much is the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route?

¥10,940 one way for adults from Tateyama to Ogizawa, or ¥19,680 round trip, as of February 27, 2026 ([Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route, fares](https://www.alpen-route.com/access_new/fare/person/index.php)).

### When can you see the snow wall?

From mid-April to mid-June, in the Murodo area at 2,450 m ([Toyama City tourism](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/en/spot/alpineroute/)).
