Takayama and Shirakawa-go are the two places most visitors to northern Gifu want to see, and they are often mentioned together. They are quite different trips. This guide compares them side by side, using figures from official sources, so you can decide whether to pick one or do both.

## Takayama vs Shirakawa-go at a glance

| | Takayama | Shirakawa-go |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Mountain town with a preserved merchant district, two morning markets and an Edo-period government office | UNESCO World Heritage village of thatched gassho-zukuri farmhouses |
| From Nagoya | About 2 h 20 min by Limited Express Hida, or 2 h 40 min by highway bus | About 2 h 50 min from Nagoya Station by highway bus |
| One-way cost from Nagoya (adult) | Train: ¥2,730 reserved-seat charge plus a separate basic fare. Bus: ¥3,600, or ¥3,300 with web discount | Bus: ¥3,600–¥4,700 depending on the date |
| Time needed | A full day, or better with a night's stay | About 2–3 hours to walk around the village |
| Morning markets | Yes, two, daily until noon | No |
| Best for | Food, sake, old streets and an overnight stay | The classic farmhouse village view, especially in snow |

*Sources: JR Central, Nohi Bus, Gifu Bus, Shirakawa-go Tourist Association and Hida Takayama Tourism, as of October 2026. Details and links below.*

## What is the difference between Takayama and Shirakawa-go?

**Takayama** is a town. Its Sanmachi old merchant district has wooden townhouses with lattice fronts, sake breweries with cedar balls over their doors, small restaurants and souvenir shops, and it is a 12-minute walk from JR Takayama Station (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)). You can also tour Takayama Jinya, the former Edo-period government office (¥500 for individuals, [Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)), and the Hida Folk Village open-air museum of old farmhouses (¥700 for adults, [Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato-tpo.jp/english12.htm)). For a deeper look, see our [Takayama guide](takayama-from-nagoya.html).

**Shirakawa-go** is a village. Its draw is the gassho-zukuri farmhouses, many of which are still homes ([Visit Gifu](https://visitgifu.com/see-do/unesco-world-heritage-site-shirakawa-go/)). The main sights are the Shiroyama viewpoint over the whole settlement, Wada House, which Visit Gifu calls the oldest and largest remaining gassho-zukuri house in the village, and the Gassho-zukuri Minkaen open-air museum. See our [Shirakawa-go guide](shirakawa-go-from-nagoya.html) for opening hours and fees.

In short, Takayama has more to do and suits a longer stay. Shirakawa-go is a single, very striking sight that most people see in a few hours.

## How long do you need in each?

**Shirakawa-go:** The Shirakawa-go Tourist Association estimates that walking around the village takes about 2–3 hours ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/), as of October 2026). If you want the view from the Shiroyama viewpoint by shuttle, note that it runs only from 10:00 to 14:40, with a break from 12:00 to 13:00 (same source).

**Takayama:** A day trip from Nagoya can cover Sanmachi, Takayama Jinya, and either Hida Folk Village or a sake brewery. The Miyagawa and Jinya-mae morning markets both close at noon (as of October 2026, [Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)), so staying a night is the easy way to see them.

## Which is easier to reach from Nagoya?

Both take a little over two hours each way.

- **Takayama:** The Limited Express Hida from JR Nagoya Station takes approximately 2 hours 20 minutes ([JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/area/hida-takayama/), as of October 2026). The Nohi Bus highway bus takes 2 hours 40 minutes and costs ¥3,600 one way, or ¥3,300 with the web discount, and every seat must be reserved ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/), as of October 2026).
- **Shirakawa-go:** The direct highway bus leaves from the Meitetsu Bus Center, about a 5-minute walk from Nagoya Station, and the trip takes about 2 hours 50 minutes in total ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/), as of October 2026). Seats must be reserved, bookings open one month before the travel date, and the adult one-way fare is ¥3,600–¥4,700 depending on the date ([Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html), as of October 2026).

Takayama has the extra option of the train, which suits travelers using a rail pass.

## Can you do both Takayama and Shirakawa-go?

Yes. The express bus between Takayama and Shirakawago Bus Terminal takes about 50 minutes ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/), as of October 2026). There are two common ways to combine them.

### 1. A guided day tour from Nagoya

Tours that visit both in one day handle all the transport and reservations. The trade-off is time: on multi-stop tours, each stop can be short. Compare the time in each place, the meeting point and the return time before booking. One option is the [Takayama and Shirakawa-go day tour from Nagoya on Klook](https://www.klook.com/activity/15800-takayama-shirakawa-go-day-trip-nagoya/).

### 2. Two days with a night in Takayama

- **Day 1:** Nagoya to Takayama by train or bus. Sanmachi, Takayama Jinya and Hida Folk Village.
- **Day 2:** The morning markets, then the bus to Shirakawa-go for 2–3 hours, then onward travel.

This is the most relaxed option and gives you the morning markets. If you are travelling on to Kanazawa, Shirakawa-go sits on the way; see our [Japan Alps route guide](nagoya-takayama-shirakawa-go-kanazawa.html). See [where to stay in Takayama](where-to-stay-takayama.html) for areas to book.

## Which is better in winter?

Shirakawa-go in snow is the image many people have in mind. On four evenings in 2027 (January 11, 17, 24 and 31), the village holds its winter light-up, and the 2026 event was reservation-only ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/); [Gifu Prefecture tourism site](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_1287.html), as of October 2026). Read our [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html) before you plan. For cherry blossom and autumn leaf dates, see the [best time to visit Shirakawa-go](best-time-to-visit-shirakawa-go.html).

Highway buses can be delayed by road and weather conditions, and Gifu Bus does not refund fares or cover missed connections caused by delays ([Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html)). In winter, leave a buffer before any onward train or flight. Our [Gifu winter guide](gifu-winter-guide.html) has more.

## So which should you choose?

**Choose Takayama** if you have one day and want food, sake, old streets and museums, or if you can stay a night.

**Choose Shirakawa-go** if the thatched farmhouse village is the one thing you want to see, and you are happy with a few hours there.

**Do both** if you have two days, or book a combined tour if you only have one.

## FAQ

### Is Takayama or Shirakawa-go better for a day trip from Nagoya?

Both work as day trips. Takayama has more to fill a day, with the old town, Takayama Jinya and Hida Folk Village. Shirakawa-go takes about 2–3 hours to walk around, according to the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association, so a day trip there leaves plenty of time on the bus. A combined tour visits both in one day.

### How far is Shirakawa-go from Takayama?

The express bus from Takayama to Shirakawago Bus Terminal takes about 50 minutes ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/access/), as of October 2026).

### Can I see Takayama and Shirakawa-go in one day?

Yes, most easily on a guided day tour from Nagoya that includes both. Expect less time in each place than if you visited them separately.

### Do I need to book buses to Takayama and Shirakawa-go?

Yes for the highway buses from Nagoya. Nohi Bus requires a reservation on every Nagoya–Takayama bus, and the Nagoya–Shirakawa-go bus has reserved seats that open for booking one month before the travel date ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/highwaybus/nagoya_en/); [Gifu Bus](https://www.gifubus.co.jp/highway/shirakawago.html), as of October 2026).
