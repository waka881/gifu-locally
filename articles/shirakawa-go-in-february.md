February is the snowiest-looking month in Shirakawa-go. This guide uses the Japan Meteorological Agency's averages for its Shirakawa station, the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association, Shirakawa Village, Nohi Bus, the Gifu Prefecture tourism site and Klook.

## How much snow is there in Shirakawa-go in February?

The most of the year on the ground. The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages for Shirakawa give a deepest snow cover of 173 cm in February, against 148 cm in January and 125 cm in March, with 251 cm of new snow falling over the month. February averages −0.8°C, with highs of 3.8°C and lows of −5.0°C ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=1306&year=&month=&day=&view=)). The full year is in [Shirakawa-go weather by month](shirakawa-go-weather-by-month.html).

## Is the Shirakawa-go light-up in February?

Not in 2027. The light-up evenings are January 11, 17, 24 and 31, 2027 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)); see our [light-up guide](shirakawa-go-light-up.html). On other nights, day visitors are not admitted to the village after dark, to protect residents' lives and privacy ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/events/106/)). If you want lit-up farmhouses in February, the Hida Folk Village in Takayama is lit every evening until February 28, 2027, 17:30–20:30 ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/event/detail_3323.html)); see the [Hida Folk Village illumination](hida-folk-village-winter-illumination.html).

## How do you get to Shirakawa-go in February?

By bus or tour is simplest. The express bus from Takayama takes about 50 minutes, ¥2,900 one way for adults from December 1, 2026, but in winter Nohi Bus warns of delays of two hours or more from heavy snow, expressway closures or traffic waiting to park ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/)). If you drive, Shirakawa Village says studless tyres are required and recommends chains and a 4WD vehicle ([Shirakawa Village FAQ](https://www.vill.shirakawa.lg.jp/1466.htm)). Routes are compared in [Takayama to Shirakawa-go](takayama-to-shirakawa-go.html) and [Shirakawa-go from Nagoya](shirakawa-go-from-nagoya.html).

## What should you wear?

Warm, waterproof boots with grip, a warm coat, gloves and a hat: daily lows average −5.0°C in February and the snow is at its deepest (JMA, above). For more winter tips, see [Shirakawa-go in winter](shirakawa-go-in-winter.html) and [Takayama in February](takayama-in-february.html).

## FAQ

### Is February a good time to visit Shirakawa-go?

For deep snow, yes: February has the deepest snow cover of the year, 173 cm on average ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=1306&year=&month=&day=&view=)). There is no light-up in February 2027, though.

### Is there a light-up in Shirakawa-go in February?

Not in 2027; the light-up evenings are January 11, 17, 24 and 31 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/shirakawa_lightup_2027en/)).

### How cold is Shirakawa-go in February?

February averages −0.8°C, with daily highs of 3.8°C and lows of −5.0°C (JMA, above).

### Can I drive to Shirakawa-go in February?

Yes, but Shirakawa Village says studless tyres are required and recommends chains and 4WD ([Shirakawa Village FAQ](https://www.vill.shirakawa.lg.jp/1466.htm)).
