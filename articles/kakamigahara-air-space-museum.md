The Gifu-Kakamigahara Air and Space Museum, known as Sorahaku, is in Kakamigahara, just east of Gifu City. This guide uses the museum's own pages.

## How much is a ticket?

¥900 for adults, ¥600 for high school students and people aged 60 and over, and free for junior high school students and younger. Elementary school children and younger must come with a guardian. Groups of 20 or more paying visitors get ¥800 and ¥500 ([Sorahaku](https://www.sorahaku.net/guide/fee/), as of November 2026).

The combined ticket with the Aichi Museum of Flight is not on sale from June 2026 to the end of January 2027, while that museum is closed (same source).

## When is it open?

10:00–17:00 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays, with last entry 30 minutes before closing. It closes on the first Tuesday of each month (the next weekday if that Tuesday is a holiday) and from December 28 to January 2, but opens on the first Tuesday of August. The museum says it may also close on some third Tuesdays for maintenance, or in bad weather, so check its calendar ([Sorahaku](https://www.sorahaku.net/guide/open/), as of November 2026).

## What can you see?

The museum's site lists an aviation area, a space area, outdoor exhibits and simulator experiences, and shows the Mars probe InSight and a rocket fairing among its displays ([Sorahaku](https://www.sorahaku.net/)).

## How do you get there?

By train and community bus. From the Meitetsu Kakamigahara Line, get off at Shin-Naka, Kakamigahara-Shiyakusho-mae or Mikakino and change to a Kakamigahara "Fureai" bus; from Shin-Naka the Kawashima line takes about 12 minutes. From the JR Takayama Line, get off at Naka and take the Inaba-nishi line from the stop north of the station rotary. All routes stop at "Koku Uchu Hakubutsukan" (Air and Space Museum). The buses don't run December 29–January 2. A taxi takes about 15 minutes from Kakamigahara-Shiyakusho-mae or about 20 minutes from JR Naka ([Sorahaku](https://www.sorahaku.net/guide/access/), as of November 2026).

JR Naka is on the JR Takayama Line from Gifu, so it is easy to combine with a stay in Gifu City; see [Nagoya or Gifu City as a base](nagoya-or-gifu-city-base.html) and [things to do in Gifu](things-to-do-in-gifu.html).

## FAQ

### How much is the Gifu-Kakamigahara Air and Space Museum?

¥900 for adults, ¥600 for high school students and over-60s, and free for junior high students and younger ([Sorahaku](https://www.sorahaku.net/guide/fee/), as of November 2026).

### What are the opening hours?

10:00–17:00 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 at weekends and on holidays, with last entry 30 minutes before closing ([Sorahaku](https://www.sorahaku.net/guide/open/)).

### When is it closed?

On the first Tuesday of each month (the next weekday if it is a holiday) and December 28–January 2 (same source).

### What is the nearest station?

There is no station next to the museum. Take a Kakamigahara community bus from Meitetsu Shin-Naka, Kakamigahara-Shiyakusho-mae or Mikakino, or from JR Naka, to "Koku Uchu Hakubutsukan" ([Sorahaku](https://www.sorahaku.net/guide/access/)).
