Kanazawa and Takayama are linked by a reserved bus that runs through Shirakawa-go, so a day in Takayama from Kanazawa is possible. This guide gives the bus times from Nohi Bus's timetable, a sample day, and the guided tour option on Klook.

## What are the bus times from Kanazawa to Takayama?

Nohi Bus lists these through buses between Kanazawa Station and the Takayama Bus Terminal, all via Shirakawa-go ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), checked November 11, 2026):

| Leave Kanazawa | Arrive Takayama | Leave Takayama | Arrive Kanazawa |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8:10 | 10:25 | 7:50 | 10:05 |
| 11:10 | 13:25 | 9:10 (from Dec 1, 2026) | 11:10 |
| 13:20 | 15:35 | 11:20 | 13:35 |
| 15:00 (from Dec 1, 2026) | 17:00 | 12:50 | 15:05 |
| 16:00 | 18:15 | 16:30 | 18:45 |

The fare is ¥4,200 one way, rising to ¥4,400 from December 1, 2026. Buses marked "R" on the timetable need a reservation, which you can make on Japan Bus Online or by phone (same source). For a day trip, the 8:10 out and the 16:30 back are the only pair that gives you most of a day. Book both before you go.

## What can you see in Takayama in about 6 hours?

Arriving at 10:25, you can still catch the end of the morning markets, which close at 12:00 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)). Then:

- **Takayama Jinya**, ¥500 for adults, open until 16:30 from November to March and 17:00 from April to October ([Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/)).
- **The Sanmachi old town**, about 12 minutes on foot from JR Takayama Station ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000153.html)), with its [sake breweries](takayama-sake-breweries.html).
- **Hida beef for lunch**; see [Hida beef in Takayama](hida-beef-takayama.html).

## Can you see Shirakawa-go and Takayama in one day from Kanazawa?

By public bus it is tight, since the two places together need about 2–3 hours in Shirakawa-go ([Shirakawa-go Tourist Association FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)) plus the time in Takayama and about 4.5 hours on buses. The easier option is a guided tour. Klook lists the Shirakawago & Takayama Full Day Tour from Kanazawa, 8–10 hours with an English-speaking guide, covering Shirakawa-go and Takayama's old town, including the Takayama Festival Floats Exhibition Hall, from US$202.09 per person ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/activity/101875-shirakawago-takayama-join-one-day-bus-tour-kanazawa/), as of November 2026). The listing doesn't say whether lunch is included, so check before you book.

## FAQ

### Can you do a day trip from Kanazawa to Takayama?

Yes. Take the 8:10 bus from Kanazawa Station (arriving 10:25) and the 16:30 back (arriving 18:45), which gives about 6 hours in Takayama ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), as of November 2026).

### How much is the bus from Kanazawa to Takayama?

¥4,200 one way, rising to ¥4,400 from December 1, 2026 (same source).

### How long is the bus from Kanazawa to Takayama?

About 2 hours 15 minutes, via Shirakawa-go (same source).

### Is there a tour from Kanazawa to Takayama and Shirakawa-go?

Yes. Klook lists the Shirakawago & Takayama Full Day Tour from Kanazawa, 8–10 hours with an English-speaking guide ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/activity/101875-shirakawago-takayama-join-one-day-bus-tour-kanazawa/), as of November 2026).
