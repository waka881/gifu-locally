## How much snow does Shirakawa-go get?

A lot. The averages give 972 cm of snowfall a year: 201 cm in December, 340 cm in January, 251 cm in February and 149 cm in March. The snow cover is deepest in February, at 173 cm on average, and is still 32 cm deep in April ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=1306&year=&month=&day=&view=)). That is about three times the 305 cm a year at Takayama ([Takayama weather by month](takayama-weather-by-month.html)). For what this means on the ground, see [Shirakawa-go in winter](shirakawa-go-in-winter.html); the [2027 winter light-up](shirakawa-go-light-up.html) is on four days in January.

## How cold is Shirakawa-go in winter?

January averages −1.0°C and February −0.8°C, with daily lows of −4.4°C and −5.0°C and highs of only about 3–4°C. December averages 1.7°C, with lows of −1.7°C. Winter days are also dark: the averages give 41.2 hours of sunshine in January and 47.0 in December (same source).

## When is the best weather to visit Shirakawa-go?

By the averages, May is the sunniest month (196.9 hours) and one of the driest (128.8 mm), with highs of 21.8°C, and the snow has gone by then. July is wet, with 277.7 mm, and December and January are the wettest months of the year, at 282.8 mm and 281.8 mm, mostly falling as snow (same source). For the seasons in more detail, see the [best time to visit Shirakawa-go](best-time-to-visit-shirakawa-go.html).

## What should you pack?

- **December to March:** a warm coat, gloves, a hat and waterproof boots with grip; daily lows are below freezing in all four months.
- **April and November:** layers and a warm jacket, with lows around 3°C, and shoes that can handle slush in April.
- **June to October:** an umbrella or rain jacket; highs reach 25–30°C from June to September.

## FAQ

### Does Shirakawa-go have snow?

Yes. The Japan Meteorological Agency's averages give about 972 cm of snowfall a year at Shirakawa, mostly from December to March ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=52&block_no=1306&year=&month=&day=&view=)).

### When is the snow deepest in Shirakawa-go?

February, when the deepest snow cover averages 173 cm; January averages 148 cm and March 125 cm (same source).

### What is the coldest month in Shirakawa-go?

January, averaging −1.0°C; February has the lowest daily lows, at −5.0°C (same source).

### Is there still snow in Shirakawa-go in April?

Some. April averages 19 cm of new snow, with snow cover up to 32 cm deep; by May it is gone (same source).
