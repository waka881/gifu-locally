Spring in Takayama brings the year's first big festival, cherry blossoms and the reopening of the mountain roads into the Northern Alps. This guide pulls together the official dates from Hida Takayama Tourism, the Gifu Prefecture tourism site, Nohi Bus and the Alpine Route.

## When is the Spring Takayama Festival?

On April 14 and 15 every year. Hida Takayama Tourism lists 12 floats, displayed from 9:30 around Takayama Jinya, karakuri puppet shows at 10:00 and 14:00 on both days, and the lantern-lit night festival from 18:00 on April 14, starting near Nakabashi Bridge. In rain, the procession, float display, puppet shows and night festival are cancelled, and the festival is not postponed ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/4000208.html)). The full timetable is in our [Takayama Festival guide](takayama-festival.html); on other days you can see real floats at the [Floats Exhibition Hall](takayama-festival-floats-exhibition-hall.html).

## When do cherry blossoms bloom in Takayama?

The Gifu Prefecture tourism site lists Shiroyama Park, with about 1,000 Somei-yoshino on the site of Takayama Castle, and Nakabashi Bridge, which has a spring light-up. It does not give a fixed date for town, but says the mountains bloom later: Okuhida about a month after the lowlands, from around late April, and Asahi, known for weeping cherries, in the second half of April ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_60.html), as of November 2026). For other spots, from Gifu City to the Shokawa-zakura, see [Gifu cherry blossom spots](gifu-cherry-blossom-spots.html).

## What opens again in spring?

- **Kamikochi:** the Nohi Bus Kamikochi line ran from April 17 to November 15 in 2026; the trip from Takayama takes about 90 minutes via Hirayu Onsen ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/kamikochi-line-en/)). See [Kamikochi from Takayama](kamikochi-from-takayama.html).
- **Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route:** open from April 15 in 2026, with the snow wall at Murodo from mid-April to mid-June ([Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route](https://alpen-route.com/en/transport/opening.html); [Toyama City tourism](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/en/spot/alpineroute/)). See [Alpine Route from Takayama](tateyama-kurobe-alpine-route-from-takayama.html).
- **Morning markets:** they run all year, but open an hour earlier, at 7:00, from April ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/touristattractions/takayamacity/historyandculture/4000162.html)). See [Takayama morning market](takayama-morning-market.html).

The [Shinhotaka Ropeway](shinhotaka-ropeway.html) runs all year, so it is also an option in spring.

## FAQ

### When is the Takayama Spring Festival?

April 14–15 every year, with the night festival on the evening of April 14 from 18:00 ([Hida Takayama Tourism](https://www.hida.jp/english/festivalsandevents/4000208.html)).

### Is April a good time to visit Takayama?

It is the month of the Spring Festival and the reopening of the Kamikochi bus and the Alpine Route (mid-April in 2026), and the morning markets open at 7:00 from April (sources above, as of November 2026).

### Where can I see cherry blossoms in Takayama?

Shiroyama Park, with about 1,000 Somei-yoshino, and Nakabashi Bridge, which is lit up in spring ([Gifu Prefecture tourism](https://www.kankou-gifu.jp/article/detail_60.html)).

### Is Kamikochi open in April?

The Kamikochi bus line from Takayama opened on April 17 in 2026; the 2027 date had not been announced as of November 2026 ([Nohi Bus](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/kamikochi-line-en/)).
